# Runegrip Clan

> First named in [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]], through [[Tiamor Runegrip]]. Distinct from the [[Stoneforge Clan]] and the [[Greybraids]] — the third [[Stonewater]] dwarf clan to appear on-page.

A dwarf clan of [[Stonewater]], led by a **matriarch** — **[[Tiamor Runegrip]]'s eldest sister** (unnamed on-page). Tiamor, who owns the [[Raise Your Spirits]] spa on the city's top tier, describes herself as the family's social, apolitical member.

## What's known

- The matriarch is **"very stressed about the recent goings-on"** — the pickaxe murders, the disappearances, and the **civil unrest** they've caused.
- **A few Runegrip clan members are among the missing-then-dead** — the victims span several clans, not one.
- City governance detail that came with it: **each clan leader deals with their own guard**, and **each section of the city is assigned a guard on a rotating schedule**.

Whether the clan has any stake in the [[Ironheart Mines]], the [[Greybraids]]' affairs, or the [[Acolytes of Oghma|cult]] is unrecorded. Tiamor has offered to feed the party whatever her contacts turn up.

# Victor Stonehammer

> **Not to be confused with [[Victor Barun]].** Victor Barun is the human retired fishing mogul of [[Fort Elm]]; Victor Stonehammer is a **dwarf** of [[Stonewater]] and an entirely separate character. The party explicitly raised and dismissed the name collision when the murdered miner's spirit named "Victor" as the killer ([[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]]). Archivist exports mislabeled this entity "Victor Barun" — that is an error.

A **dwarf** of [[Stonewater]], deceased — and returned as a **revenant**. In life he was a **prime suspect** in the string of murders of [[Ironheart Mines]] cave-in survivors. A sector of the **[[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge Guard]]** cornered him in an alley and killed him; his body **rapidly decomposed** after he went down, and his corpse went missing. This is almost certainly the **"accused man whose corpse and weapon vanished"** the party first heard about at the [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] audience in [[The Search for Dragon Eggs - Session 29 - 2026-05-10|Session 29]].

## Revenant

He returned as a **revenant** — a vengeful undead that does not rest until its vengeance is satisfied (identified by [[Seren Moss|Seren]], a cleric of [[Kelemvor]], in Session 31). Per DM canon, a revenant is a **possessing spirit**: it does not need the specific body it died in, but can inhabit **any corpse** and, over time, **morph it to resemble its original self**. The creature the party fought looks like Victor Stonehammer — but, because the Stoneforge Guard claim to have already killed the original, it is understood to be **not the original body**, just a cadaver wearing his face.

### Appearance (revenant form)

- Heavily cloaked, dragging a **stone pickaxe**.
- A rotting dwarf: patchy, chunked-out beard; a large **rock embedded in its skull** (a cave-in injury); purple bludgeoning bruises across the skin; no solid structure beneath the cloak, just rotting flesh.
- Raspy, hollow voice. Marked [[Bell]] as its "prey": *"You will not stop me from my prey."*

### Capabilities observed

- Immune to the **frightened** and **stunned** conditions.
- **Regenerates** — flesh visibly reconstitutes; extremely hard to put down.
- Effectively unharmed by radiant damage.
- Moves through the [[Stonewater Sewers]] unseen via multiple access points.
- Requires **Banishment** (a 4th-level spell) — or a satisfied vengeance — to end permanently.

## Vengeance (working theory)

The victims are **survivors of the [[Ironheart Mines]] cave-in**. The party's unconfirmed theory: Victor Stonehammer was a miner **left to die in the collapse**, now killing those who escaped without him. The murdered survivors had **seen something in the cave** — *"people in the cave"* — that someone wanted silenced, an open thread that may connect to whatever caused the cave-in.

## Status

Session 31 cliffhanger: the party contained the revenant in a [[Chained Stone Coffin]]; on opening it, the host body had **dissolved into a moving liquid** — destroyed in form but **not ended**.

**As of [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]: disembodied, at large.** The spirit abandoned its ruined host; per the warning [[Seren Moss|Seren]] gave the city guard, it can **re-form in another corpse within roughly half a day** in the city. Banishment remains impossible until the next in-fiction day — Seren's volunteered ritual is still pending. Countermeasures in motion: the [[Guard Captain (Stonewater)|guard captain]] is warning the **graveyard keepers**, [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] owes the party **lists of the cave-in's dead and survivors** (its remaining targets), and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] carries a [[Vial of Decomposed Black Goo|sample of the coffin remains]]. The captain referred the party to **[[Havid]]** for the details of how Stonehammer originally died; Baldrin, asked directly, said the name **didn't immediately stand out to him**.

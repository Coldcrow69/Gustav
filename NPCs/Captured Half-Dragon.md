A half-dragon captured by the party from the warband that attacked [[Bluefield]]. **One of the dragonkin warband** — a brood that is part of **[[The God Of The Iron Peaks|the god of the Iron Peaks]]**'s larger lineage. The warband is **partly tied to the dragon by blood and partly by red-dragonhood alone**; the captive may not be a literal blood-child of the god of the Iron Peaks.

The **eggs that were stolen** — and that the assault is meant to recover — are **[[The God Of The Iron Peaks|the god of the Iron Peaks]]'s own eggs**, **not** this captive's children. When the warband speaks of "the children" being missing, they mean the unhatched siblings/kin among their brood — not personal offspring.

## What the captive revealed

- The assault on [[Bluefield]] was motivated by the theft of the **multi-headed red dragon's eggs**.
- The dragons will not cease their attacks until the stolen eggs are recovered.

The captive's testimony was reported by [[Seren Moss|Seren]] to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] during the party's audience in [[Stonewater]]. Per the 3/1/26 recap, **the party never asked his name**.

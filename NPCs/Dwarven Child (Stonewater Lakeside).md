# Dwarven Child (Stonewater Lakeside)

> Unnamed on-page.

A **dwarven child** seen at a window of a **run-down, boarded building** overlooking the [[Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)|junk-strewn corner]] above the lake in [[Stonewater]] — roughly a hundred feet from [[Seren Moss|Seren]] and [[Mike]] as they pulled the hidden **[[Dragon Eggs|dragon egg]]** from its wooden box in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]].

The child **watched them take the egg**. [[Mike]] waved; the party chose not to make contact and left. Whether the child understood what was taken — or will tell anyone — is unknown.

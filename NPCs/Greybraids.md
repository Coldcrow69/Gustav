# Greybraids

A **dwarf clan** of [[Stonewater]]. Identified in Session 30 (2026-05-17) as the wearers of the **feather-and-dagger emblem** seen on the **suspicious cart** the party tailed into the city on Session 29.

> Aliases recorded across chronicler / archivist outputs (all refer to this clan): **Graybreed**, **Greybeards**, **Graybreeds**, **Greybraid clan**, **Graybreed clan**, **Gray Braids**. Canonical spelling: **Greybraids**.

## Emblem

- **Feather and dagger**, displayed together as a single insignia.
- Drawn into [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s notebook from memory of the cart's occupants and shown to **[[Nastrin]]** for identification.

## Suspected involvement

Two [[Egg Hunt]] threads were associated with the clan — though [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]] narrowed this to the first:

1. **Dragon egg theft.** The cart bearing the Greybraids' insignia was the **separate, second cart** that traveled loosely alongside [[Basil]]'s caravan from [[Autumnbreak]] on the road into [[Stonewater]]. The party split it off and tried to tail it through the city; it disappeared in the second tier. **This remains the clan's chief suspected involvement — but [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]] weakened it considerably:** [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s egg-tracking sweep found **no egg signals at the clan's manor or forge, nor at the city's single mine entrance**. The eggs the sweep did find were elsewhere (a [[Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)|lakeside junk pile]] and a [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside mansion]]). The cart link is unexplained but no longer points at eggs in Greybraids hands — and the party has floated the darker possibility that an egg was **planted to incite conflict between rival factions**.
2. **[[Ironheart Mines]] murders — now decoupled (Session 31).** The party's Session 30 hypothesis was that the Greybraids were behind the mine-survivor murders (a coverup; [[Thorn]] floated the clan paying [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] off the books for **coal or other raw stock for their forges**, the cave-in disturbing whatever they were quietly extracting). **Session 31 superseded this:** the killings are the work of the **revenant** of [[Victor Stonehammer]], avenging himself on cave-in survivors. The Greybraids are **no longer** the suspects for the murders — though the open question of *what the miners saw in the cave* could still touch the clan.

## City-guard context (Session 32)

The [[Captain Grazug|Stonewater guard captain]], asked about the clan: the Greybraids **depend on the mines**, the clan and the [[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge]] side **have a history**, and **nothing severe has happened between them very recently**.

## Operational posture

- *(Session 30 plan — partly superseded.)* The party intended to approach the clan **subtly** — a pair posing as discreet business partners — rather than confronting them directly, so the clan would not realize it was being investigated. The subtle-approach idea has not been revisited since the sweep.
- [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s **locator spell** was the other half of that plan — **executed in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]**, and it cleared the clan's known holdings (see above). The egg signals point elsewhere ([[Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)|lakeside]], [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside]]).

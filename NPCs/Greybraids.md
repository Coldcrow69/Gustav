# Greybraids

A **dwarf clan** of [[Stonewater]]. Identified in Session 30 (2026-05-18) as the wearers of the **feather-and-dagger emblem** seen on the **suspicious cart** the party tailed into the city on Session 29.

> Aliases recorded across chronicler / archivist outputs (all refer to this clan): **Graybreed**, **Greybeards**, **Graybreeds**, **Greybraid clan**, **Graybreed clan**. Canonical spelling: **Greybraids**.

## Emblem

- **Feather and dagger**, displayed together as a single insignia.
- Drawn into [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s notebook from memory of the cart's occupants and shown to **[[Nastrin]]** for identification.

## Suspected involvement

Two ongoing [[Egg Hunt]] threads point at the clan:

1. **Dragon egg theft.** The cart bearing the Greybraids' insignia was the **separate, second cart** that traveled loosely alongside [[Basil]]'s caravan from [[Autumnbreak]] on the road into [[Stonewater]]. The party split it off and tried to tail it through the city; it disappeared in the second tier.
2. **[[Ironheart Mines]] murders.** The party's hypothesis (Session 30): the **dead miners are cave-in survivors** who saw something underground they were not supposed to see; the murders are a coverup. [[Thorn]] floated the idea that the Greybraids might be paying [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] off the books for **coal or other raw stock for their forges**, and that the cave-in disturbed whatever they were quietly extracting. **Unverified.**

## Operational posture

- The party intends to approach the clan **subtly** — a pair posing as discreet business partners — rather than confronting them directly, so the clan does not realize they are being investigated.
- [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s planned **locator spell** for the missing dragon eggs is being paired with the Greybraids lead.

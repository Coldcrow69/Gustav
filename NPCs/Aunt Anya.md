Full name: **Anya Moss**.

One of **[[Gwyn Moss]]'s three sisters** (see [[The Aunties]]). [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s adoptive aunt at the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]].

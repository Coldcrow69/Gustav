**[[Gwyn Moss]]'s three sisters** — collectively "the Aunties" by [[Seren Moss|Seren]]. **All share the surname Moss** ([[Ilsa|Ilsa Moss]], [[Aunt Keva|Keva Moss]], [[Aunt Anya|Anya Moss]]). They help keep the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]] running. Each fills a distinct role in Seren's upbringing.

> Source: *Seren (Crypt Keepers)* long-form short story.

## The three Aunts

### [[Ilsa|Aunt Ilsa]] — the cook / cleric

Soft brown eyes; warm. Handles the cooking at the shrine; Seren typically helped her in the mornings. Doubles as a **Sister of [[Kelemvor]]** (cleric / temple worker). At Seren's departure she pressed a wrapped bundle of treats into her hands "for you and your friends."

### [[Aunt Keva]] — the fighter / sparring trainer

Sharp, hands-on-hips, sternness over real concern. Trained Seren in martial combat; she still notes Seren **doesn't guard her left side properly** and pressed Seren to keep up daily practice. Sparring is part of the shrine's defensive culture (acolytes hone battle skills against graverobbers).

### [[Aunt Anya]] — the spiritual mentor

Carries the doctrinal anchor. At Seren's departure: *"Remember that your strength comes from [[Kelemvor]]. Trust in him, uphold his tenants, and you will not fail."* Promised the temple would **pray for the party** during their travels and asked Seren to keep in touch.

## Standing in Seren's life

All three Aunts were named among the people Seren loves and was reluctant to leave when [[Victor Barun]] offered her the [[Fort Elm]] missing-farmers commission and the broader journey to [[Stonewater]].

The **elder of [[Riknozz]]'s peaceful Drow sect**. Also called the **Wise Woman** and the **Clan Mystic** in session notes. Female. Encountered in the [[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)|ruined Temple of Tyr]] beneath [[Fort Elm]] during the [[Fish People]] arc.

## Knowledge

- Identified the cavern's master as an **aboleth** that had taken up residence in the [[The Ancient Ruined City|ancient ruined city]] below — a deduction the party then confirmed.
- Held her people's oral history of the city: **"an ancient city where people lived in safety and harmony, until their society collapsed under the influence of a powerful evil."**
- Explained [[Thorn]]'s heritage: Thorn comes from a group of elves known as **Shapeshifters**, banished from both Drow and elf societies, considered **cursed by the gods**. The trait can **skip generations**; Thorn's parents likely abandoned her at birth. The elder had only ever heard stories from her father — never met another Shapeshifter.
- Explained her sect's break from violent Drow factions, choosing a peaceful path.

## Lyrion's blood debt

Heard the brothers' account of their mother's torture and murder by Drow, and weighed it against [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s killing of a Drow prisoner. Offered terms: if Lyrion helped kill the **[[The Undead Aboleth|aboleth]]**, she and her people would consider his blood debt **paid**. Lyrion accepted; the aboleth was slain.

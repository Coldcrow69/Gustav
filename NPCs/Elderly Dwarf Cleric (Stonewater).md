# Elderly Dwarf Cleric (Stonewater)

> Unnamed on-page. (Archivist variants: "Elderly Cleric," "Elderly Dwarf Woman In Clerical Robes.")

An **elderly dwarf woman**, a cleric and apparent attendant of the **[[Temple of Selûne (Stonewater)|Temple of Selûne]]** in [[Stonewater]] — the green-domed temple that also serves as the city library. Very old and wrinkled, with a **full beard graying along the edges**, in clerical robes.

## Session 31

[[Mike]] ran to the temple seeking a cleric who could **Banish** the [[Victor Stonehammer]] revenant. She didn't recognize his garbled request at first ("brandish" / "vanish"), but on understanding it was an undead matter, confirmed she **could not cast Banishment that day**. She consulted a **heavy tome of the city's registered clerics** for anyone nearby who could, found no immediate option, and chose to come see the creature herself. [[Mike]] carried her back to the [[Stonewater Sewers]] (in giant-form). On arrival she reaffirmed she lacked Banishment for the day, and asked whether anyone present could otherwise dispel the creature.

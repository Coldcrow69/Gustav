# Thalrin

**Father of [[Rhaenyra]]**, and an organizer / leading figure in **[[Roseview]]** (her fishing village in the north of [[The Wilds]]).

## Festival host

Hosted and **organized the festival** celebrating the slaying of [[The Hydra|the hydra]] that had been plaguing Roseview, after the party arrived on 10/15/25. Festival included feasting, town exploration, and a **wizard duel as entertainment** ([[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] participated and ended up singed; healed afterward by [[Seren Moss|Seren]]).

# Thalrin

**Father of [[Rhaenyra]]**, and an organizer / leading figure in **[[Roseview]]** (her fishing village in the north of [[The Wilds]]).

## Festival host

Hosted and **organized the festival** celebrating the slaying of [[The Hydra|the hydra]] that had been plaguing Roseview, after the party arrived on 10/15/25. Festival included feasting, town exploration, and a **wizard duel as entertainment** ([[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] participated and ended up singed; healed afterward by [[Seren Moss|Seren]]).

> Source: WhisperX transcript for 2025-11-05.

## The blessing

Revealed by [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] to his brother in [[Through the Bloodied Rubble - Session 37 - 2026-10-25|Session 37]] (in Undercommon, on the walk into the [[Ironheart Mines]]): back in Roseview, Thalrin **asked Eldrin to protect his daughter**. Eldrin said he would protect her with his life; Thalrin asked whether he was serious about that; Eldrin said yes — and *"it led to a longer conversation"* that gave Eldrin *"what felt like his blessing."* Rhaenyra had already **spoken to her father about Eldrin**. Eldrin, with no one else to ask (his mother dead, his father estranged and unaccounted for), went looking for their mother's opinion from Lyrion instead — and got it. Rhaenyra does not know whether **her mother** is alive (Session 36).

# Darion Thalor

**Father of [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Eldrin Thalor]].** Widower of **[[Endra Thalor]]**. A **fair-skinned high elf** (his complexion passed to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]]; [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] took after their part-drow mother).

## Past — cruelty toward Eldrin

**Used to beat [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] in the family cellar.** This is the abuse implicit in the brothers' *"dad's basement"* exchange at the [[Riddle Door]] (11/6/24 transcript) — Lyrion's *"Worse than dad's basement"* invokes the cellar as a benchmark for dread; Eldrin's agreement (*"Incredibly, I think worse than the basement, Eldren."*) confirms both brothers carry the memory.

Whether [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] also experienced direct abuse or witnessed Eldrin's is **not yet recorded**.

## Present — a dark path of vengeance

**Following the death of his wife [[Endra Thalor|Endra]] at the hands of the Drow**, Darion **began going down a dark path of vengeance**. The specifics of that path — methods, allies, current activities — are **not yet recorded in vault**. He may yet re-enter the story; treat as an active open hook for the [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]/[[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] family arc.

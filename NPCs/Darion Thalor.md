# Darion Thalor

**Father of [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Eldrin Thalor]].** Widower of **[[Endra Thalor]]**. A **fair-skinned high elf** (his complexion passed to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]]; [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] took after their part-drow mother).

## Past — cruelty toward Eldrin

**Used to beat [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] in the family cellar.** This is the abuse implicit in the brothers' *"dad's basement"* exchange at the [[Riddle Door]] (11/6/24 transcript) — Lyrion's *"Worse than dad's basement"* invokes the cellar as a benchmark for dread; Eldrin's agreement (*"Incredibly, I think worse than the basement, Eldren."*) confirms both brothers carry the memory.

Whether [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] also experienced direct abuse or witnessed Eldrin's is **not yet recorded**.

## Present — a dark path of vengeance

**Following the death of his wife [[Endra Thalor|Endra]] at the hands of the Drow**, Darion **began going down a dark path of vengeance**. The specifics of that path — methods, allies, current activities — are **not yet recorded in vault**. He may yet re-enter the story; treat as an active open hook for the [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]/[[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] family arc.

**Not seen in the afterlife.** When Lyrion died and glimpsed the far side ([[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]), he saw Endra — and, as he told Eldrin in [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]], **not their father**: *"thankfully, or unfortunately, not — I'm not sure which."* Eldrin's reading: *"Either he's not dead, or maybe the afterlife is a place worth going to."* Lyrion's private certainty, played at the table: his father wouldn't be there *even if he were dead*. His status remains unknown.

## More of the childhood (Sessions 36–37)

- **Winter river plunges** were mandatory — *"the only way I was going to be a real man"* (Eldrin). The goalposts moved every time. Once Eldrin stayed in **thirty-six minutes**, turned so blue his toes stopped working, and Endra was furious at Darion. Lyrion never went in the river "because of that."
- **The first-deer rite** — a hunter's offering to the gods: eat the eye, a bite of the heart, a bite of the liver, raw. Eldrin insists this was the whole village's custom, "not just a my-father-crazy thing."
- He **took the bow back** from Lyrion the day of the doe and handed it to Eldrin (Session 30) — the origin of everything above.

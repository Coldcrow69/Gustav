# Dura

> First appears (unnamed, an axe-bearing silhouette in a tavern hallway) in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]; named by [[Captain Grazug]] the next morning. **Member of the [[Stoneforge Clan]]** (maintainer-confirmed) — the clan has a large presence in [[Stonewater]]'s guard and military, which is why a city-guard veteran wears Stoneforge colors.

A **dwarven guardswoman** with long black hair, assigned by [[Captain Grazug]] as the party's **official minder and protector** after the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] raid — one of his "more trusted employees." Her armor is guard-like but of a different make than the captain's, in **[[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge]] colors**; her orders are to stay with the party, and the captain's orders to the party are to treat her *"with the utmost respect."*

## Known details

- **Fifteen years** in the guard. Family tradition: father a guard, grandfather army, great-grandmother army.
- **Second-eldest of nine siblings** ("you must not know a lot about dwarves" — five to ten kids is normal). The youngest brother is a traveling merchant; a sister is a tailor; another sibling runs a store in the third district. Her parents ran the household like a tight military ship.
- A homebody — has wanted to leave the city walls a few times, never has.
- **Hates riding in carts** ("a huge pain in my back") and **does not trust horses**: *"They know too much. Have you looked at a horse's eyes before?"* — instantly endearing her to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], who has held this exact position for years.
- Whiskey gal; wouldn't drink on the job even if allowed. Produces sandwiches from somewhere.

## Session 35

Took up post in the hallway of [[Good For What Ails Ya]] during the night watches — [[Rhaenyra]] spotted her by sliding a dagger under the door and watching the reflection; Dura chuckled at the trick and identified herself (*"The guard captain sent me. I'm not here to hurt you"*). Sat the dawn shift with [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], trading family history and horse-distrust for his circus pitch (deflected, amiably: *"I've been warned you guys are a little bit of troublemakers"*). Escorted the party to the guardhouse and the shops the next morning; asked by [[Thorn]] to point out candidate taverns for the party's planned relocation. Squinted at [[Mike]]'s conspicuously heavy backpack ([[Dragon Eggs|contents undisclosed]]) but let it go — *"I'm just giving you guys a hard time."*

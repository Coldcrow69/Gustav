# Dura

Full name: **Dura Granitethumb** (surname given by the DM at the top of [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]]; transcript "Granite Thumb" — one word per the maintainer). Transcript variants for the given name: **Dorn / Doran / Durr** (Session 37).

> First appears (unnamed, an axe-bearing silhouette in a tavern hallway) in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]; named by [[Captain Grazug]] the next morning. **Member of the [[Stoneforge Clan]]** (maintainer-confirmed) — the clan has a large presence in [[Stonewater]]'s guard and military, which is why a city-guard veteran wears Stoneforge colors. She works for [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] "through his estate" but does not report to him directly (her own words, Session 37).

A **dwarven guardswoman** with long black hair, assigned by [[Captain Grazug]] as the party's **official minder and protector** after the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] raid — one of his "more trusted employees." Her armor is guard-like but of a different make than the captain's, in **[[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge]] colors**; her orders are to stay with the party, and the captain's orders to the party are to treat her *"with the utmost respect."*

## Known details

- **Fifteen years** in the guard. Family tradition: father a guard, grandfather army, great-grandmother army.
- **Second-eldest of nine siblings** ("you must not know a lot about dwarves" — five to ten kids is normal). The youngest brother is a traveling merchant; a sister is a tailor; another sibling runs a store in the third district. Her parents ran the household like a tight military ship.
- A homebody — has wanted to leave the city walls a few times, never has.
- **Hates riding in carts** ("a huge pain in my back") and **does not trust horses**: *"They know too much. Have you looked at a horse's eyes before?"* — instantly endearing her to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], who has held this exact position for years.
- Whiskey gal; wouldn't drink on the job even if allowed. Produces sandwiches from somewhere.
- **Eats hearty** — her after-shift regular is a **pie shop in the second district** (meat, potatoes, carrots, veal). Reads a book on long waits; stretches when bored. Sharpens her axe as a nervous habit. **Not a fan of the cold** — gets sick often in the cavern's damp winters.
- On duty "at least until the investigation into the manor is over"; relieved by another guard for the nights, but *"even when I am not technically working I'm still supposed to keep an eye."*

## Session 36

Spent the party's spa day on a bench outside the treatment rooms at [[Raise Your Spirits]] with a book (declined the works: *"I think I would get scolded if I partook"*; also, to Eldrin's offer: "Maybe later"), fielded the party's running commentary on the shade of [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s backside with a sigh, and turned down Lyrion's *"Come get fucking plastered with us"* only on timing — *"It's better than doing paperwork."* Then led them to dinner at her pie shop (whistled up the order and **paid**, three pies for herself), recommended the **[[Sour Jackal]]** in the Clock Tower District for lodging — *"Bless Dura, this was a good choice"* (Seren) — and **took a mug** at the mead toast. To [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], on the subject of pants coming off: *"Don't do that."* *"You guys have certainly made my job interesting."*

## Session 37

Back an hour before the party woke, sharpening her axe on a stool she'd quietly borrowed from the [[Sour Jackal]]'s pub ([[Through the Bloodied Rubble - Session 37 - 2026-10-25|recap]]). Her night relief's report: no creature, no disappearances, and *"a pretty adventurous group."* Slept *"nervous but good"* — the [[Victor Stonehammer|revenant]] is overdue by the party's own timeline, and it *"has killed quite a bit of the guard."* Confirmed her assignment runs **at least until the manor investigation closes**. Gave her read on [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] with a grain of salt attached (stoic; cares about his workers; she doesn't report to him). Took two "no offense"s from [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] in one breakfast. Had her name **added to the mine writ** by [[Gwynn (Stoneforge Headmaid)|Gwynn]]; was told by Baldrin to inform her captain of the contract; was asked by Lyrion whether she was *"ready to experience psychological horrors potentially beyond your comprehension"* (*"I don't think it's gonna be that crazy"* — Rhaenyra, who has heard the aboleth stories, looked less sure than she did). Turned down **five gold to test Lyrion's [[Truth Serum (Lyrion)|potion]]** — *"At least buy me a beer first"* — and, at the bloodied cave-in, asked [[Rhaenyra]] *"is this how the group usually is?"* before following [[Seren Moss|Seren]] through the gap. Second into the breach.

## Session 35

Took up post in the hallway of [[Good For What Ails Ya]] during the night watches — [[Rhaenyra]] spotted her by sliding a dagger under the door and watching the reflection; Dura chuckled at the trick and identified herself (*"The guard captain sent me. I'm not here to hurt you"*). Sat the dawn shift with [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], trading family history and horse-distrust for his circus pitch (deflected, amiably: *"I've been warned you guys are a little bit of troublemakers"*). Escorted the party to the guardhouse and the shops the next morning; asked by [[Thorn]] to point out candidate taverns for the party's planned relocation. Squinted at [[Mike]]'s conspicuously heavy backpack ([[Dragon Eggs|contents undisclosed]]) but let it go — *"I'm just giving you guys a hard time."*

# Kelemvor

**Lord of the Dead.** Judge of the Damned. **God of death and the dead** — fair yet cold. The Great Guide who leads everyone into their next life.

## Alignment

Lawful Neutral.

## Dogma

Death is a **natural part of life** and should not be feared **as long as it is understood**. Kelemvor takes the essences of the deceased and assigns them to their appropriate places in the eternal cycle of existence.

His clergy is **bidden to help people meet their deaths with dignity at their proper time and not before**. Unlike followers of darker death-gods, they have **no intent to wreak devastation or spread death**. In word and deed they **oppose those who extend their lifespans beyond what is natural** by using necromancy and other magic.

This is the foundation of [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s "just death and protection of the dead" framing.

## Worshippers in vault

- **[[Seren Moss]]** — Cleric of Kelemvor; raised in the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]].
- **[[Gwyn Moss]]** — Shrinekeeper of the same shrine.
- **[[Ilsa|Aunt Ilsa]]** — Sister (cleric) of Kelemvor; cook and temple worker.
- Other acolytes at the shrine (unnamed).

Seren spent time in the shrines doing charity work during the [[Fish People]] arc.

## Acolyte duties (per Seren's upbringing)

- Praying
- Cleaning the chapel
- Tending the grounds (including the carefully-kept green lawn of the shrine cemetery)
- **Preparing bodies for burial**
- **Consoling the bereaved**
- **Honing battle skills to frighten away graverobbers**

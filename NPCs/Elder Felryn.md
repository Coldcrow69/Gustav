# Elder Felryn

> Transcript variants: "Felren," "Feldron." Recalled in [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]]. **Deceased** — died when [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] was "rather young."

An **elder of the Thalor brothers' home village** (unnamed on-page), remembered from **story night** — the elves' nearest thing to a spa: the elders pass around the wine and tell (or listen to) tales. Felryn's were the ridiculous ones. His signature: the time he **faced three dragons by himself and slew them** — dragons *"bigger than the biggest trees."* And, per [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], people believed him. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] remembers him only a little; Eldrin remembers him as *"always full of shit."*

Having now seen one dragon ([[The God Of The Iron Peaks]] — multi-headed, at that), the brothers' verdict: *"could you imagine taking on three? By yourself?"* And the inverse worry — *"I shudder to think of the tales we might have to tell at Elder Night if we ever return home."* *"They wouldn't believe us."*

Full name: **Gwyn Moss**.
Aliases / variant spellings: **Gwen** (in older player-character-sheet notes).

[[Seren Moss]]'s adoptive mother. **Shrinekeeper of [[Kelemvor]]** at the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]]. Her surname is the family name Seren carries. Per DM narrative, the Moss family is described as "an aging human family."

Reference image (supplemental): ![[Gwyn Moss.png]]

## Description

> Source: *Seren (OG Crypt Keepers)* short story.

A small, **gray-brown sparrow of a person**, looking as if she'd been pulled from the very earth she tended. The **white in her hair far outweighs the brown** in recent days. **Dark brown eyes**, inscrutable. Bony fingers. Voice calm and steady; demeanor unflappable.

Helps run the shrine alongside her **brother [[Uncle Bren]]** and **three sisters** ("[[The Aunties]]"), with acolytes assisting.

## With Seren

Adopted Seren when she "appeared on the steps of the shrine" as a baby. Called her "the most wonderful, unexpected gift that life has ever sent me."

After [[Victor Barun]] offered Seren employment to investigate the missing farmers in [[Fort Elm]], Seren hesitated to leave. Gwyn encouraged her — calmly, despite anticipating she'd miss her every moment she was gone — to **go, see the world, and learn as much as she could**, suggesting Seren might be meant for greater things than the one chapel. This is the conversation that puts Seren on the road with the party.

> Recorded quote: *"I've loved you from the moment you appeared on the steps of the shrine, my child. You were the most wonderful, unexpected gift that life has ever sent me. But I ask you to consider that perhaps you were meant for greater things than to stay in this one little chapel for your whole life."*

## Fish People arc — kidnap and rescue

Gwyn was **abducted during the second river attack** by the fish-monsters (kuo-toa) along with dozens of other Fort Elm townsfolk. Seren confirmed she was alive via a **Sending** spell — first hard proof a rescue was still possible.

Found in the prisoners' holding building in the [[The Ancient Ruined City|ancient ruined city]]. **Seren awoke and healed her**, and Gwyn in turn helped heal the other survivors. Returned safely to [[Fort Elm]] in the rescue caravan led by Seren, [[Bell]], and [[Teron]]; later **assisted with the wounded** at the shrine alongside Seren during the build-up to the final battle.

## Status

**Alive and recovered**, back at the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]].

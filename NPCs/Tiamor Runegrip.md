# Tiamor Runegrip

> Transcript variants: "Tiamor Brungrip," "Rune Grip," "Tia Moore." First appears in [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]].

**Owner of [[Raise Your Spirits]]**, the spa and spirit bar on [[Stonewater]]'s highest tier — and the **younger sister of the matriarch of the [[Runegrip Clan]]**. A striking, impeccably kept dwarven woman: long red hair in two braids down the length of her back, no beard, well dressed, the manner of someone who keeps up appearances professionally. Greets guests at her own reception desk; checks on them between treatments; keeps a humble round office full of ledgers.

## What she wants

- **Entertainment.** She caught the word "carnival" on the party's way in and, once they'd relaxed, pitched them in her office: a **livelier-than-usual event in a week or two**, for the **upper echelon** — **visitors from [[Silvermire]]** and the **city council**. Music, a performance; she hadn't yet set a budget, but *"given the clientele it's probably going to be a rather well-paying job"*, and a free set of treatments *"could be arranged."* She wants a **firm yes or no** from the [[Twilight Masqueraders]] once they've talked it over. *"I think you have a new little regular."* — *"I think so too."*
- **Acquaintances.** *"Outside of business I'm very much a person who likes to make acquaintances and friends with people who come and go."* She'd already heard through her contacts that a group had been sent to help with the city's troubles — *"it's nice to finally put a face to those people."*

## On the murders

Asked by [[Bell]] about the pickaxe killings: she keeps out of that side of politics — *"I'll stick to my little business up here on the hill"* — but her **eldest sister, the Runegrip matriarch, has been very stressed**: civil unrest, people scared, and the missing-then-found dead drawn from **different clans, a few from the Runegrips themselves**. Each clan leader handles their own guard; the city's sections are policed on a rotating schedule. She **offered to drum up information through her contacts** (Thorn: *"when people are feeling relaxed, they talk"*) — including on anything suspicious beyond the murders, which [[Seren Moss|Seren]] told her the guard ([[Dura]], present) already knows about. *(The maintainer has ruled this stays the general offer made on-page, not the Greybraids-specific version in the Session 37 opening recap.)*

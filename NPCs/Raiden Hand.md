# Raiden Hand

The **convening entity** at whose table the [[!Crypt Keepers|Crypt Keepers]] campaign runs. Better known to participants as **Raiden** or **the DM**.

## Operational signature

The Raiden Hand possesses:

- Complete prior intelligence on every party member's backstory.
- Total scenario-shaping authority over the world of [[Azadin]] and its inhabitants.
- Plausible deniability behind the procedural fig leaf of dice rolls.

## Notable attributed operations

- **[[The Drow Incident]]** (9/11/24): targeted asset insertion of a bound, unconscious Drow commoner into the path of the Thalor brothers, exploiting a known psychological vulnerability and producing — through cascade — the operational vehicle of the next arc ([[Sail la Vie]]).

## Authorship of [[Azadin]] itself

The Raiden Hand is the named author of the **[[Azadin]] world-doc**, the **[[Glyph Scar]]** lore framework, the **[[The Wilds]]** regional structure, the regional map, and all NPC dispositions. Effectively the prime mover of the recorded reality, though this is generally not discussed at the table.

# Mystra

**Goddess of Magic.** "Mother of all Magic." Tends to [[The Weave]] constantly, making possible all the miracles and mysteries wrought by magic and its users. Believed in some traditions to be the embodiment of the Weave and of magic itself.

## Alignment

Neutral Good (this incarnation).

## Symbol

A **ring of seven stars** surrounding a rising **red mist**, spiraling to the heavens.

## Dogma

The church preserves magical lore so that magic continues and flourishes in the future, even if the dominant races were to fall. Members seek out those skilled in magic or with the potential to use it, watching for promising spellcasters. Clerics are encouraged to **explore magical theory and create new spells and magic items**.

## Patron of

- **[[Teron]]** — Dragonborn Paladin. Guided him at the altar in the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]] after the aboleth battle to take the ancient offerings (spell scrolls, [[Sail la Vie]], 5,400 gp coin sack).

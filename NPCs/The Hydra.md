# The Hydra (Bluefield Burning)

A **five-headed hydra** that plagued **[[Roseview]]** ([[Rhaenyra]]'s home village) and stalked the riverbank between [[Fort Elm]] and Roseview in the northern [[The Wilds]]. Slain by the party during the [[Bluefield Burning]] arc.

## Description

- **5 heads**, with a **decapitated head stump that is very scarred** — visible evidence of prior combat.
- **Scales singed and damaged** — prior burns.
- Reptilian, two legs + tail (per claw-mark analysis).
- **Asymmetric front claws**: **left foot — 2 front-facing claws and 1 back claw**; **right foot — 3 front-facing claws and 1 back claw**.

## Behavior

Hunted along the riverbank. **Uprooted 6 ft-diameter trees**. Left **snake-trail tracks** going from river to land and back. Bit a chunk out of a **bobcat** (carcass found by the party).

## Encounter

The party identified its sign on 10/1/25 and **baited it ashore using bear traps along the riverbank** while hiding the [[Sail la Vie]] out of the water. It emerged at nightfall. The fight took place on 10/8/25.

## Loot

The party gathered **hydra eyes, teeth, blood, and hide** (10/15/25). **[[Mike]] cooked and ate some hydra meat** — any consequence not yet recorded.

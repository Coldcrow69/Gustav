Full name: **Keva Moss**.

One of **[[Gwyn Moss]]'s three sisters** (see [[The Aunties]]). [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s adoptive aunt at the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]].

Reference image (supplemental): ![[Keva Moss.png]]

## Role

**Fighter / sparring trainer.** Trained Seren in martial combat — part of the shrine's defensive culture (acolytes of [[Kelemvor]] hone battle skills against graverobbers). Sharp demeanor; sternness laid over real concern.

## At Seren's departure

Stepped up hands-on-hips, told Seren: *"Remember your training. And keep up with your practice every day. I've seen that you still don't guard your left side properly; work on that. Come back to us in one piece, girl."*

Open critique recorded: **Seren still doesn't guard her left side properly.**

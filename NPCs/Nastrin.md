# Nastrin

Aliases: *the stout dwarven barkeep*.

**Barkeep / proprietor at [[Good For What Ails Ya]]** — the tavern in [[Stonewater]]'s lower district where the party has been lodging during the [[Egg Hunt]] arc.

## Role

- Serves food and drink to the party each morning and evening they are in residence (daily special, breakfast mead, pastries).
- Reliable **local-knowledge source**: knows the city's clans, attractions, and what's worth a visitor's time.

## Session 30 — Identifies the Greybraids emblem (2026-05-18)

When [[Seren Moss|Seren]] sketched the **feather-and-dagger insignia** she had seen on the occupants of the suspicious cart, Nastrin identified it on sight as belonging to the **[[Greybraids]] clan** of [[Stonewater]] — providing the party with the lead that anchors the session's investigation. He earlier told [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] there might be a **play** in town that day.

Seren tipped him a silver for the identification.

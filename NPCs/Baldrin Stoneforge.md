Full name: **Baldrin Stoneforge** (also recorded as "Baldrun Stoneforge" in chronicler notes).

Foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]]. Resides in [[Stonewater]] (also recorded as "Stoneforge" / "Ironforge" in chronicler notes). Business associate of [[Victor Barun]], who sent the party to assist with disappearances and murders linked to the mines.

## Session 29 — Audience in Stonewater (2026-05-11)

[[Seren Moss|Seren]] introduced the party at Baldrin's residence, explaining they had been sent by [[Victor Barun]] to assist with the missing-people problem. She reported that [[Bluefield]] had been destroyed by a red dragon known as [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]], that the party had captured a half-dragon from the attacking force ([[Captured Half-Dragon]]) who revealed the dragon's eggs had been stolen, that local firbolgs had aided Bluefield's refugees, and that suspicious human adventurers had been observed passing through before the attack. She requested aid locating the stolen eggs to prevent further assaults.

The party then questioned Baldrin about the mine disappearances and murders — the manner of death, the type of weapon, the status of the accused man's corpse and weapon (both vanished) — and suggested a possible magical cause such as a curse or compulsion. [[Thorn]] demonstrated the cart-driver suspect's appearance by shapeshifting into him (the driver of the **separate cart** that traveled alongside [[Basil]]'s caravan — not Basil himself). [[Rhaenyra]] described a **feather-and-dagger insignia** seen on the cart's occupants and asked Baldrin its meaning, warning that the dragon could destroy the city if the eggs were not recovered. (Identified the next morning by [[Nastrin]] as the **[[Greybraids]]** clan crest — see [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-18|Session 30]].)

The party and Baldrin agreed to reconvene the next day.

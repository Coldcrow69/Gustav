Full name: **Brenan Moss**. Goes by **Bren** (and "Uncle Bren" to [[Seren Moss|Seren]]).

Reference image (supplemental): ![[Brenan Moss.jpg]]

[[Gwyn Moss]]'s brother. [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s adoptive uncle. Helps keep the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]] running alongside Gwyn and **[[The Aunties]]** (Ilsa, Keva, Anya).

## Role

**Healer / medic** of the family. **Packed Seren's healer's kit** for the journey — extra bandages in the **pocket on the left side**.

## At Seren's departure

Rocked back on his heels, indecisive, then stepped forward for a brief but **crushing hug**: *"Take care of yourself, kid. And those friends of yours."* When Seren said she'd miss him, he **cleared his throat and stepped back, looking down and away, unable to say any more.**

Among the people [[Seren Moss|Seren]] loves and was reluctant to leave when [[Victor Barun]] offered her ongoing employment.

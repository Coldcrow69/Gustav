# Kassadren

> Canonical spelling per the maintainer: **Kassadren**. Variant: **"Krasstren"** (an earlier vault revision briefly canonized this form off the transcript; superseded). **Not yet met on-page.**

**Patriarch of the [[Greybraids]]** dwarf clan of [[Stonewater]] — and, per the [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|cult Elder]]'s own statement, the person who **granted the [[Acolytes of Oghma]] legal right to occupy the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]]** where [[Seren Moss|Seren]] later sensed **at least two stolen [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]]** and an [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]] ([[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]).

His name reached the party twice in one session:

1. At the **[[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|Greybraid Manor]] gate**, where the clan guard agreed to relay [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s message "to the patriarch" — Kassadren by name.
2. At the **cliffside manor**, where the Elder cited him as the sect's landlord.

## What this means (open)

The clan whose emblem rode the egg-thieves' cart is led by the man who houses the sect sitting on the eggs. Whether Kassadren **knows** what his tenants keep in the cellar, whether the **egg theft runs through him**, and whether the "sect of healers" story is his cover or theirs — all unresolved. [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s Session 32 tracking sweep found **no eggs at the clan's own manor, forge, or mine entrance** — consistent with the eggs being parked at arm's length on his property line instead.

A reply to Lyrion's gate inquiry, if it ever comes, is to be routed through **[[Captain Grazug]]**.

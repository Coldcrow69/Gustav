# Twilight Masqueraders

A small **performing troupe** founded by **[[Thorn]]** as a **one-woman act**. Thorn expanded the troupe over time, integrating other performers as they joined her life.

## Members

- **[[Thorn]]** — founder and lead performer
- **[[Lyrion Thalor]]** — recruited during the [[Fish People]] arc (initial performance at [[Victor Barun]]'s retirement party)
- **[[Eldrin Thalor]]** — recruited alongside his brother; later class-swapped from Ranger to **Bard** via the troupe's trip to the Bard / Clown College during the 2025-09-17 downtime

Other party members ([[Bell]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Mike]], [[Rhaenyra]]) participate in adventuring alongside the troupe but are **not formal members** of it.

## Repertoire

The troupe runs a varied act blending **multiple performance disciplines**:

- **Theater and masked performance** — commedia-style harlequinade and masked acts. The "Masqueraders" name reflects this lineage.
- **Music and bardic performance** — Eldrin's lute, ballads, sung pieces. Eldrin's class swap to Bard formalized this side of the act.
- **Trapeze and acrobatics** — Thorn's specialty; see Raine's `Trapeze.png` art on [[Raine's Crypt Keeper Art]].
- **Wizard duels as showpieces** — staged magical performances. The Roseview festival (11/5/25) featured one; Lyrion participated and ended up scorched.

## Business model

**Paying gigs at festivals and private events.** The troupe hires out for retirement parties, town festivals, and similar engagements — [[Victor Barun]]'s retirement party (in [[Fort Elm]]) was a paid contract, and the [[Roseview]] hydra-slaying festival featured them. Income from performing supplements (and historically may have led to) the party's adventuring work.

## Calling cards

The troupe travels with **calling cards** — [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] presented one to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] during the [[Egg Hunt]] Session 29 audience while identifying the group as performers-turned-adventurers.

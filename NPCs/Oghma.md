# Oghma

**God of knowledge** ("patron god of knowledge," per [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s religion check in [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]] — the extent of what the party knows in-fiction so far).

## Presence in vault

- The **[[Acolytes of Oghma]]** at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] in [[Stonewater]] claim him as their patron. Given the sect's conduct — necrotic magic, an [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]] below their house, stolen [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]], killing their own — whether Oghma has anything to do with them is **very much in doubt**. No recognizable holy symbol was seen on their robes.

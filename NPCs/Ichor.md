# Ichor

> Spelling **Ichor** per the maintainer ([[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]]); transcript/Archivist variants **"Aiker," "Iker."** First appears in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]. The DM's Session 36 opening-recap slip calling him "the friendly kobold in the pumpkin" is a slip — he is a **kenku**, as described in full in Session 35. Not to be confused with [[Black Ichor]], the revenant's residue.

A **kenku** — a crow-like bird person, a startling sight in dwarven [[Stonewater]] — proprietor of **[[Ichor's Potion Explorium]]**, the pumpkin-built potion shop near the guard district. Dresses sharply: vest, white shirt, red ascot. Greets crowds with *"Hello! Hi, I'm Ichor, and welcome to Ichor's Potion Explorium."*

- Sells potions in **custom vegetable-shaped vials** — carrot (healing), onion (greater healing), pumpkin (superior healing) — plus climbing, elemental-resistance, luck, giant-strength, watchful-rest, and rarer stock (see the [[Ichor's Potion Explorium|shop page]]).
- Cannot brew resurrection-grade potions, and says so plainly.
- **Does the math on an abacus**; a fair dealer ([[Thorn]] insight-checked him on a price — telling the truth).
- Entertained [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s scroll-barter pitch against the 7,000 gp **Potion of Vitality** with professional patience — and, off-screen before [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]], **declined it**: the gap between the price and Lyrion's scrolls was too wide. Lyrion walked away disappointed and briefly, half-heartedly, considered theft. He didn't.
- Stocks **healing and recovery** potions, not utility ones — nothing like Water Breathing is brewed anywhere in [[Stonewater]]; for that the party would have to go **east, deeper into the wilds** (Session 36).

# Blibdoolpoolp

**"The Sea Mother"** / **"The Drowning Goddess."** Patron goddess of the **kuo-toa**. A paranoid, reality-denying deity of the sea of questionable sanity.

## Character

**Bloodthirsty** and full of hatred for the pain her kuo-toa "children" have suffered at the hands of many races. Special enmity is held for **surface-dwelling races**, as well as illithids and drow. **Humans and especially elves** are spited for the initial driving of her children to the underdark.

**Irrational**, prone to unpredictable mood swings and fickle changes in behavior. Highly **secretive**, shunning contact with most other deities. Believes herself to hold deeply fundamental magical secrets related to the nature of the universe, but hoards those secrets to herself.

## Powers (when manifest)

- Spellcasting — heavy in **summoning** and **elemental control of water**; lacks fire control.
- Can **render victims insane** by forcing them to meet her gaze.
- Creates **symbols of insanity** to drive foes to madness.

## Nature

In their insane state, the kuo-toa invented gods to protect themselves. The subconscious belief-energy of enough kuo-toa simultaneously can cause such an entity to **manifest in reality** — giving the kuo-toa access to a kind of divine power drawn from their own faith. Whether Blibdoolpoolp is "real" in the conventional sense or a belief-construct is left ambiguous.

## Presence in vault

- **[[Stone Idol of Blibdoolpoolp]]** — found by [[Bell]] and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] in the cavern complex beneath [[Fort Elm]] during the [[Fish People]] arc. Cylindrical, fish-spine carved along the back; glows faintly of conjuration magic; errs Divine over Arcane.
- The fish-people warband ([[The Undead Aboleth|aboleth]]'s servitors) overlapped with kuo-toa, suggesting either Blibdoolpoolp worship in the cavern complex or the aboleth's exploitation of pre-existing kuo-toa worshippers. **Unresolved.**

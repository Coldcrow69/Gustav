# Gwynn (Stoneforge Headmaid)

> **Not [[Gwyn Moss]]** — Seren's adoptive mother in Fort Elm is a different person who happens to share the name. First named in [[Through the Bloodied Rubble - Session 37 - 2026-10-25|Session 37]]; **maintainer-confirmed** as the same staff member who received [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], [[Rhaenyra]], and [[Bell]] at the manor in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]].

**Headmaid of the Stoneforge estate** in [[Stonewater]] — [[Baldrin Stoneforge]]'s household on the city's highest tier (rock garden out front). A dwarf; speaks **Dwarvish** with [[Seren Moss|Seren]] and greets her by name — *"Seren!"* — on opening the door.

- Keeps the party's **[[Mine Pass|mine writ]] current**: *"You still have that writ I gave you? I need to update it for today."* In Session 37 she **added [[Dura]]'s name** and refreshed the date, then sent them to the mine check-in to ask for Baldrin.
- *"I'm glad you checked in."* — *"Have a good day, be safe."*

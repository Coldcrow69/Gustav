# The Undead Aboleth

The **central antagonist of the [[Fish People]] arc.** An **undead aboleth** entombed for untold ages in a pit at the center of the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth|cathedral]] inside the [[The Ancient Ruined City|ancient ruined city]] beneath [[Fort Elm]].

## What it was doing

- Emitting **slime** that coated the cavern system and corrupted the [[Rollingbrook River]] above.
- Sending out **kuo-toa minions** that **transformed back into slime upon death** — confirmed by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] when he witnessed the reverse-transformation in the cathedral.
- Compelling its servants with the psionic refrain: *"Gather the living, bring me more flesh!"*
- Conjuring **fish-shaped minions from slime** during direct combat.
- Reaching into minds — communicated psionically with [[Teron]] (charming him into stabbing [[Seren Moss|Seren]]) and with Lyrion (whom it nearly trapped in an illusion of the cathedral's interior, conjuring the image of his **dead mother** as a deception).

## How it was sealed

[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] judged from **runes on spikes impaled in the creature** — left there for untold ages — and from symbols within the well, that **a very powerful spell** had once been cast to keep the thing entombed in the pit. The seal had begun to fail; the aboleth was actively trying to free itself, calling out:

> *"NO! FREE ME! FREE ME!"*

## The kill

Killed by a final **fireball from Lyrion** at the end of the final-day cathedral assault. The fight nearly slew [[Teron]] and [[Seren Moss|Seren]], who were revived by [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] afterward. [[Bell]] was charmed for much of the fight.

A psionic scream of rage echoed through the party's minds as it died.

## Loot recovered

- [[Sail la Vie]] — Teron, from the altar (the ornately decorated mahogany box turned out to be a ship)

## Initial misidentification

When Lyrion first encountered it he assumed it was an **undead beholder**. Corrected to **aboleth** after the [[Drow Elder]]'s identification and the final battle.

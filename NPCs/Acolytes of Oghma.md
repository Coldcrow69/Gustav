# Acolytes of Oghma

> Self-description — treat with suspicion. The sect **claims** to be acolytes of [[Oghma]], god of knowledge, and "a sect of healers." Their conduct in [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]] — hold-person ambushes, spectral sickles dealing **necrotic** harm, and **killing their own to prevent surrender** — says otherwise. The [[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|journal]] found in [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]] gives their true shape: a **mortality-dread support group turned remembrance-cult** — *"you are only truly dead when you are forgotten."*

A robed sect occupying the **[[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]]** in [[Stonewater]] as a "newer residency," granted legally by **[[Kassadren]]**, patriarch of the [[Greybraids]]. Roughly **a dozen** people come and go; the members seen are **mostly dwarves** (the door greeter, [[Vil]], was human). Robes are priestly-looking; [[Vil]] wore an **amulet with no recognizable holy symbol**.

Beneath their floor: a concealed cave system, an [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]], and — by [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s tracking — **at least two stolen [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]]** their [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]] denies exist.

## What the journal revealed (Session 34)

The **[[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|unnamed member's journal]]** recovered from the manor's second floor traces the sect's origin: severe **existential dread of death and of being forgotten** → a **support group** of fellow sufferers → **fanaticism**. Doctrine: *"you are only truly dead when you are forgotten"* — immortality through unforgettable deeds. The writer traveled **from the north into the wilds on a mission from the community to recover something in [[Stonewater]]**. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s read: their fear is no longer death; they mean to do something so extreme they can never be forgotten — *"something that perhaps involves powerful artifacts… dragon eggs."* Consistent with the Session 33 battle cry: *"We will not be forgotten!"*

The manor's closets held their **cultists' robes** alongside ordinary traveling and caravan supplies — a sect that moves.

## Known members

- **[[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|The Elder]]** — leader; denied everything; ordered the ambush euphemism ("see them well taken care of"); fled Witch-Bolt-maimed in Session 33 and was **not found** in the Session 34 manor search. At large.
- **[[Vil]]** — young human greeter/escort; killed by [[Mike]] on the cellar stairs (Session 33).
- **[[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|The bald mage]]** — ornate robes, wand of force bolts; **killed [[Lyrion Thalor]]** (since revived); fought the whole party in [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]] (Invisibility, Shield, Counterspell, Cone of Cold) and **escaped or hid — unaccounted for**.
- **An ornate-robed cellar figure** — hurled a fire blast down the cellar corridor (Session 33); distinct from the bald mage; presumed among the Session 33–34 dead.
- **Rank-and-file cultists** — sickles, Hold Person, Command, a conjured **spectral sickle** (slashing + necrotic; their weapons carry necrotic harm generally). Battle cry: *"We will not be forgotten!"* One cut down his own wavering comrade — *"You coward!"* — rather than let him surrender. **All rank-and-file at the manor are now dead**: [[Bell]] cleaved one in two and put her axe through a second's skull; [[Rhaenyra]] (saber-toothed tiger) tore out the last one's throat (Session 34).

## Open questions

- What the sect actually venerates — the [[Oghma]] front does not explain necrotic magic, the aberration, or the eggs; the journal gives an ideology (**remembrance**) but no god.
- What the northern community sent its agents to **recover in [[Stonewater]]** — the [[Dragon Eggs|eggs]] themselves, or something the eggs serve.
- Whether [[Kassadren]] is patron, dupe, or partner.
- Whether the sect's members overlap with the **hooded crew of the [[Greybraids]]-emblem cart** from Session 29 ([[Seren Moss|Seren]] watched for familiar faces; no match confirmed).
- How large the parent community in the north is — the manor's dozen were evidently a field cell.
- Party table-fears before the reveal — a **Tiamat** egg-cult, or "the whispered one" — remain speculation only.

# Acolytes of Oghma

> Self-description — treat with suspicion. The sect **claims** to be acolytes of [[Oghma]], god of knowledge, and "a sect of healers." Their conduct in [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]] — hold-person ambushes, spectral sickles dealing **necrotic** harm, and **killing their own to prevent surrender** — says otherwise.

A robed sect occupying the **[[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]]** in [[Stonewater]] as a "newer residency," granted legally by **[[Kassadren]]**, patriarch of the [[Greybraids]]. Roughly **a dozen** people come and go; the members seen are **mostly dwarves** (the door greeter, [[Vil]], was human). Robes are priestly-looking; [[Vil]] wore an **amulet with no recognizable holy symbol**.

Beneath their floor: a concealed cave system, an [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]], and — by [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s tracking — **at least two stolen [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]]** their [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]] denies exist.

## Known members

- **[[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|The Elder]]** — leader; denied everything; ordered the ambush euphemism ("see them well taken care of"); fled badly wounded.
- **[[Vil]]** — young human greeter/escort; killed by [[Mike]] on the cellar stairs.
- **[[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|The bald mage]]** — ornate robes, wand of force bolts; **killed [[Lyrion Thalor]]**; still standing.
- **An ornate-robed cellar figure** — hurled a fire blast down the cellar corridor; distinct from the bald mage; still active.
- **Rank-and-file cultists** — sickles, Hold Person, a conjured **spectral sickle** (slashing + necrotic). Battle cry: *"We will not be forgotten!"* One cut down his own wavering comrade — *"You coward!"* — rather than let him surrender.

## Open questions

- What the sect actually venerates — the [[Oghma]] front does not explain necrotic magic, the aberration, or the eggs.
- Whether [[Kassadren]] is patron, dupe, or partner.
- Whether the sect's members overlap with the **hooded crew of the [[Greybraids]]-emblem cart** from Session 29 ([[Seren Moss|Seren]] watched for familiar faces; no match confirmed).
- Party table-fears before the reveal — a **Tiamat** egg-cult, or "the whispered one" — remain speculation only.

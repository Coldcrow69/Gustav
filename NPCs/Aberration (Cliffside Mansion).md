# Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)

> **Unseen.** Known only through [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s divinations in [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]. **Mobile, and fleeing downward.**

A **malignant aberration** beneath the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] in [[Stonewater]] — the "unwholesome presence" Seren sensed below the building on approach, and the thing whose trail she chased through the manor's **concealed cellar door** into a **natural cave system**, past a **ladder descending deeper**, always ahead of her senses. It **moved away** as the fight began; she never caught sight of it.

The party's dread is explicit: the last aberration they faced was [[The Undead Aboleth]]. *"Surely it wouldn't be the same thing twice… could it?"*

## Working notes

- The [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|cult Elder]] **denied its existence** — while living directly above it, behind a door disguised as cellar wall.
- The caves it fled into **resemble the mine workings** — the party suspects a connection to the **pre-existing passage** the [[Ironheart Mines]] miners breached (where miners were *attacked* by something unknown).
- Whether it relates to the [[Dragon Eggs|eggs]], the cult's real object of worship, or the cave-in "attack" is unresolved.

# Murdered Mine Survivor (Stonewater Alley)

> Unnamed on-page. The **husband** of the [[Dwarven Woman (Stonewater Pickaxe Body)|dwarven woman]] who found him. (Archivist variants: "Dead Dwarf," "The Victim," "The Corpse.")

A male **dwarf**, a **survivor of the [[Ironheart Mines]] cave-in**, murdered in an alley in [[Stonewater]]'s lower district near [[Good For What Ails Ya]] — the body discovered at the close of [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-17|Session 30]] and examined at the start of [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]].

## The scene

- Killed by a **pickaxe through the eye**; the weapon bore the **[[Stoneforge Clan]] insignia** ([[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] recognized it from around [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s house).
- Slashes and **puncture wounds** to the thigh; signs of a struggle nearby (knocked-over crates and trash cans).
- His **knife was missing** — the sheath on his hip empty.
- A separate pool of **[[Black Ichor|black ichor]]** mixed with his blood (the killer's residue).

## Speak with Dead

[[Seren Moss|Seren]] arrived too late to restore him (beyond the one-minute Revivify window) and instead cast **Speak with Dead** (five questions). Confirmed: he survived the [[Ironheart Mines]] cave-in; his killer was a rotting-skinned dwarf named *"Victor"* (→ [[Victor Stonehammer]]); and during the cave-in he had seen something someone would silence him for — *"There was… people in the cave. I didn't see much. We ran."*

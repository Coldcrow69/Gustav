Aliases / variant spellings recorded: **Braun**, **Victor Barron**.

> **Not [[Victor Stonehammer]].** A separate **dwarf** of [[Stonewater]] — the revenant in [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]] — shares the given name "Victor." Victor **Barun** (the human fishing mogul described below) is unrelated and uninvolved. Archivist exports conflated the two; do not merge them.

A **middle-aged, rotund, kind and jolly fellow** — always one to help in a time of need. A **wealthy retired Fishing Mogul** who runs (or rather, ran) a fishing operation in [[Fort Elm]] and is a **popular figurehead** in town. A mutual friend of the entire party — each PC was individually invited to his gathering, the [[Prologue]] hook.

`![[victor barun.webp]]`

**Appearance reference**: an older man with a full head of **thick silver-white hair** and a **long, well-kept white beard with a darker grey moustache**. Bright, kindly eyes set in a weathered, ruddy face with deep laugh lines. Open-collared shirt under a fisherman's blue overshirt. The portrait reads warm and grandfatherly — consistent with the "kind and jolly fellow" written record.

## Household

Located in the **northern part of [[Fort Elm]]** — the **nicer part of town**. The household itself is **covered in flowering ivy and greenery**. Its placement **on the river** indicates that Barun may be a little better off than he lets on.

Business associate of [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] (foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]]). Passed control of his fishing business to [[Evelyn]] at his retirement party — the inciting event of the [[Fish People]] arc.

## Fish People arc

At Victor's retirement party, he handed off control to [[Evelyn]]. Six individuals were invited to attend, alongside several **figureheads of [[Fort Elm]]**. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] were in the performance with [[Thorn]] then left after their set; Thorn and the others were invited to stay after for a talk. **Victor's body was found** — commotion and mystery ensued. Investigation proceeded.

Subsequently revealed: the corpse was a **doppelganger** (one — not multiple). Investigation found a man named **[[Gorge]]** who was embezzling funds from Victor using the doppelganger; when the gig was up, Gorge killed the doppelganger. The dead doppelganger had **death-white skin**.

The real Victor was recovered via a **tight tunnel beneath the wine cellar** of his mansion — wet earth and gray darkness pressing in on all sides. The underground passage also contained **strange algae** and a **horrible gel-like encasement around a skeleton whose screams Seren can still hear echoing in her mind**. The descent was made by [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Bell]], and [[Teron|Teran]]; Bell and Teran seemed unshaken, while Seren has carried the memory as her worst recurring fear.

Once recovered, Victor was alive and found. He spoke with the four party members and asked them to do him a favor. Lent [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] the book [[A Brief Delve into the History of Plainmore Woods]]. He later summoned the party back to his mansion for breakfast to discuss things.

**Specific commission to [[Seren Moss|Seren]]** (day after the recovery): ongoing employment to investigate the **missing farmers** of [[Fort Elm]]. This is the offer that drew Seren onto the road with the rest of the party (see her [[Seren Moss#Backstory|backstory]]).

He eventually also tasked the party with **delivering fish to [[Stonewater]] and meeting someone there** — which became the [[Bluefield Burning]] / [[Egg Hunt]] pivot.

## Resolution of the doppelganger affair

- **Why was Victor impersonated?** — [[Gorge]]'s embezzling scheme; opportunistic, no deeper conspiracy.
- **Who was behind it?** — [[Gorge]] alone.
- **Will it happen again?** — No further doppelganger incidents on record.

## Bluefield Burning send-off

On **9/24/25** at the diner [[The Apple Thicket]] in [[Fort Elm]], Victor introduced **[[Rhaenyra]]** to the party and asked them to help her village with a multi-headed beast before continuing to [[Stonewater]]. He supplied parting gifts:

- [[Black Thermal Cube]] (to [[Bell]] via [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]])
- [[Rope of Mending]] (to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]])
- [[Everbright Lantern]] (to [[Rhaenyra]])

His parting instruction: *"Talk to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]], foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]] in [[Stonewater]]."*

## Current status

Per [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s report to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] during the [[Egg Hunt]] audience, Victor is doing well despite recent troubles in [[Fort Elm]], where a monster had been causing issues.

# Dwarven Woman (Stonewater Pickaxe Body)

> **Identity resolved (Session 31): she is the murder victim's wife/widow** — still unnamed on-page. First seen as the cliffhanger of [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-17|Session 30]]; the scene continues directly in [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]].

A **dwarven woman**, screaming and crouched over **a corpse with a pickaxe embedded in its body**, in an alley near [[Good For What Ails Ya]] in [[Stonewater]]'s lower district. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] heard the frightened voice as they were leaving the alley after their reconciliation conversation and moved toward the sound.

## Context

The body is her **husband** — a confirmed **survivor of the [[Ironheart Mines]] cave-in**, killed with a pickaxe through the eye (see [[Murdered Mine Survivor (Stonewater Alley)]]). In [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]] she ran to [[Good For What Ails Ya]] to fetch [[Seren Moss|Seren]] (sent by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]), then was led away by the guards before [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s **Speak with Dead**. The killer is the **revenant** of [[Victor Stonehammer]] — the mine murders are his vengeance on cave-in survivors, **not** (as hypothesized in Session 30) a [[Greybraids]] coverup.

## Open questions

- Her **name** (still unrecorded), and whether she herself is a mine survivor — and therefore a potential next target of the [[Victor Stonehammer]] revenant.
- She described the assailant only as **a dwarf in a black cloak**, face unseen.

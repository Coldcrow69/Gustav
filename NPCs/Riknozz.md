Aliases: **RikNoz** (Lyrion's chronicler shorthand).

A **Drow leader / captain** encountered in the cave system beneath [[Fort Elm]] during the [[Fish People]] arc.

## His sect

Riknozz's people are Drow who have **broken away from more violent sects** and chosen a more peaceful way of life. **They do not worship the [[Lolth|Spider Queen]]** — unlike the Drow Lyrion's family suffered under. The [[Drow Elder]] of this sect explained that their people had seen war and chosen peace.

## Encounters with the party

- The party met Riknozz and his people fighting fish-monsters in the caverns; the party joined on the Drow side, struck an uneasy peace, and were led to the Drow's temporary camp in the [[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)|ruined Temple of Tyr]].
- [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] had a terse exchange with Riknozz on the way to camp during which Riknozz's identity and his sect's beliefs were revealed.
- Riknozz volunteered to join the rescue effort, as more of his people had been taken prisoner by the fish monsters.
- Helped the party through the [[Riddle Door]] encounter — pushing skeletons into [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s Spirit Guardians range with [[Bell]].

## The blood debt

[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] killed a Drow prisoner on sight in the caves before Riknozz's sect was known — Lyrion's note "I think I killed someone close to him" likely refers to this. The matter was raised to the [[Drow Elder]]; **Lyrion's blood debt was settled when the party killed the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]]**.

## Status

Granted **temporary refugee status in [[Fort Elm]]** by [[Lilith]] after the arc's resolution.

# Captain Grazug

> Named in [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]] (transcript variant: "Grozeg"; formerly filed as "Guard Captain (Stonewater)"). Captain of the **Stonewater city guard** — the force in unfamiliar colors (not [[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge]], not [[Greybraids]]) based at the **third-tier Barracks**, a **retrofitted fort** with a front-desk reception area (the archivist's "Guardhouse"). The *force* was first encountered securing the Session 31 alley scene; the **captain himself** first clearly appears on-page at the [[Stonewater Sewers|sewers]] with reinforcements (the alley's lead guard reported *to* him). His having "earlier told the party to stay out of trouble" is unplaced — possibly the alley. The Session 31 and Session 32 appearances are recorded here as the **same officer** (the archivist's records treat them as one; not independently confirmed).
>
> Not to be confused with [[Lilith]], Captain of the Guard in [[Fort Elm]].

## Session 31

Arrived at the [[Stonewater Sewers]] with reinforcements after the revenant's containment — having earlier told the party to stay out of trouble — and **thanked them for saving his guard** (the [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|lady guard]] who fled to fetch help). Confirmed that the murder victims all showed **puncture wounds** and traced back to the [[Ironheart Mines]] rather than to any single clan.

## Session 32

Received [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Mike]], and [[Thorn]] at the Barracks when they offered help with the supernatural murder investigation. Heard [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s warning — the revenant can **re-form in another corpse in roughly half a day**, and Banishment is impossible until the next day — and thanked them for it.

- Stated his **number one priority is the people of the city**.
- After some fifteen to twenty minutes of paperwork, provided the **[[Writ of Deputization]]** naming the four of them for the investigation.
- Agreed to **warn the graveyard keepers** and to help assemble clerics as needed.
- On the [[Greybraids]]: the clan **depends on the mines**, the two groups **have a history**, and nothing severe has happened between them very recently.
- Referred the party to **[[Havid]]** for the details of how [[Victor Stonehammer]] died. (The archivist recorded this referral as concerning "[[Victor Barun]]" — the recurring Session 31 mislabel; Barun is unrelated.)

## Session 33

Named as the party's **message relay**: [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] asked the [[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|Greybraid Manor]] gate guard to route any reply from patriarch [[Kassadren]] through "Captain Grazug at the guard post." His writ was also the template [[Gustav]] copied for Lyrion's forged **[[Broader Warrant]]** — brandished in his force's name ("We're with the Stonewater Guard") at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]], where the party's deputized authority preceded a massacre of the [[Acolytes of Oghma|resident cult]]. What Grazug will make of all this is a problem for a future session.

# Captain Grazug

> Named in [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]] (transcript variants: "Grozeg", "Grazog", "Grousek", and the Session 35 garble "Catherine Rizal" = "Captain Grazug"; formerly filed as "Guard Captain (Stonewater)"). A **dwarf** — appearance finally established on-page at the [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]] table, the first session where the whole party (including [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], [[Bell]], and [[Rhaenyra]], who had never dealt with him directly) meets him face to face; the writ four — Lyrion, Seren, Mike, and [[Thorn]] — had been received by him at the [[Barracks (Stonewater)|Barracks]] in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]. Captain of the **Stonewater city guard** — the force in unfamiliar colors (not [[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge]], not [[Greybraids]]) based at the **third-tier Barracks**, a **retrofitted fort** with a front-desk reception area (the archivist's "Guardhouse"). The *force* was first encountered securing the Session 31 alley scene; the **captain himself** first clearly appears on-page at the [[Stonewater Sewers|sewers]] with reinforcements (the alley's lead guard reported *to* him). His having "earlier told the party to stay out of trouble" is unplaced — possibly the alley. The Session 31 and Session 32 appearances are recorded here as the **same officer** (the archivist's records treat them as one; not independently confirmed).
>
> Not to be confused with [[Lilith]], Captain of the Guard in [[Fort Elm]].

## Session 31

Arrived at the [[Stonewater Sewers]] with reinforcements after the revenant's containment — having earlier told the party to stay out of trouble — and **thanked them for saving his guard** (the [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|lady guard]] who fled to fetch help). Confirmed that the murder victims all showed **puncture wounds** and traced back to the [[Ironheart Mines]] rather than to any single clan.

## Session 32

Received [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Mike]], and [[Thorn]] at the Barracks when they offered help with the supernatural murder investigation. Heard [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s warning — the revenant can **re-form in another corpse in roughly half a day**, and Banishment is impossible until the next day — and thanked them for it.

- Stated his **number one priority is the people of the city**.
- After some fifteen to twenty minutes of paperwork, provided the **[[Writ of Deputization]]** naming the four of them for the investigation.
- Agreed to **warn the graveyard keepers** and to help assemble clerics as needed.
- On the [[Greybraids]]: the clan **depends on the mines**, the two groups **have a history**, and nothing severe has happened between them very recently.
- Referred the party to **[[Havid]]** for the details of how [[Victor Stonehammer]] died. (The archivist recorded this referral as concerning "[[Victor Barun]]" — the recurring Session 31 mislabel; Barun is unrelated.)

## Session 33

Named as the party's **message relay**: [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] asked the [[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|Greybraid Manor]] gate guard to route any reply from patriarch [[Kassadren]] through "Captain Grazug at the guard post." His writ was also the template [[Gustav]] copied for Lyrion's forged **[[Broader Warrant]]** — brandished in his force's name ("We're with the Stonewater Guard") at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]], where the party's deputized authority preceded a massacre of the [[Acolytes of Oghma|resident cult]]. What Grazug will make of all this is a problem for a future session.

## Session 35 — the reckoning

The future session arrived ([[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|recap]]). Summoned by [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s messenger to [[Good For What Ails Ya]] the evening of the raid, he heard her nearly-full account (the party's egg and the forged warrant withheld; the death downplayed — though [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], meads deep, told him the whole resurrection story) and answered with measured professionalism:

- *"For what it's worth, I believe you"* — but **opened an internal investigation**: a manor of corpses, deputized outsiders, lethal force, and no independent way to verify self-defense.
- Took **statements from everyone**: five at the tavern that night (Eldrin first — who also gave him the tunnel/cave-in connection — then [[Bell]], whose account preserved the [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]]'s *"You will be forgotten"*; [[Rhaenyra]] and [[Thorn]] briefly), and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Mike]] at the guardhouse the next morning.
- Took the **[[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|cult journal]] into evidence** (to be copied, used for identifications); dispatched **guards to lock down the manor crime scene** and posted one **atop the tunnel ladder**.
- **Kept the party's [[Writ of Deputization|deputization]] intact** — *"we may end up needing your services again"* — but assigned **[[Dura]]**, one of his most trusted, as their permanent official escort.
- Shared intelligence both ways: the **[[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|mage]]'s** description logged (and his portrait shown), the [[Greybraids]] tie noted warily — and a fresh oddity from the guardhouse: citizen reports of **something large and wingless flying at the top of the cavern** that evening before vanishing.
- Watched Thorn **transform into the wagon-driver suspect** with open shock, then pragmatically sent for a **sketch artist** to draw the borrowed face.
- Advised the party to **relocate taverns** (offered barracks housing as an option) and asked to be told where they land. As of the next morning: **no movement at the manor, no revenant reports**; he'll send word if either changes.

Seren asked one favor: if repercussions come, **let them fall on her alone**.

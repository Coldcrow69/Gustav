Full name: **Ilsa Moss**.

Full role: **Aunt Ilsa** — one of [[Gwyn Moss]]'s three sisters (see [[The Aunties]]) and a **Sister of [[Kelemvor]]** (cleric / temple worker) at the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]].

Reference image (supplemental): ![[Ilsa Moss.png]]

## Description

> Source: *Seren (Crypt Keepers)* long-form short story.

**Soft brown eyes**, warm demeanor. Handles the **cooking** at the shrine — [[Seren Moss|Seren]] typically helped her in the mornings before she left for the journey. At Seren's departure pressed a wrapped bundle of treats into her hands: *"For you and your friends. I thought it would be nice to have a couple of treats to tide you over for the journey upriver."*

Among the people [[Seren Moss|Seren]] loves and was reluctant to leave when she set out with the party.

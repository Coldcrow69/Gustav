Epithet: **god of the [[Iron Peaks]]**.

A **multi-headed, extra-large red dragon**. Responsible for the destruction of [[Bluefield]], motivated by the theft of its eggs. The [[Captured Half-Dragon]] taken by the party from the attacking force revealed the dragon's intent to continue assaults until its children are recovered.

> **Confirmed multi-headed.** The 2026-02-15 session recap states the party "escape[d] the attention of a multi-headed extra-large red dragon, looking for its children." This is consistent with [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s POV recollection in the long-form short story (the "enormous multi-headed nightmare swooping down on the inferno that had once been Bluefield"). The single-headed framing in earlier vault notes was an under-recording.

## At the Bluefield attack

The dragon flew overhead at the burning of [[Bluefield]]. Its warband — **kobolds and at least one half-dragon riding a [[Spark Beasts|Spark Beast]] (a flaming bear)** — was directly engaged by the party; the party captured the half-dragon but **escaped the dragon's own attention**.

Per [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s warning to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] during the [[Egg Hunt]] audience, the dragon poses a significant threat to nearby settlements and could destroy [[Stonewater]] if the stolen eggs are not located.

A **clown** in [[Fort Elm]]; one of [[Thorn]]'s contacts among "the freaks on the streets."

## What he told Thorn

During Session 6 of the [[Fish People]] arc (7/31/24), Soots told Thorn that a tavern on the east side of [[Fort Elm]] called the **[[Spit & Spindle]]** has an **entrance into the sewers in its back alleyway**. The knock pattern: **3 — wait — 3 — wait — 1**.

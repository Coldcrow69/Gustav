A **foreign man** leading a **caravan** the party intercepted on the road to [[Stonewater]] in spring 2026.

## Cover

Presents as **a usually-dried-fish merchant** from a town called **[[Autumnbreak]]**. The caravan had "picked up quite a few people" along its route — including the party itself, who joined for the final leg to [[Stonewater]].

## Suspicion

The party originally believed Basil's caravan **may be responsible for stealing the eggs from [[The God Of The Iron Peaks|the god of the Iron Peaks]]**. The party was actively investigating suspicious activity in one of the **last carts of the caravan** when [[Seren Moss|Seren]] held a conversation with him.

> **Session 30 correction.** The **feather-and-dagger emblem** [[Rhaenyra]] originally described was reassigned in Session 30 to the **occupants of a separate second cart** that traveled loosely alongside Basil's caravan on the road — **not** to Basil or his main caravan. Per [[Nastrin]]'s identification at [[Good For What Ails Ya]], the emblem is the crest of the **[[Greybraids]]** dwarf clan. Basil remains a person of interest, but the emblem-based suspicion now points at the Greybraids, not him.

## In Egg Hunt records

The cart with the **Greybraids emblem** is the vehicle the party tailed through [[Stonewater]] and lost in the second tier. Basil's main **caravan** is a separate vehicle that traveled loosely alongside; the two split on entering the city.

[[Thorn]]'s **shapeshift-into-the-cart-driver** demonstration at the [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] audience in Session 29 was of the **separate cart's driver** (a Greybraids associate), **not Basil**.

Basil's status: still a person of interest (the caravan picked up unfamiliar travelers en route, and the caravan-cart pairing is itself suspicious), but the emblem-evidence does not implicate him directly.

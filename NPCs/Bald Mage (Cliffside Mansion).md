# Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)

> Unnamed on-page. (Archivist: "Bald Human Mage" / "Ornate-Robed Mage" / "The Mage.") **At large — escaped or hiding, fate deliberately unresolved** as of [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]. **Killed [[Lyrion Thalor]]** (since revived).

A **bald human in ornate robes** among the [[Acolytes of Oghma]] at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] — clearly senior; the cultists call him "sir." By a wide margin the most dangerous person the party has met in [[Stonewater]].

## Session 33 — the killing

Emerged from a **northern room** mid-battle demanding to know what was happening, was told his people had been attacked, and joined the fight without hesitation. From a **wand drawn from his robe sleeve** he fired **bolts of energy** — three struck [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and dropped him; Lyrion died on the floor moments later. [[Mike]]'s **radiant sunbolts** hit him and he **did not go down**.

## Session 34 — the escape

Fought the entire party to a draw and left on his own terms ([[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|recap]]):

- **Turned invisible repeatedly**, re-hiding after each exchange; revealed only when he attacked or his reflexive **Shield** betrayed his position (once because [[Mike]]'s sunbolt shattered the ale barrel he stood beside).
- Put **52 damage in one turn** of wand bolts into [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] (who stayed up).
- **Counterspelled [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s Fireball**, and answered with **Cone of Cold** — freezing Mike unconscious with the full 40 damage.
- Pinned briefly by Eldrin's **Dissonant Whispers**, he **fled upstairs, cast on himself, and his footsteps stopped**. A door shut on the second floor; after the fighting, two more sets of doors opened and closed; then nothing.
- The **balcony door** was later found slightly ajar — but **no impressions in the mud, no disturbed foliage** below the 20–30 ft drop. Either he left no trace, left another way, or **never left at all**.

His **wand** — the weapon that killed Lyrion — went with him. Lyrion, freshly revived, openly wants it.

# Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)

> Unnamed on-page. (Archivist: "Bald Human Mage" / "Ornate-Robed Mage.") **Active and standing** as of the [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]] cliffhanger. **Killed [[Lyrion Thalor]].**

A **bald human in ornate robes** among the [[Acolytes of Oghma]] at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] — clearly senior; the cultists call him "sir."

Emerged from a **northern room** mid-battle demanding to know what was happening, was told his people had been attacked, and joined the fight without hesitation. From a **wand drawn from his robe sleeve** he fired **bolts of energy** — three struck [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and dropped him; Lyrion died on the floor moments later. [[Mike]]'s **radiant sunbolts** hit him and he **did not go down**.

Distinct from the **ornate-robed figure in the cellar** who attacked with fire — two separate ornate-robed casters were active in the manor.

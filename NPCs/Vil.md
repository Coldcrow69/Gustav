# Vil

> **Deceased** ([[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]). Per the maintainer, the door greeter and the escort are the **same person** — this page covers both moments.

A **young human** of the **[[Acolytes of Oghma]]** at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] — priestly-looking robes and an **amulet bearing no recognizable holy symbol**.

He was the frightened voice behind the inner door (*"please, we don't have anything to steal"*), unlocked it when [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] announced the Stonewater Guard, and presented the sect's cover story: acolytes of [[Oghma]], allowed to stay as a newer residency, a dozen-odd people coming and going. He couldn't authorize a search but led the party to the [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]] — who then ordered him to escort them downstairs and see them *"well taken care of."*

When the ambush sprang, Vil **fled** for the cellar, locking the door behind him. [[Mike]] kicked it down, caught him on the staircase, and slammed him headfirst into the steps — killing him instantly. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] found his remains on the stairs moments later.

Whether Vil was a believer, a minder, or just the doorman died with him.

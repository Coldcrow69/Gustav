# Aiker

> First appears in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]].

A **kenku** — a crow-like bird person, a startling sight in dwarven [[Stonewater]] — proprietor of **[[Aiker's Potion Explorium]]**, the pumpkin-built potion shop near the guard district. Dresses sharply: vest, white shirt, red ascot. Greets crowds with *"Hello! Hi, I'm Aiker, and welcome to Aiker's Potion Explorium."*

- Sells potions in **custom vegetable-shaped vials** — carrot (healing), onion (greater healing), pumpkin (superior healing) — plus climbing, elemental-resistance, luck, giant-strength, watchful-rest, and rarer stock (see the [[Aiker's Potion Explorium|shop page]]).
- Cannot brew resurrection-grade potions, and says so plainly.
- **Does the math on an abacus**; a fair dealer ([[Thorn]] insight-checked him on a price — telling the truth).
- Entertained [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s scroll-barter pitch against the 7,000 gp **Potion of Vitality** with professional patience — negotiation unresolved at session end.

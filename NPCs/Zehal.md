# Zehal

The **[[Loa]]** (spirit) bonded to [[Bell]] during the firbolg communion ritual at the [[Refugee Camp]] outside [[Bluefield]]. The ritual was performed by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Bell]], [[Kira]], and [[Mike]] in the woods that night; on its completion **a spirit spoke [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s name** and Bell acquired her bond with Zehal.

## Appearance

A **wolf-or-hound spirit** of unmistakably supernatural make. Distinctive features:

- **Long, shaggy black mane** flowing wildly around the head and neck.
- **Wooden / bone-like spikes** (read as either antlers, thorns, or ritual pikes) radiating outward from the crown and shoulders.
- **Golden glowing glyphs** — labyrinth and spiral motifs — etched / branded into the face where eyes would normally sit, and continuing in tribal patterns down the chest and forelimbs.
- **Visible eyes are absent or hidden**; the glyph-mask reads as both ornamentation and replacement.
- **Long muzzle** opening into a wide grin of **sharp white teeth**, with a **bright red tongue / inner mouth** glowing as if lit from within.
- **Wisps of red mist / smoke** trailing from the body, suggesting either spirit-essence bleed-off or active sympathetic projection.

The overall aesthetic is **dark, ritualistic, and feral** — not a tame patron. Zehal reads as a spirit that grants power on its own terms.

## What's known

- The bond is real and persistent — referenced in subsequent sessions as "the loa that's attached to Belle."
- The Loa's specific **powers**, **personality**, and **agenda** are not yet logged.
- Possible (unresolved) connection to Bell's **rage-transformation secret** — when she rages she takes on a more bestial appearance (sharp teeth, shadowed eyes, growing tail). The visual overlap between Zehal's appearance and Bell's rage form is striking and worth tracking.

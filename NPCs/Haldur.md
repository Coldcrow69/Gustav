# Haldur

> Spelling per the maintainer (given at the table: "H-A-L-D-U-R"); transcript variants "Howther," "Halder," "Haldor." First appears in [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]]. Not to be confused with any Valheim character — the table checked.

**Owner and barkeep of the [[Sour Jackal]]** in [[Stonewater]]'s Clock Tower District — and the party's landlord for the ten nights they've paid.

An **elf unlike any the party has seen**: a much **darker complexion** than the high elves, with **natural markings — not tattoos — flowing in lines across his exposed arms and chest**. [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s history check placed him: the **elven tribes of the desert north of the wilds, the [[Sunscarred Expanse]]** — a people Fort Elm knows only through traders' stories (she wondered whether desert traders bound for the sea ever passed through Fort Elm; apparently not on her watch). The DM showed a reference image of the northern elves for the table.

- Keeps the party's tab open; takes names for each room; warns that **replacement keys are a gold apiece**.
- Asked by [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] to **source the best mead he knows of** — *"I will ask around."*

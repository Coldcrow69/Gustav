# Finn

**[[Rhaenyra]]'s companion animal.** A fox-like creature that accompanies the party but **does not fight**.

`![[Finn.webp]]`

## Role

- **Non-combatant.** Travels with the party; does not engage in encounters.
- Bonded to [[Rhaenyra]]; consistent with her Druid class and Sylvan-speaking background.

## Appearance

A medium quadrupedal creature with fox-like build and clearly **fey or otherwise enchanted** features:

- **Vibrant red-orange fur** in long, layered tufts along the back, shoulders, and hindquarters; lighter cream underside.
- **Antlers** branching from the crown — branched and bony like a stag's, not horns.
- **Ribbed white dorsal spines / bone plates** running along the neck and back.
- **Tribal red flame-pattern markings** across the face and flanks.
- **Slender elongated muzzle** with sharp teeth; eyes calm.
- Typically observed at rest among **bluebell wildflowers and ferns** — calm, non-threatening posture.

The combination of fur, antlers, and pigment pattern is inconsistent with any ordinary Material-Plane animal — reads as a **fey beast** or a **[[Spark Beasts|spark-touched]]** creature native to [[The Wilds]] or its enchanted groves.

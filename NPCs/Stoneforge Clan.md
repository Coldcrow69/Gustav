# Stoneforge Clan

> Distinct from the **[[Greybraids]]** and from the city of [[Stonewater]] itself (which chroniclers sometimes mis-recorded as "Stoneforge" / "Ironforge"). The Stoneforge Clan is **[[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s** dwarf clan.

A dwarf clan of [[Stonewater]] — the clan of **[[Baldrin Stoneforge]]**, foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]]. Their **insignia** marks mine pickaxes, including the **murder weapon** that killed the [[Murdered Mine Survivor (Stonewater Alley)|mine-survivor victim]] in [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]] — a pickaxe [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] recognized from around Baldrin's house.

## Stoneforge Guard

A clan-affiliated armed force. A sector of the **Stoneforge Guard** had **cornered and killed** [[Victor Stonehammer]] — the original murder suspect — before he returned as a **revenant**. His body reportedly decomposed rapidly after he went down, and the corpse went missing.

## Presence in the city guard

**Maintainer-confirmed ([[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]):** the Stoneforge Clan has a **large presence throughout [[Stonewater]]'s guard and military** — clan membership and city-guard service overlap freely. The first named example is **[[Dura]]**, the fifteen-year guard veteran (from a multi-generation military family) whom [[Captain Grazug]] assigned as the party's minder: she serves the city guard **in Stoneforge colors**. The [[Ironheart Mines|mine]] tunnels are also under the clan's control — the "Stoneforge estate tries to keep consistent entry and exit under control," per the captain.

## The estate

The **Stoneforge estate** sits on [[Stonewater]]'s highest tier along the northern climb to the cliff-top lake, with a **rock garden** for a front lawn. Household staff on-page: the headmaid **[[Gwynn (Stoneforge Headmaid)|Gwynn]]**, who issues and updates the party's [[Mine Pass|mine writs]] ([[Through the Bloodied Rubble - Session 37 - 2026-10-25|Session 37]]). [[Dura]] describes her own position as working for [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] "through his estate" without reporting to him directly.

## Note on the alley guards

The guards who secured the Session 31 alley scene were a **different** force, in colors the party did **not** recognize (not Stoneforge, not [[Greybraids]]) — since identified as the **Stonewater Guard**, the city force under [[Captain Grazug]], based at the **third-tier Barracks**. They reopened the murder investigation and later deputized half the party.

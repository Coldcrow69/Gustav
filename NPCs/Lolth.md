# Lolth

**The Spider Queen.** The primary drow goddess in [[Azadin]] (as in standard pantheons). The most powerful and widely worshiped drow deity, dominating the drow race's lives and urging them into heavy infighting.

## Alignment

Chaotic Evil.

## Dogma

The main principle of the church is to **punish all who would disobey** the word of Lolth and her faith.

Under Lolth's dogma, **female drow are recognized as more valuable than males**, who are considered worthless — giving female drow absolute power over males.

Priestesses honor her by **conducting rituals, leading sacrifices, and spreading the faith**. Sacrifices of surface elves are conducted regularly as direct insults to elven gods.

## Presence in vault

- **[[Riknozz]]'s peaceful Drow sect does NOT worship the Spider Queen** — this is the defining doctrinal break that distinguishes them from the violent Drow factions. The [[Drow Elder]] told the party her people had seen war and chosen peace; their refuge in the ruined Temple of [[Tyr]] reflects a justice-aligned alternative.
- The violent Drow sect that **tortured and murdered [[Endra Thalor]]** is unidentified but is consistent with conventional Lolth-worshipping drow.

# Haceran Leadbreaker

> Formerly filed as **Elderly Dwarf Cleric (Stonewater)** — she gave her name in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]. (Archivist variants: "Elderly Cleric," "Elderly Dwarf Woman In Clerical Robes," "Haceran Leadbreaker Of The Temple Of Sylvanus" — the "Sylvanus/Sylvanas" temple attribution is the recurring archivist mishearing; her temple is the [[Temple of Selûne (Stonewater)|Temple of Selûne]] per the standing ruling.)

**Haceran Leadbreaker**, an **elderly dwarf woman** — a cleric and apparent attendant of the **[[Temple of Selûne (Stonewater)|Temple of Selûne]]** in [[Stonewater]], the green-domed temple that also serves as the city library. Very old and wrinkled, with a **full beard graying along the edges**, in clerical robes.

## Session 31

[[Mike]] ran to the temple seeking a cleric who could **Banish** the [[Victor Stonehammer]] revenant. She didn't recognize his garbled request at first ("brandish" / "vanish"), but on understanding it was an undead matter, confirmed she **could not cast Banishment that day**. She consulted a **heavy tome of the city's registered clerics** for anyone nearby who could, found no immediate option, and chose to come see the creature herself. [[Mike]] carried her back to the [[Stonewater Sewers]] (in giant-form). On arrival she reaffirmed she lacked Banishment for the day, and asked whether anyone present could otherwise dispel the creature.

## Session 32

Present for the opening of the [[Chained Stone Coffin]] and the discovery of the liquefied remains, and gave the party her name. She explained that much of her temple's clergy **specialize in tending to the living**, and that unless the creature were captured again there was **little more the temple could do** — though [[Seren Moss|Seren]] noted Haceran at least **knows the specific banishment rite** required to end the revenant. [[Mike]] offered her a lift and **carried her back out of the sewers**.

**[[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s former lover.** They were together **over a decade**.

> Source: *Seren (Crypt Keepers)* long-form short story.

The relationship ended painfully when Zia gave Seren an **ultimatum to put aside her responsibilities** at the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]] — Seren refused, and Zia left. The breakup hurt deeply enough that Seren did not seek out another romantic relationship for years afterward, until [[Lyrion Thalor]].

Whereabouts and details otherwise unrecorded.

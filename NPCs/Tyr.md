# Tyr

**God of Law and Justice.** Titles include **The Even-Handed**, the **Maimed God** (for his missing right hand, lost in an act of bravery and self-sacrifice), and the **Blind Overlord** (the wounded eyes symbolizing "blind justice" and the cost of a lawful life).

## Alignment

Lawful Good.

## Symbol

**Balanced scales upon a warhammer** — justice upheld with armed vigilance and benevolent might.

## Dogma

> *"Reveal the truth, punish the guilty, right the wrong, and be always true and just in your actions."*

The contrast between right and wrong is evident; the justice his followers deliver is no laughing matter. Domains: **Law** and **Justice**.

## Presence in vault

- **[[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)]]** — a ruined temple of Tyr in the cave system beneath [[Fort Elm]], used as a temporary camp by [[Riknozz]]'s peaceful Drow sect during the [[Fish People]] arc.
- **[[Necklace of Tyr]]** — a holy item [[Riknozz]] recovered from a decomposed Drow corpse in one of the slime-pool depths. Riknozz's peaceful sect's choice of a Tyr temple as refuge fits their justice-aligned conduct.

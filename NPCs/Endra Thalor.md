# Endra Thalor

**Deceased.** Mother of [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Eldrin Thalor]].

## Heritage

**Half-drow, half-high-elf.** **Light blue skinned** — passed her skin tone to [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] (who inherited her side); her other son [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] took his complexion from their fair-skinned high-elf father.

## Fate

**Tortured and murdered by Drow** — by one of the more violent sects, distinct from [[Riknozz]]'s peaceful sect (per the [[Drow Elder]]'s explanation). The horror is compounded by the fact that her killers shared her heritage — kin killing kin.

[[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] told her story to the [[Drow Elder]] when asked if they had lost someone to the Drow.

## In-game appearance

The [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]] **conjured an image of her, just as Lyrion remembered her**, in an attempt to deceive him during his solo investigation of the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]]. Lyrion answered with a fireball.

## Family

- Husband: [[Darion Thalor]] — was abusive to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] in their family cellar (see his page).
- Sons: [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Eldrin Thalor]].

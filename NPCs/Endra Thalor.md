# Endra Thalor

**Deceased.** Mother of [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Eldrin Thalor]].

## Heritage

**Half-drow, half-high-elf.** **Light blue skinned** — passed her skin tone to [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] (who inherited her side); her other son [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] took his complexion from their fair-skinned high-elf father.

## Fate

**Tortured and murdered by Drow** — by one of the more violent sects, distinct from [[Riknozz]]'s peaceful sect (per the [[Drow Elder]]'s explanation). The horror is compounded by the fact that her killers shared her heritage — kin killing kin.

[[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] told her story to the [[Drow Elder]] when asked if they had lost someone to the Drow.

## In-game appearance

The [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]] **conjured an image of her, just as Lyrion remembered her**, in an attempt to deceive him during his solo investigation of the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]]. Lyrion answered with a fireball.

In [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]], during the minute [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] lay dead at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]], he glimpsed an afterlife — rolling yellow-grass hills under a clear sky, warmth like a held breath — and in the distance **a woman he knew: Endra**. With [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]]'s and [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s voices calling faintly behind him, he told the distant figure *"I'll be back one day… and we'll all be together again"* — and turned around.

The morning after his revival ([[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]), Lyrion told [[Seren Moss|Seren]] what the vision had given him: he saw his mother **"clear as day"** — her face sharp for the first time in **decades of failed remembering**. *"You were there to call me back. Lest I may have stayed."* Seren answered with [[Kelemvor]]'s teaching: the Judge places the dead fairly, and a revival spell that works is the gods' will.

He told [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] the next day, chest-deep in a mud bath ([[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]]): *"I saw her. When I was on the other side. She was there."* Eldrin: *"Is she okay?"* — *"I don't know, Eldrin. She's dead, so I don't know if 'okay' is a term relative to that state of being."* Their **father was not there** — *"thankfully, or unfortunately, not"* — which Eldrin read two ways: either [[Darion Thalor|Darion]] isn't dead, or the afterlife is somewhere worth going.

## Remembered

- **The storm and the mud** (Session 36): after a great storm, Endra went out to harvest something that only bloomed after rain (mushrooms, per Eldrin). Lyrion — not yet able to Prestidigitate — tripped Eldrin into the mud with a tiny Mage Hand, pushed their father in after him, and jumped in himself. Endra was **furious**, and blamed Eldrin, "the problem child," while Lyrion let her. *"She said if she had to clean us, the mushrooms were going to get soggy."*
- She made the boys **wear coats** in winter; they took them off the moment they left the house and competed to go longest without (Session 37).
- She was **angry at Darion** over his winter-river "real man" plunges — once Eldrin stayed in thirty-six minutes and turned so blue his toes stopped working (Session 36).
- *"Mom liked everyone"* (Lyrion, Session 37) — the basis on which he assured Eldrin she would have liked [[Rhaenyra]].

- Husband: [[Darion Thalor]] — was abusive to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] in their family cellar (see his page).
- Sons: [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Eldrin Thalor]].

# Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)

> Unnamed on-page. (Archivist: "Elder," recorded as human male; the transcript is less certain.) **DEAD** — DM-confirmed in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]: the Elder was the sword-cultist fighting [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] in the kitchen, the man who **stabbed the fallen Lyrion** — and whom **[[Bell]] cleaved in two** (neck to armpit) moments later in [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]. The Session 34 "at large" status is retired; his Session 33 flight ended back in the fight, not out of the manor.

Leader of the **[[Acolytes of Oghma]]** at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] ([[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]).

## The audience

Received the party's investigation claim; listened to [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s account of the murders and the "unwholesome presence" she sensed below; flatly **denied** any aberration or [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]] — *"we are a sect of healers"* — and named **[[Kassadren]]** as the one who gave them legal right to the house. Demanded proof of authority; read [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s genuine [[Writ of Deputization]] and forged [[Broader Warrant]] **for a long moment** (whether he spotted the forgery is unknown), handed them back — and ordered **[[Vil]]** to take the group downstairs and see them *"well taken care of."* The ambush began the moment [[Bell]] left his line of sight.

## The fight, and the death

Tried to withdraw immediately. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] Misty-Stepped onto a table and demanded he stand his people down; the Elder ran instead. Lyrion's upcast **Witch Bolt tore away part of his shoulder**; a **Counterspell** cut off his answer mid-cast; a second Witch Bolt followed him through the door ([[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]).

He did not flee the manor. He resurfaced in the kitchen fight — the sword-cultist dueling Lyrion — and when the [[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|mage]]'s wand bolts dropped Lyrion, the Elder **drove his sword into the dying wizard** with the words [[Bell]] reported to [[Captain Grazug]]: ***"You will be forgotten."*** Bell answered the killing blow in kind — her greataxe **cleaved him in two** ([[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]; identity confirmed at the Session 35 table: *"so that one is dead"*).

The parting irony stands: the man whose creed was remembrance is on this page, unnamed.

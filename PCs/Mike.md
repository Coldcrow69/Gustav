## The "wizard" misconception

Mike is a **Monk** whose ki abilities are martial-arts magic in the conventional D&D sense (not arcane). Because his INT is low, **Mike himself believes his ki abilities are wizardry** — he sincerely calls himself a wizard, talks about "wizard duels," and treats the fire he can punch into existence as spellcasting. ([[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] knows perfectly well Mike is not a wizard.) Hence Lyrion's lament at [[Good For What Ails Ya]] in [[Egg Hunt]] Session 29 — *"I lost the best wizard battle… despite Mike not being a wizard"* — is doubly ironic: the battle was real ([[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|the fairy-tree fight]]) but the "wizardry" was Mike's monk ki.

### Mike as Lyrion's "apprentice"

Despite knowing the truth, **[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] agreed to take Mike on as his apprentice** out of his own **vain aspirations to be a famous wizard** (his vanity insisting that a famous wizard ought to have an apprentice). The arrangement plays into Mike's self-image and Lyrion's self-image at the same time.

## Egg Hunt — Session 30 (Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas, 2026-05-17)

Mike woke surprised to find his bruises faded, followed his hunger to the common room, slapped his fists on the bar, and ordered **three of the daily special** and a breakfast mead from [[Nastrin]]. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] whipped around in shock — *"Wake! You're alive!"* — and called him over to the booth.

### "Mike the Sorcerer"

In conversation with [[Thorn]] (who introduced herself and asked if he had ever considered show business), Mike said he usually only thought about magic — and added that he **used to be a wizard**. When [[Rhaenyra]] pressed, Mike said [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] had told him he was no longer his apprentice during the road trip. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] then retold the [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|fairy-grove encounter]] in full; Mike confirmed he had eventually realized he could not win the fight, fled, and **carried Lyrion away while Lyrion was still a sheep**.

Mike concluded the whole incident had clarified something for him: he was **not a wizard at all — he was a Sorcerer**. *"Mike the Sorcerer."* (Mechanically still a Monk; the relabeling is in-character self-identification only. The Monk → "wizard" misconception has now become a Monk → "sorcerer" misconception. The fire he punches into existence remains martial-arts ki, not arcane magic. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] continues to play along.)

When Lyrion apologized for nearly getting him killed, Mike said there was no need — *"there wouldn't have been much for a wizard to teach a sorcerer anyway."*

### The bark sample

Mike had gone straight to bed the night before without bathing. The next morning Lyrion realized this and searched Mike's clothes for plant residue; he found a **[[Carnivorous Plant Bark Sample|small piece of bark]]** caught in the tuft of Mike's belt. Sample now in Lyrion's possession for analysis.

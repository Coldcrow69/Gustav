## Backstory

> Source: *Seren (OG Crypt Keepers)* — short story (treated as factual canon).

**Adopted.** Seren "appeared on the steps of the shrine" of [[Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]] as a baby. Adopted and raised by **[[Gwyn Moss]]** (the family surname Seren now carries). Gwyn called her "the most wonderful, unexpected gift that life has ever sent me."

**Family:**

- **[[Gwyn Moss]]** — adoptive mother. Shrinekeeper of [[Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]]. *Kidnapped and rescued during the [[Fish People]] arc; alive and back at the shrine.*
- **[[Uncle Bren|Uncle Brenan ("Bren")]]** — Gwyn's brother. Healer / medic; packed Seren's healer's kit (extra bandages, left-side pocket).
- **[[The Aunties]]** — Gwyn's three sisters:
  - **[[Ilsa|Aunt Ilsa]]** — cook, also a Sister of [[Kelemvor]]
  - **[[Aunt Keva]]** — sparring trainer (notes Seren doesn't guard her left side)
  - **[[Aunt Anya]]** — spiritual mentor

**Upbringing.** Acolytes of [[Kelemvor]] led a strict, austere life from the moment they came to live in His shrine. As far back as Seren can remember, she spent the majority of her time learning the difficult tasks of a cleric:

- Praying
- Cleaning the chapel from top to bottom
- Tending the grounds
- Preparing bodies for burial
- Consoling the bereaved
- Honing battle skills to frighten away graverobbers

She aspired to be **shrine keeper of [[Kelemvor]] one day** after Gwyn.

**Self-assessment (post Session 1 of Fish People).** Before [[Victor Barun]]'s disastrous party, she would not have described herself as sheltered — she was, in her own words, "smug with self-righteousness," thinking she'd known everything worth knowing. The events of the underground rescue showed her how little of the wider world she had seen. The strange algae, the **gel-encased screaming skeleton**, and the death-white skin of the doppelganger jolt her out of sleep. The tight tunnel beneath [[Victor Barun]]'s wine cellar still haunts her — "very similar to being buried alive."

Until that point the most frightening thing she had faced was "a desperate duo of men hoping to find jewels in a casket." Compared to [[Teron|Teran]] and [[Bell]] — who seemed **unshaken** by the underground encounter — she felt "green and untested."

**The decision to leave.** After Session 1 of Fish People, [[Victor Barun]] offered Seren ongoing employment to investigate the **missing farmers** of [[Fort Elm]]. She hesitated — she loved [[Kelemvor]], Gwyn, Uncle Bren, the Aunties, and Fort Elm itself, and did not want to leave. Gwyn answered with characteristic calm: *"You're still so young, and you've seen so little of the wider world. If you wish to be the shrine keeper of Kelemvor one day, there is plenty of time to take up the mantle. Go, see the world, learn as much as you can. Help others, in more ways than you could if you were to stay here all your days."* — *"I ask you to consider that perhaps you were meant for greater things than to stay in this one little chapel for your whole life."*

Seren accepted. The short story ends with her standing up to pack.

**The morning of departure for Bluefield Burning.** Seren woke late (uneasy dreams; **[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] already gone from her bed**), missed helping [[Ilsa|Aunt Ilsa]] with the cooking, and faced a goodbye spread by the acolytes and her family. Each adult Aunt gave a parting gift: a wrapped bundle of treats from Ilsa, a sparring critique and discipline reminder from [[Aunt Keva|Keva]], a doctrinal blessing from [[Aunt Anya|Anya]], a packed healer's kit from [[Uncle Bren|Brenan]]. [[Gwyn Moss|Gwyn]] embraced her one last time: *"Go well, my daughter. I know you'll make me proud no matter what happens. I love you always."* Seren cried only after she turned to walk to the docks.

> A small detail recorded by the chronicler: Seren had to **bend down** to hug Gwyn (consistent with their height difference — Seren is 5'9", Gwyn is "a small, gray-brown sparrow of a person").

> She mused privately that **"so many of [the acolytes] will never leave home"** — and arrived at her own answer for why Gwyn pushed her to travel: not just a test of mettle, but to gain experience and perspective that would let her **make wiser decisions** for the shrine once she returned to take Gwyn's place.

## Past relationships

**[[Zia]]** — Seren's first love. They were together **over a decade** before Zia left, after giving Seren an ultimatum to abandon her shrine responsibilities; Seren refused. The breakup hurt deeply enough that she did not seek out another relationship until [[Lyrion Thalor]].

## Current relationship — [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]

The hand-holding moment after the [[The Undead Aboleth|aboleth]] battle in the [[Fish People]] arc was the seed; the relationship had progressed by the start of [[Bluefield Burning]]. By the morning of the Sail la Vie departure they were **sleeping together**. They have:

- Steered the [[Sail la Vie]] together at the helm
- Walked together in the [[Fort Elm Park]] looking at the stars; **first kiss** there
- Spent down-time in Fort Elm during a stretch when [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] was away for weeks — Seren **taught Lyrion holy magic**, which he was eager to learn and respectful of, succeeding at his first attempt

The night after the **dragon attack on [[Bluefield]]**, while camped on the deck of the [[Sail la Vie]] at the [[Refugee Camp]], Seren found a **letter Lyrion had slipped into her bedroll** — a frank love confession that left her flustered, joyful, and afraid. She wrestled all night with her fears: that Lyrion's thirst for knowledge could turn dark (he had bemoaned the necessity of killing the aboleth, citing what they might have learned from talking to it); that their goals are incompatible (his wanderlust vs her plan to take Gwyn's place at the shrine); that being abandoned again the way [[Zia]] had abandoned her would destroy her. She concluded the night with: *"I love him, too."*

## Fish People arc

In [[Fort Elm]] early in the campaign, Seren spent time in the shrines of [[Kelemvor]] doing charity work. She was among the party (with [[Bell]] and [[Teron]]) who descended into the tunnel beneath [[Victor Barun]]'s wine cellar — the rescue that found the real Victor alive — and witnessed the algae, the gel-encased screaming skeleton, and the doppelganger's death-white skin.

During the river combat, the kuotoa carried off hostages — **including [[Gwyn Moss|her mother Gwyn]], the Shrinekeeper of [[Kelemvor]]**. Later Seren and [[Teron]] went into the cave to investigate; combat began. Seren was dragged unconscious; on the surface she was lifted by [[Thorn]] and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and tended by a medic.

While unconscious Seren had a vision: creatures taking people down to an underground temple, being called for **flesh sacrifices**.

She possesses one of the recovered [[Facemask Device|facemask devices]] taken from a saved hostage.

### Council tension and the deeper caves

Efforts to persuade the **Council of [[Fort Elm]]** to send an army after the captives were unsuccessful — producing **brief tension between [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] and Seren**. The party retreated to the [[Shrine of Kelemvor|Temple of Kelemvor]] for the night.

Using **Sending**, she contacted Gwyn underground and confirmed the prisoners were alive — first hard proof rescue was still possible.

### The riddle door

During the [[Riddle Door|riddle-door encounter]], Seren held the line with **Spirit Guardians** while [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Thorn]], and [[Teron]] worked the gem puzzle; [[Riknozz]] and [[Bell]] pushed skeletons into her spell range.

### Reunion with her mother

Inside the [[The Ancient Ruined City|ancient ruined city]], the party found the building where the fish monsters kept their captives. **Seren awoke and healed Gwyn**, who in turn helped heal the other survivors. Seren then calmly informed the Fort Elm townspeople that the party was there to take them home.

### Aftermath / final battle

Escorted the rescued townsfolk back to Fort Elm with [[Bell]] and [[Teron]], reported to [[Lilith]], then helped Gwyn tend the wounded. In the final battle, Teron — momentarily charmed by the aboleth — **stabbed her with a spear**; she shouted him back to himself with a spell that broke the charm. The aboleth nearly slew her later; [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] revived her. When [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] tried to embrace her after the revival, **she unceremoniously threw up on him**.

### Quiet moment after

Walking back to Fort Elm, **she took Lyrion's hand shyly and suggested they give the hug a second try later**.

## Egg Hunt — Session 29 (Search for Dragon Eggs, 2026-05-10)

Upon arriving in [[Stonewater]] (recorded as Ironforge in chronicler notes), Seren stated she would keep watch for a cart the party had previously seen, in case it crossed their path.

She used **Sending** to contact [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], confirming his and [[Mike]]'s arrival in the city and arranging to meet at the tavern [[Good For What Ails Ya]] in the lower district after the audience.

At [[Baldrin Stoneforge]]'s residence, she introduced the party (saluting with a fist over her heart), explaining they had been sent by [[Victor Barun]] and detailing the destruction of [[Bluefield]] by the red dragon [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]]. She reported that the party had captured a half-dragon from the attacking force ([[Captured Half-Dragon]]) who revealed the dragon's eggs had been stolen, that local firbolgs had aided Bluefield's refugees, and that suspicious human adventurers had passed through before the attack. She requested aid locating the stolen eggs to prevent further assaults, warning that the dragons would not cease attacks until their children were recovered.

She participated in questioning Baldrin about the mine disappearances and murders — manner of death, weapon used, status of the accused man's corpse and weapon — and suggested a possible magical cause such as a curse or compulsion. She also reported encountering a caravan with unfamiliar travelers and expressed regret for losing track of it in the city, asking for help finding it.

At the tavern after the meeting, she sat beside [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and tried to coax mild food into him to absorb the alcohol.

## Egg Hunt — Session 30 (Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas, 2026-05-17)

After Lyrion fell asleep the night before, Seren crept into [[Mike]]'s room, found him still unconscious from the road, **healed him quietly**, left a cold compress on his forehead, and slipped out without waking him. Mike was unaware of the visit when he woke the next morning.

At breakfast, Seren came in carrying pastries from elsewhere. She summarized the [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] audience and the suspicious-cart tail for [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Mike]]. She proposed using a **locator spell** for the missing dragon eggs and pairing it with the **feather-and-dagger emblem** they had seen on the cart. She approached [[Nastrin]], opened her notebook, sketched the emblem, and Nastrin identified it as the **[[Greybraids]]** — Seren thanked him, left a silver, and brought the lead back to the table.

When the group debated splitting up, Seren said she did not like the idea but admitted that was emotion rather than logic, and said she should accompany whichever group went after the Greybraids since she was the one who could attempt the locator spell.

### Disguise request

Turned to [[Thorn]] and asked for a disguise — *"Do you still have your disguise kit? Do you think you could make me look different?"* — Thorn took her by the hand and pulled her upstairs to alter her hair and makeup, moving her **off her usual blonde**.

**The finished disguise**: a **blue-skinned elf**, her coloring recalling [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s. (It was still half-finished — coloring only — when she was called to the Session 31 alley scene; she wore the full disguise **throughout [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]**. The archivist misread this as Seren "transitioning to a human cleric" — wrong on both counts; it is Thorn's disguise work, and the party gave no cover names because no one asked for any.)

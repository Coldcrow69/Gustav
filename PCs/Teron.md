Aliases: **Teran**, **Teren** (chronicler spelling variants).

Player: **Eve** *(original Fish People-era player; subsequently quit the campaign)*.

> **Status: Written out.** Teron has been swept under the rug as the campaign moved past the [[Fish People]] arc. The party does not discuss him; he is **not** referenced or invoked in [[Bluefield Burning]] or [[Egg Hunt]] sessions. This page exists as historical record only.

## Character Sheet

- Race: **Emerald Dragonborn**
- Class: **Paladin** *(patron deity [[Mystra]])*
- Demeanor: "friendly dragonborn paladin searching for a forgotten past" (DM narrative)

## Backstory (historical)

**Amnesia.** Teron could not remember much of his past. Moments he *did* remember slipped away as soon as he tried to examine them. He carried a **map of his hometown** he could not place; he confided this to [[Bell]] during sparring on the night of [[Victor Barun]]'s retirement party. Bell did not recognize the area. **The map disappeared with Teron** when he left the campaign and is **no longer plot-relevant**.

**Patron**: [[Mystra]] guided him at the altar in the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]] after the aboleth battle.

## Fish People arc (historical)

In [[Fort Elm]] early in the campaign, Teron trained on the east side of the city. He was one of the three party members ([[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Bell]], and Teron) to descend through the tight tunnel beneath [[Victor Barun]]'s wine cellar to recover the real Victor. Bell and Teron seemed unshaken by the underground encounter; Seren was deeply shaken.

During the second cave excursion he and [[Seren Moss|Seren]] entered the cave; combat began. Teron was taken by a large squid-like creature and dragged unconscious into a cavern. He awoke and fought off creatures "rambo style," returning to the surface. He carried one of the recovered [[Facemask Device|facemask devices]].

### Class abilities used

- **Divine Sense** — "something is wrong below the earth" at the gulch descent.
- **Telepathy** — reached out to [[Bell]] inside the bioluminescent-algae cavern.
- **Dragon-fear breath weapon** — DC 13 Wis save, used during the aboleth fight when he was momentarily charmed.

### [[The Drow prisoner incident]]

When [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] killed a Drow prisoner on sight, Teron **shield-bashed Lyrion**, knocking him off his feet; [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] retaliated by shooting Teron with an arrow. Teron declared his intent to report the murder. Lyrion later offered **trial by combat** to atone. The [[Drow Elder]] ultimately accepted Lyrion's blood debt as paid in exchange for aboleth-slaying.

### Final battle and aftermath

Teron taunted the [[The Undead Aboleth|aboleth]] at the start of the final fight; it charmed him and he **stabbed [[Seren Moss|Seren]] with a spear** before [[Seren Moss|Seren]] broke the charm with a spell. Nearly slain; revived by Eldrin. **Guided by [[Mystra]]**, he took the altar offerings: spell scrolls, the mahogany box (= [[Sail la Vie]]), and a 5,400 gp coin sack.

## Departure

In the ~9-month gap between the close of [[Fish People]] (12/19/24) and the start of [[Bluefield Burning]] (9/24/25), **Eve quit the campaign**. Teron was written out off-screen; the party does not bring him up. The mahogany box he had recovered stayed with the party as [[Sail la Vie]].

## Fish People arc

Bell was camping south of [[Fort Elm]] during the events leading into the retirement party.

She was one of the three party members ([[Seren Moss|Seren]], Bell, and [[Teron|Teran]]) to descend through the **tight tunnel beneath [[Victor Barun]]'s wine cellar** to recover the real Victor. Per Seren's recollection, Bell seemed **unshaken** by the underground encounter.

**Sparring with [[Teron]]** the same evening — during which Teron revealed his amnesia and showed her his **map of his hometown**. Bell was unable to recognize the area.

**Camp raided.** When the party headed into the forest to find alternate cave entrances, Bell led them to her camp and discovered her **hut ransacked** — food gone, **wet earth with unusual webbed prints** (not boot, not paw). She tracked the prints northwest, parallel to the town wall (lost at 15-20 ft from camp), then deeper into the forest where she found an **old oak tree on a small hill with a gulch** (see [[The Gulch]]) ~500-600 ft from her camp. **One of her stolen hunting knives sat at the gulch edge.** Faint water-drops came from deeper in. The gulch became the party's alternate entry to the cave system.

Subsequently confirmed: **all her weapons and hunting traps had been stolen** from the hut. She took **pliers** to inventory.

During the deeper descent Bell tried **Beast Speech** on the cavern slime/foam — it did not seem to be paying attention to her.

**Riddle door — undead trigger.** As she tried to disarm one of the skeletons in the fountain chamber, **Bell accidentally triggered the room's defenses**, causing all the undead to stand. The party had to fight through; once Lyrion solved the [[Riddle Door|gem puzzle]], the animating magic withered.

**Charmed in final battle.** During the fight against the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]], **Bell spent much of the battle charmed by the enemy.**

**Loot dive.** After the aboleth's death, **Bell dove into the pit** to see if anything was worth scavenging — and retrieved a [[Spell Scroll Case (Aboleth Pit)|spell scroll case]] Lyrion judged to be very valuable and powerful.

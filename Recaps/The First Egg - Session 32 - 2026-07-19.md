## Recap

The session opened where Session 31 cut: the [[Chained Stone Coffin]] unchained and opened, and inside — no body. Whatever had bound the revenant's stolen flesh together had let go entirely, leaving a shifting, liquefied mass of rot. The party stood over a coffin of moving goo, uncertain whether the thing inside was destroyed, dormant, or departing.

They treated it as a crime scene. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] checked the adjacent den room — the [[Severed Dwarven Torso|gnawed torso]] was still there, unmoving — and handed [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] an empty vial; Eldrin collected a **[[Vial of Decomposed Black Goo|sample of the decomposed black goo]]** from the coffin. [[Rhaenyra]], peering back into the revenant's den (the archivist's "small crypt"), noted its contents amounted to basic cleaning and maintenance gear — including **unmarked pickaxes**, none bearing the [[Stoneforge Clan]] insignia of the murder weapon. She considered resealing the coffin, but had no shaping magic left. The working understanding hardened: the revenant is a **possessing spirit**; abandoning a ruined host is not death. [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s read — relayed as a warning to the city guard — was that it could **re-form in another corpse within roughly half a day** in a city this size, and that no one could Banish it until the next day.

[[Haceran Leadbreaker]] — the [[Temple of Selûne (Stonewater)|Temple of Selûne]]'s elderly dwarf cleric, now giving her name — could offer little more: much of her temple's clergy specialize in tending the living, and unless the creature were captured again there was not much the temple could do. Seren noted Haceran at least knew the specific banishment rite needed. [[Mike]] offered the old cleric a lift out of the sewers and carried her up.

Back above ground, the party debated next moves. Lyrion argued the urgent task was to **identify the cave-in survivors** — the revenant's remaining targets — and floated, with limited enthusiasm from the group, using survivors as bait. [[Bell]] pushed to notify the guards, the city's churches, and the **gravekeepers**, since every corpse in the city was now a potential host. The consensus plan: put the investigation on **official footing** with the city guard (Lyrion's proposal — an official writ before anything else), and split — one group to [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] and the [[Ironheart Mines]], the other to the guard. Lyrion told Eldrin that if things went wrong, **explosions would be the signal**; Eldrin: *"I'll be there."* The two groups agreed on a meeting time and parted.

### The mine group — Eldrin, Rhaenyra, Bell

At [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s manor the three were admitted, offered refreshments — Eldrin accepted, and sampled the alcohol with visible suspicion — and issued **writs authorizing entry to the [[Ironheart Mines]]** (the long-promised [[Mine Pass|mine pass]], finally in hand). An escort walked them through the mine checkpoints and down into the workings, leaving them with Baldrin himself at the **collapsed tunnel**.

Baldrin was franker underground than he had been in his parlor. The collapsed shaft had been due for nothing but **general preventative maintenance** — by his account it simply should not have come down. The **surviving miners' stories don't match one another**, and the mismatches have led him to suspect **foul play** in the cave-in. Clearing the blockage by normal methods will take time, though he allowed that the party's magic might open a smaller way through sooner. Pressed by Bell, he **agreed to provide complete lists of the dead and the surviving miners**. Eldrin — working the sweet-talk angle the group had assigned him — asked whether the name **[[Victor Stonehammer]]** meant anything to Baldrin; it did not immediately stand out to him. He also noted there are a **few other mine entrances** he knows of (outside the city — [[Stonewater]] itself has only the one, per the maintainer).

Rhaenyra studied the collapse and summoned a **small spider**, proposing to send it through the cracks to scout the far side, and asked whether the caverns beyond were easy to get lost in. The session ended before the scout went through — the collapse's far side is still unseen.

### The guard group — Lyrion, Seren, Mike, Thorn

At the third-tier Barracks (the archivist's "Guardhouse"), the four met the **[[Guard Captain (Stonewater)|guard captain]]** and offered their help with what was now openly a supernatural murder investigation. Seren laid out the revenant, the half-day re-forming window, and the next-day banishment timeline; the captain **thanked them for the warning**, said plainly that his first loyalty was **to the people of the city**, and — after fifteen or twenty minutes of paperwork — produced documentation **deputizing the four of them** for the investigation. He agreed to **warn the graveyard keepers** and to help assemble clerics as needed. Asked about the [[Greybraids]], he offered context rather than accusation: the clan **depends on the mines**, the two groups **have a history**, and nothing severe has happened between them very recently. For the details of **how Victor Stonehammer died**, he referred the party to **[[Havid]]**, a man knowledgeable about the [[Ironheart Mines]] — a lead the party has not yet followed.

### The tracking run — Seren and Mike

With official standing secured, Lyrion and Thorn returned to [[Good For What Ails Ya]] while Seren — still wearing [[Thorn]]'s disguise, a **blue-skinned elf** whose coloring recalls Lyrion's — activated her **dragon-egg tracking spell** (one hour's duration) and swept the city with [[Mike]], who simply **carried her** at a brisk, purposeful pace to cover ground faster, smiling when told to make it a race.

The sweep **cleared the [[Greybraids]]' known holdings** — their manor and their forge — and the **city's single mine entrance**; none showed **any egg signal at all**. What it found instead: **two eggs at an abandoned mansion on the cliffside**, and **one egg hidden in a junk pile by a lake**.

They took the lakeside signal first. At a **[[Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)|junk-strewn corner above the lake]]**, Seren located a **large, pristine wooden box** hidden among the refuse — far too clean for where it sat. Mike forced it open with his crowbar: inside, packed in protective padding, a **[[Dragon Eggs|dragon egg]]**. Mike shuffled gear between packs — discarding his bulky fur blankets to make room — and stowed the egg in his backpack. As they left, they noticed a **[[Dwarven Child (Stonewater Lakeside)|dwarven child]]** watching them from the window of a run-down, boarded building overlooking the corner, perhaps a hundred feet off. Mike waved. They chose not to make contact and carried on until they were out of sight.

Walking off, the two turned over what the find implied — perhaps a **smuggler's cache**, perhaps something uglier: an egg **planted where its discovery would incite conflict between rival factions**. The session ended with one egg in the party's custody and two more signals waiting on the cliffside.

## 6/19/24 — Session 2

- Putrid / sour / rotten smell wafting from the river, around seafoam-like masses along the **River of Rolling Brook** (the river surrounding [[Fort Elm]]). [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] investigated and determined it was **not magically caused**.
- [[Teron|Taron]] and [[Bell]] practice-spar through the night. **Teron admits he can't remember more than his basic past and the last 2 weeks.** Teron shows Bell the **map of his hometown**; she doesn't recognize the area.
- [[Victor Barun|Victor]] has the party summoned to his house the next day.
- **Divers went into the Reservoir / cave system** — no more doppelganger spawns found; **more seafoam growing**; huge cave system under [[Fort Elm]] discovered.
- **Victor's job briefing:** an acquaintance in [[Stonewater]] in the north of [[The Wilds]] needs help investigating **8 disappearances** from the same wing of the [[Ironheart Mines|mine]] they all work in. The disappeared are **farmers and local groundskeepers** for the area that supplies **wheat and grain to Fort Elm**. Hired help has either **left citing supernatural things** or disappeared entirely. Victor believes this group most capable of dealing with supernatural causes. He also asks the party to **cart a shipment of fish** to Stonewater for him. **Reward: not discussed yet.**
- **Glass shattering, plate falls. House tilts. The floor gives out and the house falls down around the party.** Party knocked unconscious; voice heard in their minds: *"Gather the living, bring me more flesh!"*

## 6/26/24 — Session 3

- Party awakens one by one to sounds of chaos, in a **sinkhole full of water** where the house used to be.
- **Fish people** (Sahuagin & Kuo-Toa) heading towards the river with villagers — **wearing something over their mouths** — on their shoulders.
- The Sahuagin and Kuo-Toa have **glassy glazed eyes and are foaming at the mouth**.
- **FIGHT.**

## 7/3/24 — Session 4

- Aftermath of the fight. **Guards and guard captain [[Lilith]]** arrive at the scene.
- Belief: the abductors **aren't going to kill captives outright** — they would have done so on land; they kept them alive in order to bring them to **the whirlpool**.
- **Four air-sack masks** found ([[Facemask Device]]). Two more needed if the party wants to descend, or a different method to go deep.
- [[Thorn]] tests a mask in the water — mouth and nose stay dry; **the mask works**.
- [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Teron]], [[Thorn]], and [[Seren Moss|Seren]] dive into the whirlpool. **Thorn is on a 240-ft rope leash held by [[Bell]] from shore**, with the rope also tied to a large tree.
- Water is **slimy and mucus-y**, similar to the cavern where Victor was found. Whirlpool **leads back to the same cavern**.
- Teron and Seren keep descending; **Thorn and Lyrion turn back as they reach the end of the rope**.
- A **skeletal figure (a Spawn)** appears from below.
- Seren and Teron enter combat: **1 Spawn, 2 Sahuagin**.

## 7/17/24 — Session 5

- Teron and Seren in battle (1 Spawn, 2 Sahuagin).
- Lyrion and Thorn hear Seren cast **Toll the Dead** and **unlatch from the rope** Bell is still pulling, turning around to help.
- **Teron goes unconscious and is pulled deeper into the cavern.**
- Lyrion arrives.

## 7/31/24 — Session 6

- **Lilith and her guards found about 6 more water-breathing masks** — masks tally is now ~10.
- The party will look for **other entrances** to the cavern before diving again.
- Investigation split:
  - [[Teron]] looks at guard records — disappearance at a cave on the **north side of the city past the river ([[Lotus Run]])**; continues to look into further disappearances.
  - [[Thorn]] talks to "the freaks on the streets" — speaks with a clown named **[[Soots]]**. Soots tells her of a tavern on the east side called the **[[Spit & Spindle]]** that has an **entrance into the sewers in their back alleyway** (knock 3 times, wait, knock 3 times, wait, knock 1 time).
  - [[Seren Moss|Seren]] goes to inform her family that her mother has been taken.
  - [[Bell]] heads home to check on her hut — **the hut is ransacked**, food gone, **wet earth with unusual webbed prints** (not boot or paw). Either multiple people or one person pacing back and forth. Tracks suggest searching for someone or something specific. Direction of approach not immediately clear.
  - [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] go with Seren and then to the **Council Chambers** with Victor.

## 8/21/24 — Session 7

- Bell tracks the webbed prints: **northwest, parallel to the town wall**, lost at 15-20 ft from camp. Not humanoid but not necessarily beast-like.
- Bell finds an **old oak tree on a small hill with a gulch in it, ~500–600 ft from her camp**. **One of Bell's hunting knives is sitting at the gulch edge.** Faint **drops of water (not running)** coming from deeper in. The Kuo-Toa and slime monsters could fit through.
- Bell returns to the barracks. **Lilith has headed to the coast** and ordered people to **guard the opening in the river**.
- Bell tells the gate guard to relay the gulch info to Lilith.
- Group meets in the plaza with Victor when Bell arrives. **Teron mentions another area further north** where people have been disappearing for many years.
- Victor mentions **[[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] made a not-great impression on the councilor**.
- Council is **unwilling to send their own guard** for fear of opening the city up for other attacks.
- Party follows Seren back to her temple. **Town criers announce a curfew.** The river takes on an **unnatural glow**.
- **Long rest** at the temple.
- The next morning, Seren and Teron help with breakfast. Plan: head to market for caving equipment, then to the northern area where people have been disappearing. **Rotten smell of sulfur** in the air, similar to stale water or sewage.

## 9/6/24 — Session 8

- Shopping done. Check in with Lilith about plans, and to get a pass for the gates.
- **Council has decided to hire / ask for volunteers for a search-and-rescue.** Lilith urges the party to follow up on their investigations. **Lilith gives the party a scroll with her seal on it** for safe passage through the gates ([[Lilith's Seal Scroll]]).
- **The scroll is stored in [[Gustav]].** Lyrion tells Gustav to **memorize Lilith's handwriting for future use**.
- To Bell's camp.
- **Seren sends a Sending spell to her mother** ([[Gwyn Moss|Gwyn]]) and **gets a response**: the prisoners are tied up in a dark area; some have been taken below.
- Bell runs the information back to the gate guards to be relayed to Lilith for the search-and-rescue team.
- [[Thorn]] (Changeling) emulates Kuo-Toa feet and **shapeshifts [[Thorn's Feet|her foot]]** to match the tracks around Bell's camp. Tracks similar to Kuo-Toa prints. **Lyrion draws her shapeshifted foot for science** ([[Thorn's Feet]]) in exchange for promising her **one free performance**.
- Lyrion does **not detect anything magical** around the camp.
- **All Bell's weapons and hunting traps have been stolen.** She takes **pliers** to inventory.

## 9/11/24 — Session 9

- **Teron gives each party member a [[Facemask Device|water-breathing mask]].**
- Putrid smell from the crevasse. Thorn throws lights into the opening.
- Bell and Teron anchor rope; party rappels in. Order: Teron, then Eldrin, then Bell, then Seren, Lyrion, Thorn.
- Teron leads. Trickling water heard. **Eldrin sees faint claw marks** in the stone and dirt — similar to those around Bell's camp; webbed.
- **Piece of torn cotton manufactured cloth** found.
- **Teron's Divine Sense**: not Celestial, fiend, or undead, but **"something is wrong below the earth."**
- Putrid scent worsens with flowing water. Foam patches / slime layer on walls. **Lyrion examines river foam, wall foam, and Kuo-Toa slime — all the same substance.**
- **Bell tries Beast Speech** on the foam — it does not seem to be paying attention.
- **Lyrion puts a tiny drop of acid into the slime vial** — does not neutralize.
- Flowing water heard ahead. Open cavern with **bioluminescent algae** and a Kuo-Toa in the middle.
- **Teron telepathically reaches out to Bell** to warn her the Kuo-Toa has spotted them.
- Party masks up. **Teron is stabbed with a spear.**
- **FIGHT.** Sahuagin (13 hits), Sahuagin Priestess (12 hits), Sahuagin Champion.
- **Lyrion kills a Drow commoner prisoner.** Teron attacks Lyrion with his shield. Eldrin attacks Teron.
- Rescued hostages near the killed Drow: **[[Marcus]] and [[Avery]] — brother-and-sister hunters from around Fort Elm**.
- **Small stone idol** found on a flat marble/limestone rock with water dripping on it — **cylindrical, fish-spine carved along the back**. Hefty. Glows faintly of **conjuration magic**. Bell calls Lyrion over. Cannot identify yet; Lyrion notes it errs Divine over Arcane; Seren may be able to tell more. (See [[Stone Idol of Blibdoolpoolp]].)

## 10/2/24 — Session 10

- Stone idol identified: **[[Blibdoolpoolp]], "The Drowning Goddess"** — patron god of the Kuo-Toa.
- Discussion of Lyrion's actions put aside for now.
- Continuing to spiral down through the cave system.
- **Strip of cloth buried under some rocks** — and something glinting (a piece of metal). **Human skeleton, completely decayed.** Right hand holds a **piece of rusted metal that maybe used to be a shield**. **[[Dagger of Strange Iconography|A dagger]] is found in the ribcage of the skeleton**.
  - **Seal carved into the pommel base**: a **tree, half sprouted above ground and roots below ground**.
- **Detect Magic** pings a **nearly pristine [[Coin of Strange Iconography|golden coin]]** (divination):
  - Face: human silhouette with long flowing hair tied into a braid, short features, human ears, draped in a robe or cloak.
  - Back: same tree-and-roots symbol as on the dagger pommel.
- **The coin is placed into [[Gustav]] for safekeeping.**
- Cave narrows. Very old structural fragments — wood, planks, bricks.
- Cave opens into a **cylindrical / domed cavern**. Building remnants + skeletons everywhere. **All skeletons wear/hold bronze items** with the same crest.
- Race makeup of the dead: **mostly human, then elves, a few dwarfs, one or two halflings**.
- **Common history note**: after the **[[Glyph Scar|Glyph War]]**, bronze was used before the transition to iron or steel — placing the battlefield in the post-Scar transitional period (per the canonical 700–900 year ambiguity, see [[Glyph Scar]]).
- Wet tracks in the dome but no drops of water from the ceiling.
- **Hole in the left wall** with a "spring/river" coming out — too small for any creature — looks like **slime mucus more than water**.
- Lyrion recalls **stories of entities able to affect their surroundings, like ancient dragons**.
- Path gets steep; combat heard ahead. Teron starts running down the path.

## 10/9/24 — Session 11

- Eldrin notes that the party shouldn't be here and is in trouble.
- **FIGHT.** Sahuagin Champion (16 hits, **insectoid/crab-based armor**); Sahuagin Priestess (14); Sahuagin (12).
- **Drow guards have saved the kidnapped humans from the fish people.** Heading to the Drow camp.
- **[[Riknozz]]** — Drow leader, introduced.
- Teron recognizes the location: **this is where he woke up after being dragged underwater while unconscious**. Teron pulls bodies out of the pool — no recognized humans; there are also **Drow bodies, more decomposed than the humans**. Riknozz recognizes the Drow corpses and **removes a [[Necklace of Tyr]] from one**.
- Riknozz suggests speaking to the **clan mystic** about what the lair could be.
- Partial skeletal remains among the Drow camp. Scouts have found a **ruined city below with desecrated grounds**. **An ancient broken cathedral is where the Drow have made their camp.** The **top half of a broken stained-glass mural shows [[Tyr]]** — confirming this is a [[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)|ruined Temple of Tyr]].
- Gwyn is **not** among the Fort Elm residents the Drow saved.
- Party meets the **Drow wise woman** (the **[[Drow Elder|clan mystic]]**). Theory: the cause is an **aboleth** — an ancient being that **reincarnates through ghastly means**, possibly originating from the ruined city, possibly **from before the [[Glyph Scar|Glyph War]]**.
- Decision: continue deeper to find kidnapped villagers and investigate. **Riknozz joins the party.**
- **Short rest.**

## 10/30/24 — Session 12

- **Lyrion suggests trial by combat** with the Drow as atonement for killing the prisoner — proposed to Teron as a way to mend the rift.
- Party descends a few hundred more feet.
- Down here: **cobblestone, and a wagon**.
- **Eldrin, Teron, Bell and Seren gain darkvision for an hour** (cast on them by another party member).
- **Fountain and a statue** emanating spooky light, in a circular room. Seren senses at least one undead; going further she senses **4 more**.
- **Stone gate embedded with glistening jewels — five seated in the arch, one slot empty.** (See [[Riddle Door]] — the missing sixth gem is the white Opal in the fountain statue's hand; **six gems total**.)
- **FIGHT.** Skeleton (16 hits); Skeletal Knight (20 hits).
- **Energy from dead skeletons flows into the statue; 2 more undead created.**
- All gems pulse together. Identified gems:
  - **Green = Emerald**
  - **White = Opal**
  - **Dark Gray = Nuummite**
  - **Lime Green = Peridot**
  - **Red = Ruby**
  - **Purple = Amethyst**

## 11/6/24 — Session 12.5 *(gap session — not in Arc One notes; reconstructed from transcript)*

- The party (with [[Riknozz]] still attached) is at the bottom of the [[Riddle Door]] staircase, looking down into the chamber that opens onto [[The Ancient Ruined City]].
- The riddle answer (**OPEN** — Opal, Peridot, Emerald, Nuummite) is recapped for catch-up.
- **[[Thorn]] looted the dome-chamber corpses** for armor and useful items while the others stared down the stairs. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] gave reluctant blessing: *"these were [not] lovingly put to rest and buried with items of sentimentality by their families… we're trying to assist the living."*
- [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] to [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] at the threshold: *"I have a terrible feeling about what lays below us."* — *"Worse than dad's basement."* — *"Incredibly, I think worse than the basement, Eldren."* **Canonized**: the brothers' father (see [[Darion Thalor]]) used to beat [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] in the family cellar.
- Cloud-nose / water-breathing masks distributed; party advanced down into the ancient city.

## 11/13/24 — Session 13 (continued in DM summary)

- Lyrion is caught by something and **surrounded by illusions** inside the cathedral.
- **Rotting tentacles** appearing from the well; Eldrin has found Lyrion and they are running away.
- Monster yelling **"FREE ME!"**
- A rotting tentacle comes up the staircase toward where the rest of the group is waiting.
- Eldrin and Lyrion return to the group. **Three glowing eyes** stare up the stairs at the party.
- Party retreats up the tunnel toward the Drow camp.
- Thorn, Lyrion, and Eldrin go on with the Drow to their encampment (Thorn's Shapeshifter audience with the clan mystic).
- Seren, Teron, and Bell get all the civilians out of the cavern and back to [[Fort Elm]].

## 12/11/24 — Session 14 (per transcript, content in DM summary)

- Recap framing in the transcript: *"You all had turned tail and fled with the hostages… You all slowly ascended back up the caves, dropping off the [[Riknozz|Drow]] and their companions at the Drow temple, before heading up."*
- The party split: **[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], and [[Thorn]] stay at the Drow camp** ([[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)]]) to handle Drow business; **[[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Bell]], and [[Teron]] escort the civilians** back to [[Fort Elm]].
- The civilians arrive at Fort Elm around nightfall. [[Lilith]] is called; the gates open and the group is escorted to the barracks.
- Back at the Drow camp: the Shapeshifter-heritage audience with the [[Drow Elder]] begins (continued in the [[Fish People|DM summary]] — Thorn's lineage reveal, Lyrion's atonement and the trial-by-combat / blood-debt resolution).

## 12/18/24 — Session 15 (per transcript, content in DM summary)

- Preparations for the final assault on the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]].
- Temple-night planning + impromptu celebration; **Thorn's magical tent** in the guest room of the [[Shrine of Kelemvor]]; [[Teron]] cooks; Seren raids the temple cellars for drinks; the group has a heartfelt chat about the future.
- Approach descent into the caves once more; ominous tentacles hinted at the door.

## 12/19/24 — Session 16 (per transcript, content in DM summary)

- **Final battle against the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]].**
- [[Teron]] taunts the creature; it charms him; his eyes filmed with slime; he stabs [[Seren Moss|Seren]] with a spear.
- Teron uses **dragon-fear breath weapon**: wisdom save DC 13; allies near him are frightened of Teron (cannot move toward him, disadvantage while they can see him).
- Combat continues; Lyrion fireballs; Eldrin fires arrows; Seren breaks the charm.
- Aboleth slain by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s decisive fireball.
- [[Bell]] dives the pit for the [[Spell Scroll Case (Aboleth Pit)|scroll case]]; [[Teron]] guided by [[Mystra]] to the altar — recovers spell scrolls, the mahogany box ([[Sail la Vie]]), and the 5,400 gp coin sack.
- **[[Thorn]] recognizes the box as a transforming boat**. Standard rules cap capacity at 4, but a **house rule** lets it carry the whole party (see [[Sail la Vie]] and [[!Crypt Keepers#House Rules|House Rules]]).
- Return to [[Fort Elm]]: guard salute, 3,000 gp council bounty.

## Recap

Late the night before, after [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] had fallen asleep, [[Seren Moss|Seren]] checked on the still-unconscious [[Mike]], cast a quiet healing spell, left a cold compress on his forehead, and slipped away without waking him.

The next morning the party reconvened in the common room of [[Good For What Ails Ya]]. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] was first down — he greeted the dwarven barkeep **[[Nastrin]]**, ordered the meal of the day and a breakfast mead, asked what was worth seeing in [[Stonewater]] (a play, possibly, that day), and settled into a booth. [[Mike]] came down next, surprised to find his bruises faded; he ordered three of the special and a mead. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] whipped around: *"Wake! You're alive."* [[Rhaenyra]] joined, then [[Thorn]] (sleepy, ordered her usual from [[Nastrin]]), then [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], nursing the prior night's headache, half-heartedly Prestidigitating himself clean, mistaking a random patron for the server and asking for tea with honey. Seren returned with pastries from elsewhere; Lyrion belatedly registered Thorn's presence, and Thorn reminded him they had already spoken in the alley the night before.

[[Thorn]] introduced herself to [[Mike]] — *"You must be the tall one they've been telling me about"* — and offered a handshake; Mike returned it with a hard grip. Thorn asked if he had ever considered show business. Mike said no, he usually only thought about magic, and added that he used to be a wizard. [[Rhaenyra]] questioned that — and Lyrion stepped in to tell the story of the **[[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|fairy-grove encounter]]** east of Stonewater: a zone of unstable magic at the center of which sat a carnivorous plant projecting an angler-fish-style fae-illusion lure; Lyrion **Polymorphed himself into a sheep** to escape the plant's reach (a deliberate 4th-level cast — see [[Lyrion Thalor]] mechanical notes; the in-character framing of "magic twisting" is Lyrion's dramatic retelling, consistent with the zone's general magical instability but not the cause of the transformation itself); the plant attacked Mike; Mike fled the fight and carried Lyrion (still a sheep) away. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] broke into laughter — *"when our father called you sheepish, he didn't mean it literally"* — Seren added that her own Sending had been scrambled in the same zone, Lyrion lamented that he had nearly gotten Mike killed and learned nothing usable, and Mike concluded the incident had clarified for him that **he was not a wizard at all but a sorcerer** — *"Mike the Sorcerer."* Lyrion put a hand on him and clarified he had dismissed Mike as an apprentice not in anger but because Mike's magic was not wizardry, and because Lyrion no longer trusted himself to act as Mike's master after nearly getting him killed. Mike replied that there was nothing to apologize for and not much a wizard could teach a sorcerer regardless.

Lyrion realized then that Mike had gone straight to bed the night before without bathing; he searched Mike's clothes for plant residue and **pulled a small bit of bark caught in Mike's belt-tuft**. He held it up — a useful sample. Seren asked whether he could discern magical properties from it. Lyrion shrugged: *"Who could say? I doubt my abilities as a scholar at this point."* Thorn suggested Eldrin teach Lyrion to fight. Seren reassured him the party covered each other's gaps.

Talk shifted to the road. Lyrion asked whether the rest of the party had met their contact in Stonewater; Seren summarized the **[[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] audience** and the **suspicious cart** they had spotted alongside [[Basil]]'s caravan and tried to tail through the city — lost in the second tier. Thorn noted Baldrin had kept framing his cooperation as a favor despite the party having helped save his town. Seren proposed using a spell to **locate the missing dragon eggs**, pairing it with the **feather-and-dagger clan emblem** they had seen on the cart's occupants. Seren went to Nastrin, opened her notebook, sketched the emblem, and Nastrin identified it: **the [[Greybraids]], a dwarf clan in the city.** Seren thanked him, left a silver, and brought the name back to the table. Lyrion lowered his voice and warned that if the Greybraids were tied to the egg theft, they would have to be approached very quietly so they would not realize they were being investigated.

[[Thorn]] connected the dots to the **[[Ironheart Mines]] murders**: Baldrin had said the bodies turning up around town looked killed by pickaxe; the victims were known to be **survivors of the recent cave-in** in the mines. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] concluded the dead miners had likely **seen something they should not have**. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] said she had first guessed something like the mind-twisting threat from [[Fort Elm]], but this was beginning to sound like a mortal conspiracy. [[Bell]] noted that both the egg-theft and the mine-killings revolved around [[Stonewater]].

The party debated the order of operations: mines first or Greybraids first. [[Rhaenyra]] reminded them [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] was expecting them that morning to provide a [[Mine Pass|mine pass]]. Thorn argued the mines could surface useful intelligence before they approached the clan; Bell warned that several party members had already been seen by the suspicious cart's occupants — but Lyrion and Mike had not, and Thorn could change appearances. Seren disliked splitting up again but admitted that was emotion more than logic, and said she should accompany whichever group went after the Greybraids since she was the one who could attempt the locator spell. Lyrion squeezed her hand and argued for the split anyway: a small pair posing as subtle business partners to the Greybraids, the rest investigating the cave-in before the Greybraids noticed anything. The group resolved to go to Baldrin first for the mine pass and noted that **Baldrin himself might not be trustworthy** despite presenting as the wronged employer.

[[Seren Moss|Seren]] asked [[Thorn]] for a disguise. Thorn took her by the hand and hurried her upstairs to alter her hair and makeup — moving her off her usual blonde. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] took a drink from his canteen, sighed, and asked [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] to walk with him. Eldrin agreed; the two brothers left the tavern and walked to **the same alley** where Lyrion had been sick the night before.

In the alley, Lyrion invited Eldrin to say what he was angry about plainly. Eldrin said it was that Lyrion had nearly gotten himself killed — and that after losing their mother [[Endra Thalor|Endra]] and long ago losing the man their father [[Darion Thalor]] used to be, he could not stand watching his brother be reckless. Lyrion answered with a memory of a childhood hunting trip when their father had ordered him to shoot a doe, taken the bow back when Lyrion could not do it, handed it to Eldrin, and from that day forward had treated Eldrin as the capable son — leaving Lyrion out of hunting, sparring, and militia training. He had grown up feeling weak in their father's eyes, and after their mother's death had long carried the thought that **if he had been more like Eldrin, things might have been different**. He told Eldrin, with tears building, that he now felt a pull toward **something larger** happening in the world — not just in Fort Elm or Stonewater — and believed he was meant to be part of it, even if he was clumsy and foolhardy in trying. He apologized for nearly getting Mike killed, for frightening Seren, and for frightening Eldrin, and asked Eldrin to give him a chance to become whatever he was meant to be.

Eldrin replied that Lyrion misunderstood him. Eldrin had worked so hard to become a great hunter **precisely because Lyrion was meant for greater things** and should never have had to live the life of a warrior if Eldrin could spare him from it. His bravery, he said, was not confidence in himself — it was the need to protect his little brother. He was not upset that Lyrion wanted to solve what was happening in the world; he was upset that Lyrion had tried to do it **alone**, when he had family and friends who would stand beside him. Lyrion broke down crying, admitted Eldrin was right, called him *"my greatest research assistant and the greatest brother I have ever had,"* and apologized for being stupid and self-absorbed. Eldrin replied that geniuses were said to be that way — and added that [[Seren Moss|Seren]] had been frightened for him even if she would not say so. Lyrion said he had been able to tell, stood up, and the brothers embraced in a long hug. As they started to leave the alley, Lyrion remarked that **even he could tell [[Rhaenyra]] was interested in Eldrin as more than a friend**.

A frightened human voice nearby cut both brothers off. Eldrin moved toward the sound; Lyrion followed. They came upon **a [[Dwarven Woman (Stonewater Pickaxe Body)|dwarven woman]]** screaming, crouched over **a corpse with a pickaxe embedded in its body**.

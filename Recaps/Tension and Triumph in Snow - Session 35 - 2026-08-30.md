## Recap

### Opening the boxes

The two locked, magical, dragon-egg-sized boxes from the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|manor]] finally gave up their contents: a **top hat**, surprisingly light (~½ lb), in [[Mike]]'s box, and a **bundle of smooth black silk**, folded with care, in [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]]'s. Neither item would explain itself without a night's rest spent attuning (a new table rule: identifying a magic item takes a long rest with it). Also surfaced from the personal-effects chest: a handbound **prayer book titled [[Tenets of the Ones Forgotten]]** — the [[Acolytes of Oghma|cult]]'s doctrine in liturgical form, the same creed as the [[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|journal]]: *you do not truly die if you are never forgotten*. Eldrin handed it straight to [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] ("that seems a Lyrion thing"), whose to-read pile now includes a scroll case, Bell's second case, and a cult hymnal.

### Finishing the sweep

Lyrion could barely stand — leaning on [[Seren Moss|Seren]], **five levels of exhaustion deep** — so the sweep went quickly. The upstairs side bedrooms told the story of a **large group in residence**: beds plus packed bedrolls against the walls, traveling supplies, and about **10 gold** scattered through the backpacks. The library held only the **previous owner's Dwarvish collection** — business theory, ledgers, a history of the mines, gazetteers of trading cities in the wilds and the desert north; not one work of fiction. Seren took **the mine history and the trading-cities volume** for study, and read the shelf itself as a clue: whoever lived here before the cult was childless, commercial, and probably tied to the mines' operation.

And the gold: the table settled Session 34's dangling question by having **Eldrin take the whole chest — roughly 6,000 gp, 120 pounds of it** (maintainer-confirmed: all of it). His cover story downstairs later: *"Someone decided to be generous."* [[Thorn]], told only that the circus is now funded: *"You're welcome. I always knew somebody would see our talents."*

Lyrion Prestidigitated the blood off everyone's clothes (the sword-hole in his shirt was beyond the cantrip), and the party walked back into a city winding down for the evening — about five o'clock, nothing following them.

### The captain comes to dinner

At [[Good For What Ails Ya]], Seren hired a street messenger (5 copper) to ask **[[Captain Grazug]]** to meet her for a drink. Then the party split by wear: Lyrion shambled upstairs — Seren got him arranged in bed, and he caught her hand as she turned to go: *"Will you stay for just a little longer?"* He fell asleep with tears sliding down his face. [[Mike]] hit his own bed like a felled tree.

Downstairs, [[Thorn]] finally got the day's news — *"Lyrion died."* — and worked the room the way only Thorn can: a whiskey slid silently in front of the hollow-eyed Eldrin, another offered to Seren (declined: *"I'm a cleric. I don't need alcohol to repress my emotions"* — she took it later anyway, after the captain left), a sketch pad out to design **circus outfits for everyone** now that the show is funded. [[Bell]] told [[Rhaenyra]] and the table what the possession felt like — she has run hot in fights before, *"but that is the first time it truly felt like something external was pushing on me"* — and voiced the fear that matters: next time it might be a friend without a tiger's hide. Rhaenyra, flatly: *"You were possessed."* Bell wants it discussed as a group. Eldrin stared into his mead and dodged every probe — *"maybe I'm just tired"* — processing a brother twice nearly lost.

Grazug arrived half an hour after the summons. Seren laid out **everything, nearly**: the road from Fort Elm, the dragons, the stolen [[Dragon Eggs|eggs]], the thieves lost in the city, the suspected [[Greybraids]] tie, the detection sweep, both visits to the manor, the paralysis that opened the fight, the hidden tunnel running north toward the [[Ironheart Mines]], the fled [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]], and the [[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|mage]] who escaped — *"This goes beyond politics. This is existential."* She held back exactly two things: **the egg in the party's custody, and the forged [[Broader Warrant]]** — and downplayed Lyrion's death to "gravely injured." (Eldrin, interviewed separately and several meads deep, cheerfully told the captain the whole resurrection story, diamond and all.)

The captain's response was better than the party feared: *"For what it's worth, I believe you."* But process is process:

- **An investigation is open** — a manor full of bodies requires one, self-defense or not; the party is deputized but not guard, and Seren asked that any repercussions **fall on her alone**.
- **Statements taken on the spot** — Eldrin (the full chaotic narrative, including that the cultists guarded the tunnel-cellar and the mine collapse connection), Bell (the paralysis, and the Elder's words as he drove his sword into Lyrion: ***"You will be forgotten"***), Rhaenyra and Thorn briefly. The **[[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|cult journal]] went into evidence** — the guard will copy it and hunt for identification.
- **Guards dispatched** to the manor to lock down the crime scene.
- **The deputization stands** — *"we may end up needing your services again"* — but the party gets a **minder**: someone official, at their side at all times, to be treated *"with the utmost respect."*
- And a fresh puzzle from the guardhouse: multiple citizens reported **something large flying at the top of the cavern — with no wings** — before it disappeared. Seren's shudder: a shapeshifter? The mage? The aberration? *(New thread.)*

Then Thorn ended the evening by **transforming into the wagon driver** in front of the captain — the suspect who drove the eggs into the city — and sat for the guard's sketch artist, who allowed it was the easiest job of his career (*"normally the perp isn't sitting right in front of me"*). Grazug also finally shared portraits: **the mage** — human, jaundiced, glowing eyes, smirking, a **mark tattooed or scarred into his forehead** — was seen clearly for the first time by most of the party, and so, for that matter, was **Grazug himself** (a dwarf; three of the seven — [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], [[Bell]], and [[Rhaenyra]] — had never actually met him; the writ four were received at the Barracks in Session 32).

### The night watch and the axe in the hallway

The party set watches anyway: **Thorn** (wig-making for the egg — more below) → **Rhaenyra** → **Eldrin**. On Rhaenyra's shift, **heavy metal footsteps** stopped outside the door — she slid a dagger under the door to watch the reflection and found **a dwarf in unfamiliar-make armor sitting cross-legged in the hallway, battleaxe across her lap**. A dry chuckle from outside: *"The guard captain sent me. I'm not here to hurt you."* The dagger slid back under the door. Message relayed to Thorn; everyone slept.

Eldrin took the dawn shift sitting beside her, and the party met **[[Dura]]**: fifteen years in the guard, **[[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge]] colors** (the clan runs deep in Stonewater's guard and military), second-eldest of **nine siblings** in a military family, hates riding in carts, does not trust horses (*"They know too much. Have you looked at a horse's eyes?"* — Eldrin, vindicated at last, nearly proposed on the spot). He pitched her the circus. She'd been warned the party were troublemakers; he pled entrapment by fate.

### Morning — the little spoon and the first snow

Lyrion woke beside Seren and thanked her plainly: *"I am alive thanks to you."* Then, quietly, the conversation the table will remember: he asked her how the afterlife is portioned — whether everyone shares one — and told her what he saw. **His mother** — [[Endra Thalor]] — clear as day in a place he could barely describe, her face sharp for the first time in decades of failed remembering. *"You were there to call me back. Lest I may have stayed. But I don't think it's my time yet."* Seren gave him [[Kelemvor]]'s teaching: the Judge places the dead fairly, by conduct and faith — and if a revival spell works, **it was the gods' will that you return**. *(A long rest took Lyrion to 4 of 6 exhaustion; Mike is down to 1.)*

Thorn burst in on the morning like a one-woman parade: **hot springs today** — the barkeep had confirmed springs on the city's eastern side, up on the highest cliff, and a session-long consensus formed that a man who died yesterday has earned a spa day. *"It's not every day that one of your friends dies and then comes back — so I think it's cause for celebration."*

The egg problem got solved en route to comedy: Thorn had spent her watch giving the [[Dragon Eggs|dragon egg]] **a wig and a full face of makeup** ("the prettiest girl at the party"), and proposed carrying it openly as her baby. The DM's ruling — *"it's so egg-shaped"* — and the table's horror ("Humpty-Dumpty-looking-ass baby") sent the egg **into Mike's backpack** instead, riding along until the party finds a **new tavern** to relocate to (Seren's precaution, seconded by the captain: they've been attacked once; don't sleep where the enemy knows to look). Mike, meanwhile, had attuned to the top hat overnight: the tag reads **Mordenkainen's Magical Hat of Disguises** — Disguise Self at will while worn ([[Hat of Disguises (Mordenkainen)|item page]]). Mike's takeaway: *"It's a sorcerer's hat."* A prank conspiracy (Thorn, Mike, Rhaenyra) immediately formed around the hot springs: confront Eldrin with **multiple Rhaenyras**, one of them nine feet tall. Eldrin has not yet shown Lyrion the **black silk cloth** — he's saving it for the two of them alone; it remains unidentified.

Breakfast ("anything but eggs"), then out into **the first snowfall of the year** sifting down through the cavern's light-slits — late fall, the mountains above already white. Mike hadn't seen snow since leaving his village; it also reminded the party they still owe themselves replacement **fur blankets** (dropped at the lakeside in Session 32). And in a table moment worthy of the vault's margins, Seren mused that she could *swear* there was another guy with them back in the fish-people days — *"I can't remember his name now, it's so strange"* — while Lyrion counted on his fingers and concluded no one was missing. *(The written-out [[Teron]], remembered as a hole in the world.)*

### The guardhouse and the pumpkin

At the guardhouse, Grazug took **Lyrion's statement** (truthful, terse, monosyllabic where possible — a man who wanted a bath, not a deposition) and **Mike's** (in which the phrase "I'm a sorcerer" was volunteered, by Mike's count, at least six times, and underlined on the captain's clipboard accordingly). Status from the guard side: **no movement at the manor overnight**, a **guard posted atop the tunnel ladder**, the mine itself not yet entered — and **no reports of the [[Victor Stonehammer|revenant]] resurfacing yet** (Mike's reminder also jogged Seren: she needs Banishment back among her prepared spells). Thorn asked Dura to point out candidate taverns as they walk.

Shopping came next — a jeweler in the third district is the target for a **replacement Revivify diamond** (300 gp minimum, *"the gods are very specific"*) — but the party made it only as far as **[[Aiker's Potion Explorium]]**: a shop built inside an actual hollowed **pumpkin**, run by **[[Aiker]]**, a dapper **kenku** in vest and ascot who sells potions in **vegetable-shaped vials** (carrot healing, onion greater, pumpkin superior). The party restocked on healing potions *(ledger deliberately unrecorded)*; Thorn bought herself a minor luck potion on gambler's instinct; and two discoveries stole the scene:

- Rummaging for barter material, Lyrion opened the **[[Arcane Scroll Case (Cliffside Mansion)|scroll case]]** Eldrin had recovered for him at the manor — and found a scroll in cramped ancient wizard-cursive channeling magic beyond anything he can yet cast: a **[[Scroll of Chain Lightning|scroll of Chain Lightning]]** (6th level). He rolled it back up very, very carefully.
- Aiker's back shelf holds a **Potion of Vitality** — a very rare brew that erases all exhaustion — priced at a brutal 7,000 gp. Lyrion, who needs it more than anyone alive, went full Order-of-Scribes salesman: five self-scribed scrolls (Divination — *"a spell scroll that lets you talk to a god"* — Fireball, Invisibility, Witch Bolt, Mage Armor) plus coin, Gustav thumped on the counter as a credential. The kenku pulled out an abacus. **The haggling was still running when the session ended** — to be settled off-screen.

Next session: the hot springs. The DM was helpfully reassuring: *"All I'm saying is, there's a massive skeleton in the lake."*

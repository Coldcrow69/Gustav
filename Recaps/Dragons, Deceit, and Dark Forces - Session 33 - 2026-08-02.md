## Recap

### The mine — the tunnel that shouldn't exist

At the collapsed exploratory shaft, [[Rhaenyra]] finished casting **Find Familiar** and sent a **tiny, adorable crab** through the rubble. Through its eyes the group saw about **fifteen feet of tunnel** beyond the collapse, opening into a space the miners had *broken into* — with a **pre-existing passage branching left and right**. Sent right, the crab found the passage running toward a **large cavern roughly four hundred feet on**; ordered to sweep both branches, it never came back — after some twenty minutes the familiar link was **severed**. The group's read: the **left branch** is where the answers are, and the crab's fate is its own warning.

Questioning their **mine guide** (the foreman left with them), they got the paperwork side: this was one of the **furthest-reaching exploratory shafts**, and by the guide's own **map it should simply have ended here** — there should be **no intersection**. Combined with the [[Murdered Mine Survivor (Stonewater Alley)|dead survivor]]'s testimony, the sequence is now clear: the miners **breached into a passage that was already there**, were **attacked**, and the mine **collapsed on them as they fled**. The party deliberately **did not tell the guide** that the crab had confirmed the tunnel continues. They left without running into [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] again — his **job offer** (he stated in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]] that he wants to formally hire the party; terms never specified) remains something Bell wants pinned down before the party gets, in her words, screwed over.

### The Greybraid gate

Walking back through the city — in the same window in which [[Seren Moss|Seren]] and [[Mike]] were making their [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]] egg run — [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Thorn]] detoured to **[[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|Greybraid Manor]]**: opulent, a notch smaller than the Stoneforge estate, **clan guards on the front gate**. Lyrion presented the party's **[[Writ of Deputization|deputization papers]]** to an older, grizzled dwarf guard, who examined them, found them legitimate — and asked if they weren't looking for the **Stoneforge estate** instead. Lyrion asked to speak with someone who deals regularly with the [[Stoneforge Clan]] about the cave-in, pressed the urgency, and asked that any reply be sent through **[[Captain Grazug]]** at the guard post. His parting hook, aimed over the guard's shoulder at the clan's leadership: shared information could benefit the **patriarch's** business dealings with Stoneforge — *"I know your clans don't always see eye to eye, and their control of the mines is… to say the least."* The guard: *"I shall relay that to the patriarch."* In the exchange the patriarch was named: **[[Kassadren]]**. Lyrion and Thorn were not admitted, and walked on.

### One egg on the table

Back at [[Good For What Ails Ya]] — Lyrion and Thorn arriving first, practicing a card trick — Seren and Mike returned and, quietly: *"Found one of them. I believe I know the location of the others."* The group took it upstairs, locked the door, and [[Mike]] unpacked the **[[Dragon Eggs|egg]]**: **warm to the touch, more stone-like than smooth**, effectively a big hot rock. Lyrion tuned out the world, pulled out [[Gustav]], and started sketching and measuring; Mike set a **small minecart he had stolen somewhere along the way** next to the egg — *"he's giving the baby a toy."* Seren scrubbed off the last of [[Thorn]]'s disguise. [[Bell]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] got back from the mine, learned from the barkeep that "the shorter blue fellow" was upstairs, and Eldrin announced himself at the room door in a disguised voice as the **"Stonewater Guard, open up!"** — panic inside, Lyrion swinging the door open brandishing the writ, and a full Eldrin bear-hug and belly laugh on the other side.

Discoveries were traded both ways: the crab's tunnel and the foreman's too-short map on one side; on the other, the egg — and Seren's report that she had sensed **at least two more eggs** in an abandoned-looking manor on the **southeastern cliffs** (the DM's recap framed the manor signal as the **majority** of the clutch). Why one egg sat alone in a junk pile across town: [[Mike]]'s theory — the **transporter withheld one from their employer**, a rift the party might exploit to *"have them fight each other"*; [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s theory — a **drop point for the [[Greybraids]]**, proof of delivery: *"here's one, pay us more for the others."* They debated keeping the egg warm — Mike offered his fire, but the party's **[[Black Thermal Cube]]** (ambient heat, can't burn) looked the safer incubator, with the half-joking fear of accidentally **hatching** it.

The plan: move on the cliffside manor **immediately**, before the eggs could be relocated. **[[Thorn]] and [[Rhaenyra]] stayed behind to guard the recovered egg** at the tavern — the in-fiction assignment that covered Jenna's and Miranda's absence from the session. Seren re-armored (Lyrion helping, openly relieved to have her out of the disguise — and stepping on a rake by declaring her human features "one of my favorite things about you," to the half-elf). The party took an hour's rest, roughly 4:30 in the afternoon. And Seren — *"I cannot believe I'm suggesting this"* — asked Lyrion whether [[Gustav]] could copy [[Captain Grazug]]'s writ into **something more forceful**. Lyrion, coyly: *"Gustav and I do love a small act of forgery here and there."* He forged the **[[Broader Warrant]]** as they traveled — sweeping legalese authority to search anywhere and detain anyone.

### The cliffside manor

The manor: **run-down, windows boarded**, a **graveyard out front** (Eldrin held his breath crossing it). Cover story: traveling musicians hired for a gig — Lyrion dug a **drum** out of Eldrin's bag and handed it to Seren: *"You're our drummer."* Then Seren's **detection spell** made the cover moot: an **[[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]]**, somewhere **below the building**. The party remembered the last aberration they fought ([[The Undead Aboleth]]); Lyrion's *"surely it wouldn't be the same thing twice… could it?"* hung there.

[[Mike]] "picked the lock" — the front door burst open on a dim mud room showing **signs of regular traffic** through a supposedly abandoned building. From behind an inner door, a frightened voice begged them not to rob anyone. Lyrion, in full authority: **"We're with the Stonewater Guard. We have a writ of investigation. Come out slowly."** Out came **[[Vil]]** — a young human in priestly robes wearing an amulet with **no recognizable holy symbol** — claiming the residents were **[[Acolytes of Oghma]]**, newly granted the property. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] (religion check) recognized **[[Oghma]]** as the **god of knowledge** — which did not stop the table's pre-reveal dread that this was a **Tiamat** egg-cult, or Bell's *"as long as it's not the whispered one."* Seren offered a half-truth: deputized investigators hunting the revenant behind the city's murders, and she could sense **a malignant presence in this house**. Vil couldn't authorize a search but led them to the sect's **[[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]]**, past startled robed residents — **mostly dwarves**, perhaps a dozen in the building, people "coming and going" often. Seren quietly watched for faces from the **hooded cart crew** the party had tailed in Session 29.

The Elder heard Seren out; Lyrion skipped to the end and openly accused the sect of **harboring an aberration beneath the house and the stolen dragon eggs** — "contraband of the highest order." The Elder denied everything, insisted they were a **sect of healers**, and named their landlord: the right to occupy the house had been granted by **[[Kassadren]]** — the **Greybraids' patriarch**. He demanded proof of authority; Lyrion handed over the genuine **[[Writ of Deputization]]** *and* the forged **[[Broader Warrant]]**. The Elder read for a long, long moment (Eldrin defused the silence by knocking over a teapot and offering to pay for it), handed the papers back — and ordered **[[Vil]]** to escort the group downstairs and see them *"well taken care of."*

### "We will not be forgotten"

At the cellar door, as [[Bell]] stepped out of the Elder's line of sight, **hostile magic seized her** — paralyzed mid-stride. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] broke it instantly with **Lesser Restoration**; a second hold attempt on Bell failed against a natural 20, and a third try on Seren failed too. A robed figure conjured a **spectral sickle** — *"Leave this place"* — another shouted **"We will not be forgotten!"**, and the manor erupted.

- **[[Vil]] fled** for the cellar stairs, locking the door behind him; [[Mike]] **kicked it down**, caught him on the staircase, and slammed him headfirst into the steps with his full weight — killing him outright. Seren, following, found the remains and pressed on. (*"My god, we jumped to bloodshed so fast."* — Mike has been carrying his rage since **[[Bluefield]]**, where the innocents he tried to save burned.)
- **[[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]]** ran a cultist through the chest mid-swing at Lyrion (*"I barely stabbed him!"*), then followed into the cellar and killed a second one there, checking that Seren was holding together.
- **[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]** Misty-Stepped onto a table, [[Gustav]] floating at his shoulder, the room smelling of ozone: *"Stand down your men or you'll all die here."* The [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]] ran instead — Lyrion's upcast **Witch Bolt** tore a chunk out of his shoulder, a **Counterspell** (*"Silence!"*) ate the Elder's answer, and a second Witch Bolt followed him out of the room. The Elder escaped, badly wounded. One terrified cultist who obeyed Lyrion's *"sit down"* — hands up, *"I'm not gonna surrender to thieves"* wavering — was later **cut down by his own comrade** (*"You coward!"*) to keep him from fleeing or surrendering.
- **[[Bell]]**, hemmed in by cultists in bad light, was carved by the **spectral sickle** (slashing plus **necrotic**), lit a wall sconce to fight by, cleaved one man near in half, took a Hold Person to the face and shrugged it off, went **invisible** (Hidden Step) — one cultist literally ran into her stomach — and fought a cornered retreat.
- In the **cellar**, an **[[Acolytes of Oghma|ornate-robed figure]]** turned and hurled a **blast of fire** down the corridor — Mike ducked through unscathed, Seren caught half behind her shield, **Eldrin ate the full blast** and had to Healing-Word himself up.
- **[[Seren Moss|Seren]]**, tracking the aberration's pull even as it **moved away**, spotted a cut-brick **rectangle outline behind the cellar barrels** and pressed it open: a **concealed stone door** onto a **natural cave system** — *"this looks familiar,"* she said, of tunnels uncomfortably like the mine's. The trail led on; she followed at a run, finding a **ladder descending deeper**, the aberration always ahead of her senses. Eldrin followed her down the hidden route; the group was, by everyone's admission, **badly split**.

### The fall of Lyrion Thalor

Upstairs, a **northern door opened**: a **[[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|bald human in ornate robes]]** demanded to know what was happening, was told *"we were attacked, sir,"* — and joined the fight. From a wand slipped out of his sleeve he fired **bolts of energy**: all three hit [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], who gasped, fumbled for [[Gustav]] — and dropped to his knees, then face-first to the floor. [[Bell]], enraged and blaming herself, fought to stand over him; [[Mike]] doubled back up the stairs and answered with **radiant sunbolts** that staggered but did not fell the mage. Lyrion's death save failed — the roll would have passed, but the **exhaustion he still carries from the [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|carnivorous-plant fight]]** dragged it under. And as Bell tried to shield him, a cultist **drove a sword into his downed body**.

**Lyrion Thalor died on the manor floor.** The session ended there — with [[Seren Moss|Seren]] (five-carat diamond in her pack, one minute on the clock) two floors away in a dark tunnel, and the DM's closing ruling: **next session opens with a ten-round countdown for Revivify.** Deep in the caves, [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] felt only that the others weren't following — and a very bad feeling in his gut.

# The Search for Dragon Eggs - Session 29 - 2026-05-11

> **Campaign Session 29.** First session of the [[Egg Hunt]] arc. First session recorded in this Obsidian vault (the party migrated note-taking platforms at this point — earlier sessions are summarized in [[Fish People]], [[Bluefield Burning]], and OneNote-era raw notes).

As the sun dipped below the horizon, casting a warm glow over the city of [[Stonewater]], the party approached its imposing entrance. [[Thorn]] led her companions through the bustling streets while [[Seren Moss|Seren]] kept a vigilant eye out for a cart they had previously spotted. Their mission was urgent; they sought [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] (Baldrin Stoneforge), the foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]], to assist with troubling disappearances in the area. After asking a local for directions, the group identified themselves as performers-turned-adventurers, and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] presented a calling card to establish their credibility.

Upon reaching [[Baldrin Stoneforge]]'s residence, they were welcomed inside. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] introduced herself and conveyed their purpose, detailing the destruction of [[Bluefield]] by a **multi-headed red dragon** known as [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]]. She recounted their encounter with a [[Captured Half-Dragon]] who revealed that dragon eggs had been stolen, urging [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] for assistance in recovering them to prevent further devastation. The conversation shifted to the recent disappearances and murders in the mines. The party inquired about the nature of the deaths, the suspected weapon, and the missing corpse of the accused man, suggesting that a magical influence might be at play.

The urgency of their mission deepened as they reported the road from [[Autumnbreak]]: a **caravan** led by [[Basil]] (presenting as a dried-fish merchant) that had picked up unfamiliar travelers en route, traveling loosely alongside a **separate second cart**. On entering [[Stonewater]] the caravan and the separate cart **split**; the party tailed the cart through the city and **lost it in the second tier**. [[Thorn]] showcased her shapeshifting abilities by transforming into the **separate cart's driver**, providing a clear description of the man they sought (not Basil). [[Rhaenyra]] added details about an insignia — a **feather-and-dagger emblem** — she had seen on the cart's occupants, pressing [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] for its significance. (The emblem would be identified the following morning by **[[Nastrin]]**, the barkeep at [[Good For What Ails Ya]], as the crest of the **[[Greybraids]]** dwarf clan — see [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-18|Session 30]].) With the threat of the dragon looming, [[Rhaenyra]] warned that the city could face destruction if the eggs were not recovered. The meeting concluded with an agreement to reconvene the following day, and the party departed with a sense of purpose.

Meanwhile, [[Mike]] and [[Lyrion Thalor]] arrived at the tavern [[Good For What Ails Ya]], where [[Mike]], weary from their journey, went straight to bed. [[Lyrion Thalor]] chose to linger at the bar, ordering festive drinks adorned with sparklers. The rest of the party soon joined him, with [[Seren Moss|Seren]] sitting beside [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Rhaenyra]] playfully removing his drink. [[Lyrion Thalor]] shared tales of his earlier misadventures, lamenting a "wizard battle" he had lost, despite [[Mike]] not being a wizard. He recounted the chaos of an anti-magic zone and the magical instability that followed.

As the evening wore on, [[Lyrion Thalor]] abruptly excused himself and stumbled outside, where he faced the harsh reality of his overindulgence. [[Thorn]], still disguised as [[Lyrion Thalor]], used Mage Hand to hold back his hair as he vomited, leading to a moment of confusion when [[Lyrion Thalor]] saw his own face. In a flurry of emotions, he embraced [[Thorn]], mistaking her for himself, and expressed his frustrations about imperfection. [[Thorn]] offered him comfort, helping him back inside, where [[Lyrion Thalor]] cleaned himself with Prestidigitation.

The group settled down for dinner, with [[Seren Moss|Seren]] attempting to coax [[Lyrion Thalor]] into eating something mild to counteract the effects of the alcohol. They confirmed that [[Mike]] was resting upstairs, and as the night drew to a close, the party secured rooms at the tavern, ready to resume their quest in the morning.

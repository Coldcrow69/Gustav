## Recap

### Morning at the Sour Jackal

The party chose not to play out the rest of the drinking — and not to go back for the **fur blankets** on the cliffside that night (Lyrion and Eldrin: *just buy more blankets*). Everyone long-rested, and the DM ruled on the spa's dividend: **[[Mike]] and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] are clear of exhaustion.** *"I haven't felt this good in so long."* Lyrion prepared **Banishment** — it's a wizard spell too; *"the only class that doesn't have access to Banishment is Bard"* — so the party now has two casters ready for the [[Victor Stonehammer|revenant]].

[[Thorn]], ear to her door, overheard the night guard giving [[Dura]] her handover in the hallway: **no sign of the creature, no disappearances linked to it** — and an editorial, offered not quite as a complaint, that the party had drunk a great deal and was *"a pretty adventurous group, not typically what they have seen in Stonewater."* Thorn went back to bed. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] and Lyrion, dressing, gossiped: *"Rhaenyra and Eldrin, huh?"* Lyrion, measured: his brother has always been a ladies' man and broken many hearts — but *"to say he is the same person he was when we left home would be disingenuous,"* and besides, *"it's been a very long while since he's been to a brothel, so I think the chances are quite low."* Seren: *"I quite like Rhaenyra."* Lyrion knocked on every door (*"you dying?"* — Thorn; *"in a city this size it is statistically likely that murder occurs as we speak!"*), and got *"Be out in a minute"* from Eldrin's room, with a giggle from [[Rhaenyra]] behind it.

Dura was on her stolen stool in the hallway sharpening her axe — a nervous habit — arrived an hour before and nervous for a reason: *"The timeline that you all gave said the revenant should be running around by now, and we haven't heard anything about it."* She's assigned to the party **at least until the manor investigation is over**; the creature, she noted, *"has killed quite a bit of the guard."*

### The egg moved

As Mike swung his backpack on, the [[Dragon Eggs|egg]] **jostled — once**. He noticed; Dura, waving good-morning from her stool, did not. At breakfast, in the lowest voice an eight-foot goliath has: *"We may have a new situation forming."* — *"We have so many situations, Mike."* — *"We have a new one hatching."* Lyrion stared at the backpack; it stayed still; Mike has felt it move only the once, *"but the fact that it has moved once means it's starting to become active."* Seren: *"Well — I'm glad it's alive."* Nobody at the table knows how dragons work.

Over breakfast with Dura: the revenant is overdue. Seren's read — revenants are blind rage and vengeance, not planners, so lying low makes no sense; Rhaenyra — they act on opportunity. **Lyrion's theory**: the killings have been too targeted, too prepared, for a spirit acting alone — *"I strongly believe that there may be necromantic forces at work here"* — someone is **using the revenant as a tool**. *"There's a nasty thought."* Thorn, looking around the table: **no more splitting up in fights.** Seren nodded at the table, still carrying the guilt of running downstairs at the manor; Thorn gave her a small hug.

Plan for the day: check in with the Stoneforges — Bell: *"They did want to actually physically hire us"* — because *"the search for the eggs and the search for what happened in the mines is going to be the same search"* (Seren). Mike, catching up on what the guard knows: the stolen eggs, yes; the one on his back, no. Bell worried the cave-in couldn't be cleared quickly; Rhaenyra and Seren both have **Stone Shape**, and the DM allowed that two castings would probably open the rubble, based on what the crab familiar saw. Lyrion: if they go through, perhaps without the Stoneforges' involvement — *"I haven't met anyone since coming to this city that I particularly trust. No offense."* (To Dura. Twice.) Bell thinks Baldrin honestly wants it solved; Seren found his welcome chilly given how eager Victor Barun had been; Eldrin, the better judge of character: *"as trustworthy as he could be to someone that he didn't fully [trust] — we are strangers to him, brother."* Dura, who works for the man through his estate without reporting to him: stoic, and by everything she's heard from miners and her own family, someone who *"cares about the well-being of his workers"* — *"take my opinion with a grain of salt."* Consensus: talk to him first.

### Winter in the cavern

The streets were **very cold** and nearly empty: with the temperature down, people were using the spaces in front of their homes as **cold storage** — jars, boxes, chests set outside. Only flurries get through the cracks, but the cave is so humid that **ice forms around the ponds and basins**; there's no way to heat the cavern, so homes run on fireplaces, people stay in through the deep winter, and after the first major snow everyone stocks up. Dura: *"I get sick very often down here."* The Thalors remembered their mother forcing coats on them and the game of **who could go longest without putting it back on** — Eldrin always won, and kept his off a few extra minutes to rub it in. Seren tested her **Dwarvish** on Dura (*"a little bit"* of an accent — *"It's very good"*) and ruled that the brothers' Common has *"more of an affectation"* than an accent. Lyrion blushed. Thorn: *"Get a room."* Seren: *"We have one!"*

### The Stoneforge estate, and Gwynn

The estate: a rock garden for a front lawn, lights on, fireplace lit. Seren knocked, and the door opened on **[[Gwynn (Stoneforge Headmaid)|Gwynn, the headmaid]]** — *"Seren!"* — who knew her on sight and answered in Dwarvish: [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] was already at work in the mines. *"You still have that writ I gave you? I need to update it for today."* Rhaenyra produced the [[Mine Pass|mine writ]]; Gwynn stepped away and came back with **Dura's name added and the date refreshed**, and directions: show it at the mine check-in, ask for Baldrin. *"Have a good day — be safe."* Eldrin called *"Safe travels!"* after a woman who was not going anywhere, and was patted on the arm.

At the mine entrance (busy as ever, a queue at check-in), Seren squeezed Lyrion's hand: *"Is it strange that I'm very reluctant to go in there?"* — *"Given what we've experienced, no. But when all of us are together there are very few things that truly scare me."* (Centipedes. Dura was asked not to answer whether the mine has centipedes, and didn't answer fast enough.)

### Baldrin's office — ten thousand gold

Past the great central chasm the miners use to reach the lower levels, into an administrative corridor; first door, a knock, *"Come in."* **[[Baldrin Stoneforge]]'s office**: a fireplace, bookcases of financial reports and hand-bound ledgers, nothing decorative except one thing — **an old, rusted pickaxe head mounted on a wooden plaque above the fire**. The older dwarf, glasses off, in his work clothes: *"Just the people I was actually looking for. Shut the door behind you."*

His proposition, in full: he's convinced *"there is something more afoot than just a cave-in,"* and with the guard taking heavy casualties from the city's disappearances and murders (*"no offense, Dura"*), he won't stretch them further. His crews have made significant headway on the collapse — **enough rubble left as a stopper** so nothing inside gets out, **several guards posted at it**, and **a gap cleared large enough for the party to use as an entrance**. He wants the section beyond **investigated and made safe** for his workers; finding the source would be *"greatly appreciated"*; safety is the point. Compensation: **up to ten thousand gold.** Thorn choked on nothing. He'd like them to start as soon as they can; he is their point of contact, with **message runners** available (trustworthy, he says); *"Dura — make sure you let your captain know."* Seren looked around the table, found only nods — Lyrion: *"Our recent business takes us into these mines anyway, so may as well get paid for it"* — and shook on it: *"Sounds like we have a deal, Baldrin."*

On the way out Lyrion asked about the pickaxe. Not an heirloom — **Baldrin's own first pickaxe**, from the day he started in the mines; his family owned them before him, but he *"worked my way up from the very bottom of the chain,"* breaking stone with the other miners for ten or fifteen years before learning the business. Lyrion, with permission (*"Careful with it! It's old."*), **inspected it against the murder weapons**: only the head, dull and rusted from use and the cave's humidity — **any maker's mark or branding is lost under the rust**. He put it back with a smile and a nod.

### Down to the breach

Hallway morale: Lyrion, hand on Dura's shoulder — *"Are you ready to experience psychological horrors potentially beyond your comprehension?"* — *"I don't think it's gonna be that crazy."* — *"Sweet summer child. We never think it's going to be that crazy. And then you see an undead aboleth crawl out of an ancient well…"* Rhaenyra looked nervous for the first time. Dura: *"Regardless, I was stationed here to watch over you all."* (Thorn wants her cut in; Lyrion probed for the price — **five gold to test a potion** that would, at worst, upset her stomach? *"Not right now. Maybe if you get me drunk enough. At least buy me a beer first."* Thorn: *"I have done a lot worse for a lot less."* Dura: *"I didn't know you were that kind of —"*) Mike, out loud at the check-in desk: *"So if we do die, everyone dies"* — covered by Eldrin's deception check ("he's talking about his gas") and an elbow to the ribs.

A mine worker — an office type, not a laborer — walked them down the branching passages and left them at the last fork: *"follow the left path, there's no more branches, it's a straight shot."* Lanterns every sixty to a hundred feet, pools of dark between; Mike lit his lantern, Seren cast **Light on [[Bell]]'s pendant**.

And on the walk, in **Undercommon** — Lyrion's least favorite language — Eldrin dropped back beside his brother: *"Do you think mom would have liked Rhaenyra?"* — *"Mom liked everyone, Eldrin."* — *"Not just her. Her for me."* Back in [[Roseview]], Eldrin told him, **[[Thalrin]] had asked him to protect his daughter**; Eldrin said he would with his life; Thalrin asked if he was serious, and the conversation that followed gave Eldrin what felt like **his blessing** — Rhaenyra had already talked to her father about him. With no one else to ask, Eldrin wanted to know what their mother would have thought. Lyrion, eyes very wide, switching back to Common and putting a hand on his shoulder: *"Mother and I have both only ever wanted for your happiness. I know that is often what you take in the least regard. So if this brings you happiness, then yes — it is what Mother and I would both want for you."* Eldrin hasn't been open about it — *"having a private room has given some things away"* — and there'd been no good moment: *"The world has literally been on fire. Dragons. You almost died."* — *"I did die."* — *"I'm not accepting that you died, because we brought you back."* Rhaenyra glanced back over her shoulder and smiled; Eldrin gave her a thumbs up. (And the potion Lyrion keeps trying to get someone to drink? *"This is actually the [[Truth Serum (Lyrion)|truth serum]] that I picked up at a vast discount a while back"* — the kind that *"could kill someone"*; Eldrin suggested pouring it in Mike's mead.)

### The bloodied rubble

They rounded the last corner to the cave-in: **partially cleared — and no guards.** Several were supposed to be here. *"Don't be suspicious, don't be suspicious."* Seren cast **Detect Good and Evil**: no creature of any such type within thirty feet — but a **faint pull toward the rubble**, a lingering sense of an **aberration**, *"the same creature type that I sensed in the basement of the manor."*

The rocks were wet. **Blood** — and a **second red liquid, not blood, not mixing with the blood**. Thorn poked it: it stretched when she pulled her finger back and **snapped back to the rock**, elastic, viscous, snot- or snail-like, not acidic, nearly odorless but for a hint of iron that might just be the blood. Lyrion's first thought was the slime that coated everything under Fort Elm; the DM: no — that was foamy, this is something else. The blood was **fresh, not yet dried**, and in *"somebody died here"* quantities; and the marks around the gap told the story — **the guards had been seized and dragged through the rubble** to the other side. Seren: *"Wherever the guards are, they may still be alive."* Thorn: *"Urgent, then."*

The opening is five and a half, six feet tall — Mike (eight feet) and Bell must crouch and squeeze through the slime. Order of march: **Seren first** (*"ladies first"*), **Dura right behind her** (to Rhaenyra: *"is this how the group usually is?"* — *"Yes it is"*), then **Rhaenyra**; **Lyrion in the rear** to pull Mike and Bell through if they stick; and Bell, remembering the last time she was last through a doorway (paralyzed, mind-controlled), with Thorn's ruling: *"so you don't go last."* Seren squeezed into the gap. **The session ended with the party going through the bloodied, slime-coated breach** — into the dark where the guards went.

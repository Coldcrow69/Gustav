## Recap

### "Mike — Lyrion is dead. Get him to Seren!"

The session opened where 33 ended: [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] dead on the kitchen floor of the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]], the ten-round Revivify clock running. [[Bell]] screamed his name loud enough that [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] heard it two floors down — panic, not a war cry — and turned to relay it to [[Seren Moss|Seren]], deep in the tunnel below: *"Something is wrong with Lyrion. We have to go."* Seren sprinted back for the ladder.

Upstairs, Bell answered the killing blow in kind: her greataxe **cleaved the sword-cultist in two, neck to armpit**, then she shoved the [[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|robed mage]] bodily into a corner and bellowed the order that organized the round: **"Mike — Lyrion is dead. Get him to Seren!"** A cultist fanatic carved her with a necrotic-laced blade; her eyes glowed dark green. The mage, recovering, **turned invisible** in his corner.

[[Mike]] fought through, hoisted Lyrion's body, and ran. Bell **grappled the cultist blocking his path** and dragged the man bodily aside — until a cultist spellcaster spent **his last spell of the day on Command ("Drop!")** and forced her to release him. *(The Archivist summary pins the Command on the mage — wrong; it was a rank-and-file caster emptying his tank. The mage still had much worse in reserve.)* Mike handed the body off to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], who — over a spirited table argument about carry weight, settled by STR 18 and a lifetime of carrying his 180-lb little brother — **Misty Stepped to the basement stairs with the corpse over his shoulder**, on the RAW ruling that a corpse is an object, not a creature.

The invisible mage made him pay for it: **three wand bolts, 52 damage in a single turn** (one max-rolled, one crit), invisibility dropping as he fired. Eldrin stayed on his feet — *"Not dead yet! It's just a flesh wound"* — and Seren, arriving at the edge of sight but out of reach, threw him **11 points of Channel Divinity healing at range**. Eldrin barred the basement door, laid his brother down, and told her: *"Whatever comes through this door — I need you to heal my brother."*

### Meanwhile, at the tavern

A brief rewind to initiative count zero: at [[Good For What Ails Ya]], [[Thorn]] **hid the recovered [[Dragon Eggs|dragon egg]] in a chest in the room's closet and covered it with an illusion**, then told [[Rhaenyra]]: *"If you want to go join them at the manor — I have a bad feeling. I think I can watch this by myself now."* Rhaenyra went. (The in-fiction release that returns Miranda's PC to the table.)

### The five-carat casting

**[[Seren Moss|Seren]] cast Revivify.** The [[Powerful Diamond|five-carat diamond]] lit from within with silver light, threw prisms across the basement, and **shattered into wisps** — consumed.

And Lyrion, for a short true moment, was **dead**: rolling hills of yellow grass under a clear sky, warmth like a held breath, and in the distance **a woman he knew — [[Endra Thalor|his mother]]**. Behind him, faintly: Eldrin screaming *"GET UP, DAMMIT!"*, and Seren — *"please come back to me if you can."* He looked at the distant figure and answered the living: *"I'll be back one day… and we'll all be together again."* Then he turned around.

He woke gasping at 1 HP — *"I need a vacation"* — to Seren bursting into tears and Eldrin's hand on his cheek pouring in a Cure Wounds: **"Don't you ever fucking do that again."** *("I don't think I've ever been as scared. And that's saying something.")* Lyrion's first coherent questions were about the mage's **wand** — *"Did he drop it anywhere? Is he still alive?"* — and he re-cast Mage Armor on himself (it had been riding on Seren and dropped the moment he died). The revival left him **5 of 6 exhaustion levels deep** — one step from death by fatigue.

### Bell and the whispers

Upstairs, Bell put her greataxe **straight down through the second fanatic's skull** — and as he died, **whispers began at the edge of her mind**. Rhaenyra arrived through the front door, unshielded her [[Everbright Lantern]] over a foyer of corpses, and **wild-shaped into a saber-toothed tiger**; she hamstrung the last cultist, was shaken off and slashed, then **shoved him prone and tore his throat out** — head separated from body. The manor's rank-and-file were done.

The whispers were not. They sharpened into words — ***"Hunt! Kill! Murderer! Hunt!"*** — and Bell **dropped her greataxe on the floor** and, for the first time ever, extended the **claws of her bestial rage-form** and used them as weapons, savaging the already-dying cultist. The tiger's animal instincts screamed that the shadow-wreathed thing in front of her was **more dangerous than she was**. Then Bell **failed a save, and the [[Loa]] took her**: *"Bell, you see a target in front of you."* Two claw strikes, ~19 damage, into [[Rhaenyra]]'s tiger form. Rhaenyra disengaged, leapt to the front doorway, and let out **the loudest roar Stonewater's cliffs have heard** — a call for help.

The next round Bell saw Mike — a friend, no enemies in reach — made her save with advantage, and **the frenzy receded** to the back of her skull. She picked up her axe. *"Did you get Lyrion to Seren?"* Mike: *"Yes — please don't attack us."* Her apology to Rhaenyra was a promise: *"We're going to have to figure out what triggered that and how to combat it going forward. That was not good."* She kept her rage deliberately extended until the mage was accounted for.

### Hunting the invisible man

In the cellar, Mike hunted the re-invisible mage by demolition: a Radiant Sunbolt **shattered an ale barrel** — and his second bolt **struck the invisible man standing in front of it**, the mage's reflexive Shield betraying his position. Roughly 23 damage went into him across the exchange. The mage's answer was the session's hardest hit: **Cone of Cold**. Seren and Eldrin saved and took 20; **Mike failed, took the full 40, and went down frozen half-solid, icicles forming on his back**. He failed a death save before **Seren pumped 29 points of healing into him** and he cracked the ice off his own shoulders; she followed with Channel Divinity to hobble the mage's next rolls.

Lyrion — dead ten minutes ago, exhausted to the edge of the grave — walked back into the fight and threw a **Fireball**. The mage **Counterspelled it** (after a long table rules-debate, the 2024 one-slotted-spell-per-turn ruling stood: no counter-counterspell). [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]]'s **Dissonant Whispers** (13 psychic) burned the mage's reaction and pinned him in place; the mage answered by fleeing **up the stairs**, casting on himself as he went — and his footsteps simply stopped. Lyrion tracked the sound, guessed, and put his **second Fireball into the room at the top of the stairs**: no discernible hit — and the **house caught fire**.

Somewhere above, Rhaenyra (dropped back to her own shape) heard **a door shut on the second floor**; then two more sets of doors opened and closed; then nothing. Combat ended with the mage **unaccounted for**.

### Firefighting and reckoning

The party beat the fire before it took the manor: Lyrion held the spread with **Control Flames**, Bell and Mike smothered rooms with floor-runners, Bell hauled kitchen water by the bucket. The building ended **charred but standing**.

Then the news went around. Seren reported the cave below: a **dug-out tunnel connecting to what looks like an existing mineshaft** — and, down in that cave, **the singed crates the dragon eggs had been kept in, empty**. The remaining eggs are **gone**, presumably carried down the tunnel — the party's suspect: the fled [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]]. Bell connected the tunnel to the **dug-out cavern behind the mine cave-in** — *"not dug by Baldrin's people."* Seren restated the cult's landlord claim — permission to occupy granted by **[[Kassadren]] Greybraid** — and argued the thing had outgrown them: **involve the city authorities before the eggs leave Stonewater**. Eldrin, Mike, and Lyrion agreed. *"We've seen what happens when we fucking break up."*

### Searching the manor

- **Basement:** every barrel checked — ale, no eggs. On a table, a **singed scroll case** holding a **[[Map of the Mines (Cliffside Mansion)|copied map of the mines]]** — with a **cavernous space drawn in and a crude path running from the mines to this manor**. It matches what the party has seen beyond the collapse. Eldrin: *"I think we may have found who was behind the tunnel behind the collapse."* [[Gustav]] **copied the map** (~2 minutes) so the party keeps one if the authorities take the original.
- **Second floor** (swept together, [[Rhaenyra]] running Detect Magic): **two locked, magical, dragon-egg-sized treasure boxes** that resist forcing — **[[Mike]] carries both** "just to be safe." A room of heavy chests and a closet of traveling supplies and **cultists' robes**. A Detect Magic ping behind a wall revealed a **swivel bookshelf** hiding a room of further chests.
- **The money:** one chest held sacks of gold — **at least 5,000–6,000 gp at a glance**. Seren argued against taking it (*"anything we take might be used against us"* on the way to the guard); Rhaenyra floated half; Mike proposed holding it all and surrendering it if asked. **Undecided at session end.**
- **The [[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|journal]]:** a chest of personal effects held an unnamed **journal** — early pages of raw dread about death and being forgotten, like a therapy diary; then the writer finds **a support group of people with the same mortality-terror**; then the writing **dissolves into fanaticism**: *"you are only truly dead when you are forgotten."* The writer traveled **from the north down into the wilds on a mission from this community to recover something in [[Stonewater]]**. Lyrion's read: *"I don't think death was their fear anymore… perhaps all they're out to do now is something so extreme that they'll never be forgotten. That would be their immortality in their eyes. Something that perhaps involves powerful artifacts… dragon eggs."* (And, deadpan: *"Father always said that therapy was for hacks. Perhaps he was on to something?"*)
- Also recovered: a blank leather-bound book, a small coin pouch — and a **second, magical [[Arcane Scroll Case (Cliffside Mansion)|scroll case]]** in a script Eldrin couldn't read (*"looks like something your brother would have written"*). He wrapped it up for Lyrion. **Unopened.**
- **The balcony:** Rhaenyra found a room whose balcony door hung **slightly ajar** — the whole city calm and glittering below, a 20–30 ft drop, and **no impressions in the mud, no disturbed foliage**. If the mage went over, he left no mark. The table's darker joke: he never left.

The session ended mid-sweep (Chase had to leave) — all six present party members alive, the manor smoldering, the mage a hole in the world.

## Recap

The party had slept off their travel injuries at [[Good For What Ails Ya]] and convened over breakfast to plan the day. Their leads, as the DM recapped: the stolen dragon eggs, with the [[Greybraids]] dwarf clan the prime suspects (one of the egg-thieves wore the clan's insignia on his cloak), and a standing morning meeting with [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] to be shown the [[Ironheart Mines]] cave-in. They had floated splitting up — some to the mines, some to approach the Greybraids — and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] had stepped into the alley to talk (the [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-17|Session 30]] reconciliation). The session opens there: the two brothers rounding the corner onto a bloodied scene.

A [[Dwarven Woman (Stonewater Pickaxe Body)|dwarven woman]] knelt sobbing over a male dwarf's body, a pickaxe buried in its skull. Investigating together (rolling well), the brothers saw no one fleeing, but Lyrion noted two things: the victim's knife was gone — the sheath on his hip empty — and a separate pool of **[[Black Ichor|black ichor]]** mixed into the blood. He dipped a finger and smelled it: iron, like blood, but old, as if it had been decomposing a while. The woman pointed down the alley — *"He went that way"* — describing the assailant only as a dwarf in a black cloak whose face she hadn't seen. Eldrin gave chase; Lyrion stayed with the body. The scene showed signs of a struggle, crates and trash cans knocked over. Examining the corpse, Lyrion found slashes and puncture wounds to the thigh and the killing blow through the eye, the pickaxe still embedded — and the weapon bore the **[[Stoneforge Clan]] insignia**, the same crest he had seen around [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s house.

Using his hunter's tracking (a skill outlasting the Ranger levels he traded for Bard), Eldrin followed a trail of black-ichor droplets that, unnaturally, never changed size — neither clotting nor bleeding out. It led to a hinged **[[Stonewater Sewers|sewer grate]]** in the alley, where the trail abruptly stopped. He pried the grate open, smelled rot, and saw a ladder and, below, a larger pool of ichor leading around a corner. Peering around it, he heard metal grinding on stone and then a wooden door shut. Catching himself doing exactly what he'd scolded Lyrion for, he climbed back out, noted the grate's location, and headed back toward the tavern.

Lyrion judged the victim beyond ordinary healing (a revivify-level effort at best) and sent the distraught woman — the victim's wife — through the alleys to fetch [[Seren Moss|Seren]] at [[Good For What Ails Ya]]. Seren was upstairs, mid-disguise at [[Thorn]]'s hands (the Session 30 plan to approach the Greybraids unseen); [[Rhaenyra]] ran up to get her, and she hurried down half-finished — only her coloring changed, off her usual blonde, in a borrowed dress — with [[Mike]] (comically convinced they were hunting a plant called "Moss") trailing behind. By then the city guard had reached the alley, wearing tabards in colors the party didn't recognize — not [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s [[Stoneforge Clan]] and not the [[Greybraids]]. They questioned Lyrion (who wiped the ichor off on a guard's tabard and Prestidigitated himself clean) and sat him against a wall while they secured the scene. Returning, Eldrin was intercepted by a guard and escorted to his brother.

Seren reached the body. She produced a five-carat diamond to attempt a restoration, but the man was beyond its window — the magic only works within a minute of death — and she put it away, telling the widow she was sorry she hadn't been quick enough. With the grieving woman led away out of sight, Seren cast **Speak with Dead**, and a pale-blue apparition rose from the corpse. Over five questions she learned: the man was a **survivor of the [[Ironheart Mines]] cave-in**; his killer was a dwarf named *"Victor"* whose **skin was rotting**; and yes, during the cave-in he had seen something someone might want to silence him for — *"There was… people in the cave. I didn't see much. We ran."* Eldrin worried aloud the name might mean [[Victor Barun]]; Seren pointed out that this Victor "ended up being a dwarf," ruling that out.

Before more could be asked, the lead guard stopped them with a revelation. The perpetrator should already have been dealt with: the **Stoneforge Guard** had cornered and killed a prime suspect in the killings — **[[Victor Stonehammer]]** — but his body had **rapidly decomposed** after he went down, and now he seemed to have returned, recognized by someone at the scene. Lyrion and Seren exchanged a silent *"necromancy,"* and Lyrion told the guard plainly that they had a necromancer problem. The guard said he would report to his captain and reopen the investigation, inviting them to the third-tier Barracks with anything further. As the questioning wrapped, the guard who had been eavesdropping — a woman — quietly slipped away, following the blood trail.

Eldrin pulled Lyrion aside and admitted he hadn't been fully honest: he'd found the sewer, and now the lady guard had gone down the same trail and he feared she'd get herself killed. They resolved to follow and protect her. Eldrin drew the rest of the party off from the crowd with a pretext about "a really cool store," and they circled back to the grate — propped open, though Eldrin had shut it, meaning the guard had already descended. They climbed down. Lyrion, realizing they were skipping the Baldrin meeting, framed it as an in-and-out, twenty-minute detour. Seren — without her shield or armor under the disguise — took **Mage Armor** from Lyrion; Mike lit his lantern; Bell handed Mike a healing potion as they advanced.

They caught up just as the lady guard opened a wooden oak door and recoiled. A cloaked figure emerged, dragging a stone pickaxe, and brought it down at her. Eldrin's **Phantasmal Killer** and Seren's **Sanctuary** on the guard bought her an opening, and she disengaged to go for reinforcements. The creature loosed a blood-curdling scream and marked Bell — *"You will not stop me from my prey."* When Mike's flaming strike forced the cloak up, the party saw it clearly: a rotting dwarf's face, the beard patchy and chunked out, a large **rock embedded in its skull**, purple bludgeoning across the skin — and under the cloak no real structure at all, just rotting flesh. Studied in undeath as a cleric of [[Kelemvor]], Seren named it: a **revenant**, a vengeful undead that won't rest until its vengeance is sated. She revised the party's necromancer theory — this thing wasn't summoned and controlled, it was acting on its own (which Lyrion's later Detect Magic, finding no magic in the lair, would confirm).

A revenant is hard to end: a powerful cleric can Banish it outright; otherwise it must be destroyed and a banishment ritual cast in its final moments, or it simply regenerates and returns. Neither Seren nor Lyrion (an Order of Scribes wizard who also knows Banishment) had the spell prepared — they had not planned for a fight that day — so they moved to contain it. Seren's **Turn Undead** left it incapacitated (it was immune to fear, but not to that); Bell grappled and shoved it prone; the party bound its hands and legs with rope and covered its eyes, even as its flesh visibly reconstituted. Lyrion peeked into its den — a former sewer utility closet holding lumber, stone, a bucket of dried mortar, rat carcasses, and a gnawed **[[Severed Dwarven Torso|dwarven torso]]** (no limbs, dead at least a week). [[Rhaenyra]] had **Stone Shape** prepared and grew a **[[Chained Stone Coffin|stone coffin with a lid]]** around the bound creature; they chained the lid down and spiked the chain to the floor — buying time, though everyone understood it would eventually break free.

They sent Mike, the fastest, to find a cleric who could Banish it. He sprinted to the green-domed **[[Temple of Selûne (Stonewater)|Temple of Selûne]]** — which doubles as the city library — and, after some confusion over the word "banish," was taken to an **[[Haceran Leadbreaker|elderly dwarf cleric]]** (she gives her name — Haceran Leadbreaker — in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]). She couldn't cast Banishment that day either; she searched a heavy tome of the city's registered clerics for anyone nearby who could, found no immediate option, and chose to come see the creature herself. Mike scooped her up, went giant-form, and ran her back.

The guard captain and reinforcements arrived. The captain — who had earlier told them to stay out of trouble — thanked them for saving his guard, and confirmed that the murders all showed puncture wounds and that the victims traced back to the mines rather than to any single clan. Seren explained the revenant and volunteered to perform the banishment herself the next day if no one else could, urging them to reinforce the containment meanwhile and noting the party had been sent to [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] anyway and would speak with him. The DM-canon the party pieced together: a revenant is a **possessing spirit** that does not need the body it died in — it can inhabit any corpse and, over time, morph it to resemble its original self. So the thing "likely is" Victor Stonehammer by appearance, but, since the original was already killed, this is *not* his original body — just a cadaver wearing his face.

Inside the coffin, the ropes snapped; then came not pounding but the wet sound of flesh on flesh, and then silence. With no Banishment available — the elderly cleric and Seren both lacked it for the day, and there was no temple of [[Kelemvor]] in the city — Lyrion readied **Remove Curse** (which requires a touch) for the moment the lid opened. They undid the chains and lifted it — to find no body at all. Whatever bound the rotting flesh to bone had completely dissolved, leaving a coffin of liquid. Not de-animated. **Moving.** The session ended there.

## Open threads

- **The revenant of [[Victor Stonehammer]]** — its host body dissolved into a moving liquid inside the [[Chained Stone Coffin]]; contained but **not ended**. Still requires Banishment (or a satisfied vengeance) to destroy. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] has volunteered to attempt the ritual the next day.
- **Why is Victor Stonehammer killing the cave-in survivors?** — the party's working theory (unconfirmed): he was a miner **left to die in the cave-in**, now taking vengeance on those who escaped without him.
- **What did the miners see in the cave?** — the victim's last words were *"people in the cave."* Whatever the survivors are being silenced over is unknown, and may connect to the cause of the cave-in.
- **The murder weapon's [[Stoneforge Clan]] insignia** — the pickaxe is [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s clan's, and the **Stoneforge Guard** were the ones who first cornered and "killed" Victor Stonehammer. Baldrin's possible involvement remains flagged.

- **The unfamiliar city guard** — a force in colors the party didn't recognize (not [[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge]], not [[Greybraids]]), based at the **third-tier Barracks**.
- **Mine pass / Baldrin meeting** — skipped to chase the revenant; the [[Mine Pass|mine pass]] is still uncollected and the cave-in visit still pending.

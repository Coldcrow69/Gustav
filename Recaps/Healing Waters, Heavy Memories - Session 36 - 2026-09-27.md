## Recap

### Off-screen, between sessions

Two bits of business resolved at the top: the party **bought three diamonds at 300 gp each** — Revivify fuel, *"if it ever comes down to that again"* (Lyrion: *"It probably will"*) — and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s scroll-barter for the 7,000 gp **Potion of Vitality** at [[Ichor's Potion Explorium]] **failed**: the price gap was more than his scrolls could cover. He gave the theft of it "not so brief and only a little bit half-hearted" thought and did not. [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s follow-up — any shop selling **utility potions** (she wanted Water Breathing, "for shenanigans")? — got the city's answer: no; nothing of the sort is brewed here, and the party would have to go **east, deeper into the wilds**, to find it. ([[Rhaenyra]], it emerged, knows Water Breathing anyway.) Lyrion means to spend his soak studying the [[Scroll of Chain Lightning]].

### The climb to Raise Your Spirits

The party took the **northern staircase** up — avoiding the manor — passing the [[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|Greybraid Manor]], the Greybraid forge, and the **Stoneforge estate** on the way to the city's highest tier: a cliff-top **plateau holding a lake**, the whole of [[Stonewater]] spread out below and the manor visible in the distance. Lyrion, still four levels of exhaustion deep, took the stairs like a man on his last hit points and slumped through the door of a large stone building — dark timber framing, circular roofs of purplish tile, non-native greenery, a wide patio. The sign: **[[Raise Your Spirits]]**.

The owner met them at the reception desk — **[[Tiamor Runegrip]]**, a striking, impeccably kept dwarven woman with long red hair in two braids down her back (no beard — the table checked). She sat Lyrion on a bench (*"You look tired, son."* / *"Physically, no. Mentally, also no."*) and sold the party **the works: 30 gp a head, 210 gp for seven** — Lyrion paid from his own purse — an itinerary of hot-stone massage, charcoal-paste body massage, rock-salt cleanse, mud bath, the **Stonewater Plunge**, the hot springs, rock-needle acupuncture, and an **open bar**. No mead (*"This is bullshit"* — Eldrin, later), but house spirits from their own distillery. [[Dura]] declined admission — *"I think I would get scolded if I partook"* — and spent the day on a bench outside the treatment rooms with a book. Pressed on where the party was from, the table went silent long enough to be a bit; Seren offered Fort Elm; Lyrion offered *"a traveling troupe of sorts, a carnival if you will,"* and Tiamor's ears pricked up.

### The treatments (and the bar)

The spa isn't built for seven, so the party split into two rotations — [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Mike]], and [[Thorn]]; [[Bell]], [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], and [[Rhaenyra]] — gear locked in changing-room trunks ([[Dragon Eggs|the egg]] included, in Mike's backpack, on the theory that a man carrying a backpack through a mud bath draws more attention than a locked trunk). Mike and Bell needed two massage tables pushed end to end. The drinks menu, for the record: **Rockbiter Whiskey** (dark, smoky honey — not from honeybees), **Fairy's Breath** (mint vodka), **Goodberry Gin** (raspberry-ish, dangerously smooth, *heals 1 HP*), **Willow Whiskey** (cinnamon — the table's verdict: *"It's fucking Fireball"*), **Cold Gin** (charcoal-filtered), and an imported **[[Silvermire]] rum** (fruity, coconut — a flavor Mike had never met). Mike ordered two shots of the strongest thing (*"Are any of these made of stars?"*) and carried them to Lyrion, who had asked for *"something that tastes like stardust"*; one cinnamon shot was enough. Thorn got the bartender to build her a **piña colada** — pineapple wedge, tiny umbrella — and named it herself.

Seren was the spa's one skeptic: baffled by the premise, uncomfortable being waited on, uncomfortable being touched by strangers, going along with every treatment *because they'd already paid*. Lyrion, moaning on a granite-heated table: *"Would you deprive yourself of this? It's really wonderful."* Her position by the end: new experiences are nice, but next time she'll skip straight to the springs.

In the charcoal room (Bell, Rhaenyra, and Eldrin all in the other rotation), Lyrion asked seriously what had happened with **Bell's spirit** at the manor. Seren's read: Bell **lost control of the [[Zehal|Loa]] in a moment of heightened emotion**, as she had once before around the time the party first met Mike — hard to call her back from. Lyrion wanted to help and, newly humbled, admitted he didn't know that anyone but Bell could: *"We could ask God about it later"* (his Divination — a ten-minute ritual).

### The mud bath — mothers

A spa attendant delivered the lore with the towels: when the city was founded, before the water system was built, miners cleaned the dust off in **pools of mud** — "mixed results," but people found it relaxing, and a tradition was born. Lyrion walked in holding [[Gustav]] and **submerged the two of them** completely, a mud monster rising; Eldrin dunked his own head and surfaced with *"Lyrion, I'm a tree."* *"Fuck off, Eldrin."*

Then the brothers, chest-deep in warm mud, remembered **a childhood storm**: their mother going out afterward to harvest something that only bloomed after rain (mushrooms); Lyrion, not yet able to Prestidigitate, tripping Eldrin with a tiny Mage Hand, pushing their father in after him, and jumping in himself; [[Endra Thalor|Mother]] furious — at Eldrin, the "problem child," whom Lyrion happily let take the blame. (*"I just got caught for all of your shit!"*) The smile dropped: *"I saw her. When I was on the other side. She was there."* — *"Is she okay?"* — *"I don't know, Eldrin. She's dead."* And their father? Lyrion laughed before he answered: *"No. Thankfully — or unfortunately — not. I'm not sure which."* Eldrin: *"Either he's not dead, or maybe the afterlife is a place worth going to."*

The question went around the pool. **Rhaenyra** remembers her mother in pieces — the way the sun came through her hair — and doesn't know whether she's alive. **Thorn** was **raised in a carnival and they all died**; she never knew her birth mother — her parents *"just left me on the road."* **Mike** (a goliath, the DM confirmed) knew his parents and had a normal enough childhood, but was signed to an apprenticeship as soon as he could use his hands and spent his youth with his master — and nothing he made was ever good enough for them. **Bell** knows who gave birth to her, but firbolgs are raised by the clan as a whole — *"you guys have also met what's left of where I came from."* **Seren**'s family is lovely; her mother essentially pushed her out the door to adventure. And **Eldrin**, asked what he'd actually wanted from life: *"Raise a couple of kids. A quiet, peaceful life."* At least two — a child should know what it is to have a sibling. Seren, to Lyrion: *"Do you not want children?"* — *"I suppose I'd never thought of it,"* and the table watched him think about it for a long second.

Side-lore from the same soak: elves aren't good at relaxing — the nearest thing the Thalors' home had was **story night**, elders passing wine and telling tales, and **[[Elder Felryn]]** who swore he'd slain **three dragons bigger than the biggest trees** single-handed. (*"Eldrin, I shudder to think of the tales we might have to tell at Elder Night if we ever return home."* / *"They wouldn't believe us."*) Mike's people relax by making things. Eldrin's first-deer rite, raw: the eye, a bite of the heart, a bite of the liver — *"everyone did it, that wasn't even just a my-father-crazy thing."* Thorn walked away without a word.

### The springs, the plunge, and the thing in the lake

The hot springs are tiered natural pools against the cave wall, up to six feet deep, high enough to look down on the whole city with flurries of snow drifting across it from the cracks in the cavern roof. Lyrion, floating: *"This is far more beautiful a cave than I ever thought I would find."* Close to the shore of the lake, in lantern light, what looks like pointed rock resolves into **several enormous ribs**. A spa employee gave the city's answer: the skeleton has been there **since Stonewater's founding and probably long before**; the **scholars at the college in the upper tiers** believe it belonged to a **gargantuan beast of these mountains from before the [[Glyph Scar]]**, never identified; its minerals and calcium feed the lake and account for the water's health benefits. Rhaenyra: *"Almost like a guardian of the water."*

In the water, Lyrion thanked Seren properly for his life — *"I don't think I would be, if it wasn't for you now, maybe more than once"* — called her spellwork impressive, and when she deflected to [[Kelemvor]] doing the heavy lifting, tried a line (*"you look so incredible while you do it"*), then confessed: *"Does that line work? Eldrin told me I should try. I do mean it though, for the record."* Seren sank to her nose in the water, blushing. **The DM noted for the record that Lyrion still has a scar on his chest** — the Revivify didn't erase the sword.

The **Stonewater Plunge** — a fenced stair down into the lake itself — was ice. Seren jumped first, teeth chattering; Rhaenyra dipped a hoof and declared it done; Gustav, awake for the first time all day, gave Lyrion a look that said *do not* — and **Eldrin's Mage Hand shoved his brother in** (the childhood debt repaid; Eldrin jumped after). Something brushed Lyrion's leg; a small fish darted off. Eldrin, on their father: winter river plunges were *"the only way I was going to be a real man"* — once he stayed in thirty-six minutes, turned so blue his toes stopped working, and Mother was furious at Father. Only Lyrion took the **rock-needle acupuncture** — "designed for dwarves, and you are but a delicate elf" — and strutted out claiming it hadn't hurt. Mike and Lyrion came out **noticeably rejuvenated**, if not yet mechanically restored (*"a one rejuvenated, but after some sleep you'll feel a lot better"*).

### Tiamor's office

Before they left, Tiamor asked for a word in her office — a humble round room of ledgers. She wants **entertainment for a livelier event in a week or two**: *"the upper echelon"* — **visitors from [[Silvermire]], and the city council**. Thorn pointed at the troupe's assets (Eldrin: *"I'm a bard"*; the blue one does magic tricks; she dances — with fire, lately; the rest, *"we haven't figured that out yet"*). Pay: no budget set, but *"rather well-paying"* given the clientele, plus — Lyrion negotiating from the doorway — a free set of treatments. Tiamor asked for a firm yes or no in a few days; Thorn, in the hallway: *"Guys — we have our first gig!"*

Bell asked her about the **pickaxe murders**. Tiamor's **eldest sister is the matriarch of the [[Runegrip Clan]]**, and she's been stressed — civil unrest, people scared; the missing-then-found dead come from **different clans, Runegrip among them**. Tiamor keeps out of politics (*"each clan leader has to deal with their own guard"* — each section of the city gets a guard on a rotating schedule) and works "purely on a social level," but she'd already heard through her contacts that a group had been sent to help, and she **offered to drum up information** — *"when people are feeling relaxed, they talk."* Seren, gesturing at Dura, confirmed the guard knows what they're working on.

The hallway afterward produced a reckoning: Eldrin asked what, exactly, the party is being **paid** for all this. Answer: the [[Writ of Deputization|deputization]] came with no money; [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s hire was never priced (the DM: no terms, because the scale was unknown — and then the manor happened). Lyrion: *"Somebody had better pay us for our other project. I don't know who yet. But somebody."* Eldrin: *"I think they pay us by letting us live."*

### Pie, the Sour Jackal, and a toast

Lyrion put a hand on Dura's armored shoulder — *"Come get fucking plastered with us"* — and she allowed that she's off the clock in a few hours but never really off duty; her relief comes later in the night. Asked for dinner, she led them to **her regular: a pie shop in the second district** — a hatch in the wall, one kind of pie (meat, potatoes, carrots, veal), miners shoveling it in on the walk home. Dura whistled up the order, **paid**, and took three for herself.

Then to the **Clock Tower District**, "surprisingly close to where you guys bought that diamond," and **[[Sour Jackal|the Sour Jackal]]** — a jackal on its hind legs holding a pint on the sign, rowdier and fuller than Good For What Ails Ya, run by an **elf of a kind none of them had seen**: dark-complexioned, natural flowing markings across his arms and chest. Seren's history check placed him — a desert elf from the **[[Sunscarred Expanse]]**, the region north above the wilds, known to Fort Elm only through traders' stories. His name is **[[Haldur]]**. He had four rooms; the party took **all four, ten nights, 20 gp** (Eldrin, from the manor fund): [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] and [[Rhaenyra]] in one — *"I pat my little mud spot next to me for you, babe"* had already told the table what it needed to know — [[Seren Moss|Seren]] and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] in another, [[Bell]] and [[Thorn]] in a third, and [[Mike]] alone (*"he snores really loudly and he's stinky"*). Replacement keys: one gold. The rooms are bigger, the furniture imported, the beds better. Eldrin asked Haldur to source the best mead he can find. **The egg stays with Mike**, carried.

And finally, back down to [[Good For What Ails Ya]], lively with card games and numbered-tile gambling, where **[[Nastrin]]'s special mead order had arrived**. Eldrin asked the price of the barrel (*"I paid 35 gold for it"*), said *"50 gold it is,"* and **bought the whole thing** — Dura took a mug — and raised the toast. First, a name was required. The troupe has one; the table had simply forgotten it; Lyrion consulted Gustav: **the [[Twilight Masqueraders]]**. *"To the Twilight Masqueraders — there when everyone needs us, always in the wrong place at the right time. To a better tomorrow, and to great friends. And to the best mead this city has ever known — allegedly."* It was immaculate, true amber, nectar of the gods (Seren: *"My specialty is death. So no, I don't sense any of my divinity in it"*). Lyrion drank responsibly and proposed the troupe **perform right there, that night**; Thorn reached for her swords; the choreography discussion ended the session on Thorn planning to have Bell and Mike throw people past each other and asking Rhaenyra whether she can do flips. *"I honestly can't say that I've tried."* *"You look flippable."*

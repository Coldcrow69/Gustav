# Bluefield Burning — Sessions 1–5 (Arc Two Session Notes)

> Source: *Crypt Keepers Arc Two Session Notes* — sparse dated log, **9/24/25 → 10/29/25**. Notes thin out quickly; many sessions have only a line or two. The arc continues beyond this log (see [[Transcripts/Transcript Index]] for the post-10/29 sessions); the dragon attack on [[Bluefield]] itself happens later (~11/19/25) — not in this PDF.

## 9/24/25 — Session 1

- Something is troubling [[Victor Barun|Victor]]; he leaves to go handle stuff.
- The party meets Victor at a diner — **[[The Apple Thicket]]** — in [[Fort Elm]].
- **[[Rhaenyra]] is introduced** here.
- Rhaenyra's **fishing village** has been plagued by a **multi-headed beast with a large scar over its chest** (later revealed to be a five-headed hydra). Victor asks the party to **help her village before continuing their travels to [[Stonewater]]**.
- **Gifts** (Victor's handed out at this meeting; Seren's cloaks given separately at the docks on the **next-morning departure, 9/25/25** — see [[Seren's Cloaks]]):
  - **Thick winter cloaks** for everyone (from [[Seren Moss|Seren]]).
  - **[[Black Thermal Cube]]** — burnt around the edges, smelling of brimstone. **Elven command word "Ember"**; glows with warmth without burning the hands; portable campfire. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] tosses it over to **[[Bell]]**.
  - **[[Rope of Mending]]** — a spool of rope with an arcane sheen — handed to **[[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]]**.
  - **[[Everbright Lantern]]** — shuttered lantern, flame will never go out — handed to **[[Rhaenyra]]**.
- Head back to the temple for the night. **Long rest.**
- Next morning the party heads to the **[[Sail la Vie]]**.
- Victor's parting instruction: *"Talk to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]], foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]] in [[Stonewater]]."*
- The party sets sail.

## 10/1/25 — Session 2

- Sailing northward, the party finds a **downed 6 ft-diameter tree** that appears to have been uprooted unnaturally.
- After docking, they find **tracks like a snake trail** around the tree — leading from the river and back into the river. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] finds **reptilian claw marks**.
- [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] believes this sounds like a **hydra**.
- They find a **bobcat corpse with a huge chunk bitten out of its body**.
- **Claw marks**: 2 legs with a tail. Left foot has **2 front-facing claws and 1 back claw**; right foot has **3 front-facing claws and 1 back claw**. (Asymmetric — possibly significant.)
- More fallen trees nearby but no other carcasses.
- **Setting up bear traps along the riverbank** and baiting them to trick the monster into coming back on land.
- The boat is **removed from the water**.
- Bait the trap and wait through nightfall for the hydra to emerge.
- The hydra appears: **5 heads**, a **decapitated head stump that is very scarred**, scales **singed and damaged**. (See [[The Hydra]].)

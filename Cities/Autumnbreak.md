# Autumnbreak

A **coastal city** in [[Azadin]], located at the **delta of the [[Winternora Channel]]** where that north-to-south river empties into the ocean.

Origin town of **[[Basil]]**'s **dried-fish-merchant caravan** that the party intercepted on the road to [[Stonewater]] in spring 2026. The caravan picked up multiple travelers along its route (including the party itself, who hitched the final leg to [[Stonewater]]). A **separate second cart** bearing the **feather-and-dagger emblem of the [[Greybraids]]** dwarf clan traveled loosely alongside the caravan — per [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-17|Session 30]], that cart (not Basil's main caravan) is the chief suspect for the egg theft that triggered [[The God Of The Iron Peaks|the red dragon's]] assault on [[Bluefield]]. Basil remains a person of interest but the emblem-evidence does not point at him directly.

Aliases recorded by chroniclers: **Bluefields**.

A small village along the river in [[The Wilds]], north of [[Fort Elm]] and south of [[Stonewater]]. **Distinct from [[Roseview]]** ([[Rhaenyra]]'s home village). The party found this village on fire and besieged by a dragonkin warband looking for stolen red dragon eggs.

## Destruction

Bluefield was destroyed by a **multi-headed, extra-large red dragon** known as [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]]. The attack devastated the settlement; local firbolgs assisted refugees in escaping. The destruction prompted a search for stolen dragon eggs, believed to be the reason for the dragon's assault. A [[Captured Half-Dragon]] from the attacking force revealed this information.

### The party's approach (per WhisperX recap, 11/19/25)

Sailing north from [[Roseview]] toward [[Stonewater]] in the [[Sail la Vie]] on a twilight evening, the party encountered a **young pregnant couple with their animal** trying to cross the river; the party offered them passage to Bluefield, their destination. About an hour out from Bluefield, the party began to see **light pollution and the smell of burning wood** rising over the trees — far too much to be a bonfire. As they came in, they saw **multiple buildings on fire** along the river.

### The fight (per 2/15/26 recap)

The party fought **kobolds and a half-dragon riding a [[Spark Beasts|Spark Beast]] — a flaming bear**. They captured the half-dragon ([[Captured Half-Dragon]]). They **escaped the attention of [[The God Of The Iron Peaks|the multi-headed red dragon]]** itself. From interrogation: the warband said the dragon's children (the eggs) had been taken.

### Aftermath

The party met a **firbolg ([[Kira]])** familiar to [[Bell]], who led them to the [[Refugee Camp]] ~20 miles away. The destruction was later reported to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] in [[Stonewater]], along with the circumstances surrounding the attack and a request for assistance in preventing further assaults. The party also noted suspicious human adventurers passing through before the attack (linked to [[Basil]]'s caravan from [[Autumnbreak]], which the party later traveled with; the **feather-and-dagger emblem** sighting that anchored their suspicions has since — per [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-17|Session 30]] — been reassigned to a **separate cart** bearing the [[Greybraids]] crest, not Basil's main caravan).

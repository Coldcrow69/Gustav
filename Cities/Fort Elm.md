A primarily human trading post in the interior of [[The Wilds]], located at the fork of two rivers — one of which is the [[Rollingbrook River]]. **Where the campaign began.** One of the **larger settlements** in The Wilds.

## Description (DM world-doc)

Nestled within the heart of [[The Wilds]] — heavily wooded hills and towering mountains — Fort Elm is a **bastion of civilization** amidst rugged landscapes, a testament to human tenacity and a beacon of hope in the face of the wilderness's relentless grasp.

Within its borders unfolds a **symphony of greenery**: vibrant flora, architecture interwoven seamlessly with the lush surroundings, and a resplendent beauty heightened by its unique positioning atop the banks of a swift and meandering river.

A **sense of adventure lingers in the air** — the town's denizens coexist harmoniously with the untamed wilderness that encircles them, drawing inspiration from its untamed majesty while forging their own path. The town's tale is one of **determination, resilience, and an unquenchable thirst for the freedom wilderness provides**.

## Government

Run by an **elected council of 10 individuals**, each overseeing certain **districts** in the city.

## Economy

**Primary trade**: **Fish**, **Salt**, and other **spices**.

## Historical note

Fort Elm (and [[The Wilds]] as a whole) was one of the **few places not completely ravaged** by the [[Glyph Scar]] war — meaning the town has **a lot of history to uncover and stories to tell**.

## Layout

- **Northern district** (nicer part of town): the [[Victor Barun]] / Braun household — covered in flowering ivy and greenery, sited on the river. Its placement on the river hints Barun is wealthier than he lets on.
- **East side**: training grounds — where [[Teron]] was training during the [[Prologue]]
- **South**: open country / wilderness — where [[Bell]] was camping
- **Riverside / mansion district**: [[Victor Barun]]'s mansion (the **collapsed house** of the [[Fish People]] arc). **Two distinct underground events** happened beneath it during Session 1: (1) the **tight tunnel beneath the wine cellar** — where the real Victor was found amid strange algae and a gel-encased screaming skeleton, recovered by [[Seren Moss|Seren]] / [[Bell]] / [[Teron|Teran]]; and (2) the post-collapse **secret tunnel and open reservoir** containing a slime creature, discovered after the house collapsed and the party awoke in a sinkhole.
- **[[Shrine of Kelemvor]]**: where [[Seren Moss|Seren]] was raised. Carefully-kept green lawn, gravestones of various materials with fresh flowers, wrought-iron fence enclosing the yard to protect the dead from robbers and vandals. Staffed by [[Gwyn Moss]] (shrine keeper), [[Uncle Bren]], [[The Aunties]], and acolytes including [[Ilsa]]
- **Local garrison**: where [[Lilith]] (Captain of the Guard) brought the party to regroup at the end of Session 5 of [[Fish People]]

## Recent events

The town was the site of the [[Fish People]] arc. The [[Rollingbrook River]] suffered ecological collapse — vile fish creatures, slime, sour smell, foul foam — and an underground cult / temple network was discovered beneath the city. The corruption was traced to a [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]] in the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth|cathedral]] of [[The Ancient Ruined City]] deep below Fort Elm; the party killed it. **Council bounty of 3,000 gp** was paid to the party for slaying the monster.

In the arc's aftermath, **[[Riknozz]]'s peaceful Drow sect was granted temporary refugee status** in Fort Elm by [[Lilith]].

**Resolved background mystery**: the **missing farmers** of Fort Elm — the specific commission [[Victor Barun]] gave [[Seren Moss|Seren]] which drew her onto the road with the party — were **kidnapped by the fish people** during the [[Fish People]] arc.

Per [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s later report to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] during the [[Egg Hunt]], [[Victor Barun]] is doing well despite recent troubles in Fort Elm, where a monster had been causing issues.

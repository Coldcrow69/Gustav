# Silvermire

> Transcript variants: "Silvermeyer," "Silbermire." Page created on the maintainer's call after [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]]; **not yet visited**, location unrecorded.

A settlement known so far only by what reaches [[Stonewater]] from it:

- **Silvermire rum** — a "special import" on the bar at [[Raise Your Spirits]]: fruity, coconut-flavored, a taste [[Mike]] had never met.
- **Visitors from Silvermire** are expected at the spa's **upper-echelon event in a week or two**, alongside Stonewater's **city council** — the occasion [[Tiamor Runegrip]] wants the [[Twilight Masqueraders]] to play.

Whether it is a city of [[The Wilds]], a mountain neighbor in the [[Iron Peaks]], or somewhere farther afield is unknown. The rum's coconut suggests a warmer or coastal origin; that is inference, not canon.

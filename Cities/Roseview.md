# Roseview

A small **fishing village** in the north of [[The Wilds]], **[[Rhaenyra]]'s home village**. **Not [[Bluefield]]** — Roseview and Bluefield are distinct settlements; the party left Roseview heading north along the river route toward [[Stonewater]] before encountering Bluefield burning.

## Bluefield Burning arc events

- The village had been plagued by **[[The Hydra|a five-headed hydra]]** — the immediate cause of [[Rhaenyra]] traveling to [[Fort Elm]] to seek help. [[Victor Barun]] introduced her to the party at [[The Apple Thicket]] on 9/24/25.
- The party sailed north on the [[Sail la Vie]], intercepted and killed the hydra along the riverbank (10/8/25), and **arrived at Roseview on 10/15/25**.
- On 11/5/25, **[[Thalrin]] hosted a festival** celebrating the hydra slaying. Feasting, town exploration, and a **wizard duel** as entertainment (which left [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] singed and required healing from [[Seren Moss|Seren]]).
- The village has **no tavern or inn**; the party slept on the boat.
- On 11/19/25, the party gave "heartfelt goodbyes" to Roseview and set sail north toward Stonewater.

## Notable inhabitants

- [[Rhaenyra]] — daughter of the village's most prominent family
- [[Thalrin]] — village leader / organizer of the festival

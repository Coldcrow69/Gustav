A Dwarf fortress at the SW base of [[Iron Peaks|The Iron Peaks]]. Major city and power in [[The Wilds]] region.

Aliases recorded by chroniclers: **Stoneforge**, **Ironforge** (all refer to this city in Session 29 records).

![[Stone_Water.jpg]]

## City map (top-down)

The city sits inside a roughly oval cavern bounded by sheer rock walls. Multiple **lakes and underground rivers** thread through the cavern floor — broad blue waterways branching around the districts and exiting under the perimeter walls. **Bridges** span every channel.

Visible districts and features:

- **Central plaza / forum** at the heart of the city — circular ringed structures around an open hub.
- **Dense residential blocks** in the south and southeast, ranks of small structures packed tightly.
- **Tiered upper districts** to the north — larger, more ornate buildings rising on terraces (matches "increasingly ornate buildings as one ascends").
- **Industrial / market quarters** along the eastern wall, with regimented rectangular plots.
- **Outer cavern margins** lined with the natural rock wall; multiple **gate or tunnel passages** breach the wall at the cardinal compass points.
- **Lower district** (matching [[Good For What Ails Ya]]'s placement) implied at the bottom edge of the map, riverside.

The water network and the multi-level construction together explain the city's "Stonewater" name — built into rock, threaded with water.

Associated NPCs: [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] (foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]]).
Associated locations: [[Ironheart Mines]], [[Good For What Ails Ya]] (tavern in the lower district), [[Stonewater Sewers]], [[Temple of Selûne (Stonewater)]] (green-domed temple-library in the upper tier), [[Barracks (Stonewater)]] (the city guard's third-tier fort), [[Aiker's Potion Explorium]].

**Geography (maintainer-confirmed):** Stonewater lies **directly west of [[Fort Elm]]** and **south-west of the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]]** as the crow flies. The party's route ran by river: **north from Fort Elm** (past [[Roseview]] and [[Bluefield]]), **west** on a second river past the camp, then **south** on the final leg — which is why travel notes describe both "north" and "south" journeys toward the same city.

## Description

The city is built into rock, featuring cavernous architecture with natural openings that allow light to filter in. It includes a mix of markets and homes, with increasingly ornate buildings as one ascends. Merchants and locals engage in trade, creating a bustling atmosphere. Streets are dimly lit at night; buildings give off a vibrant glow that casts colorful reflections on the cobblestones.

## Recent events

The [[Ironheart Mines]], overseen by [[Baldrin Stoneforge]], are a significant part of the city's operations. Recently the area has experienced disappearances and murders linked to the mines. Baldrin's residence serves as a point of contact for those seeking information about these issues. Concerns include the consistency of the manner of death, the weapon used, and the disappearance of the accused man's corpse and weapon. **As of [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]],** the murderer is revealed to be the **revenant** of [[Victor Stonehammer]] — the vanished "accused man" — preying on cave-in survivors from the [[Stonewater Sewers]]; the party contained it but did not destroy it. The city's guard forces include the clan-affiliated [[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge Guard]] and a separate city watch — the **Stonewater Guard** under [[Captain Grazug]] — based at the third-tier Barracks. (The two are distinct forces, but not disjoint in personnel: per the maintainer, Session 35, the Stoneforge Clan has a large presence throughout the city guard and military — see [[Stoneforge Clan]].)

The city has been affected by the destruction of [[Bluefield]] by the **multi-headed red dragon** [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]]. There are concerns regarding a [[Captured Half-Dragon]] and the theft of dragon eggs. [[Rhaenyra]] warned that Stonewater could be destroyed if the eggs are not recovered. A suspicious caravan with unfamiliar travelers has also been reported, prompting requests for assistance in tracking it down.

[[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Mike]] arrived in Stonewater ahead of the rest of the party, going to the tavern [[Good For What Ails Ya]] in the lower district to await them.

**As of [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]:** the revenant is **disembodied but expected to re-form within roughly half a day**; the [[Captain Grazug|city guard captain]] has **deputized** [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Mike]], and [[Thorn]] for the investigation and is warning the **graveyard keepers**. [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s egg-tracking sweep of the city found **one dragon egg in a [[Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)|lakeside junk pile]]** (recovered — see [[Dragon Eggs]]) and **at least two more at an abandoned [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside mansion]]** (not yet approached); the [[Greybraids]]' holdings showed no eggs.

**As of [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]:** the deputized party has raided the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] on the **southeastern cliffs** — occupied by the **[[Acolytes of Oghma]]**, housed there by **[[Kassadren]]**, patriarch of the [[Greybraids]] — and found an [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]] fleeing into **natural caves** beneath the building (possibly connected to the [[Ironheart Mines]]' breached passage). The raid ended mid-battle with **[[Lyrion Thalor]] dead on the manor floor** and the Revivify window running. The city's guard force is now named: the **Stonewater Guard**, under [[Captain Grazug]] at the third-tier Barracks. Per the maintainer ([[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]), the **[[Stoneforge Clan]] has a large presence in the city's guard and military** — clan colors appear inside the city force.

**As of [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]:** the manor raid is reported and an **official investigation is open**; the manor is a guarded crime scene; the party moves with an assigned guard escort, **[[Dura]]**. The **first snow of the year** is falling through the cavern's light-slits (late fall; the mountains above hold permanent snow). Multiple citizens reported **something large and wingless flying at the top of the cavern** before it vanished — unexplained. City geography filled in this session: **hot springs** on the eastern side atop the highest cliff (with a "full treatment plan"); **breweries** in the upper districts; **jewelers** in the third district; **[[Aiker's Potion Explorium]]** (a shop inside a hollowed pumpkin, run by a kenku) near the guardhouse; **street messengers** run notes for 5 copper. Dwarven families here typically run five to ten children, per [[Dura]]. The [[Victor Stonehammer|revenant]], expected since Session 32 to re-form within roughly half a day, has **still not resurfaced** as of the Session 35 morning — a full night past the estimate; the guard will send word if it appears.

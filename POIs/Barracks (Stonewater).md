# Barracks (Stonewater)

> Archivist alias: **"the Guardhouse."** Named with the city qualifier — other towns keep their own barracks and guardhouses; this page is [[Stonewater]]'s only.

Headquarters of the **Stonewater Guard** — the city watch under [[Captain Grazug]] — on the city's **third tier**: a **retrofitted fort** with a front-desk reception area.

## On-page history

- **Session 31** ([[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|recap]]): after the alley murder scene, the lead guard invited the party to bring anything further "to the third-tier Barracks" — the first the party heard of the place.
- **Session 32** ([[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|recap]]): [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Mike]], and [[Thorn]] were received here by [[Captain Grazug]] and **[[Writ of Deputization|deputized]]** for the supernatural-murder investigation.
- **Session 35** ([[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|recap]]): the morning after the manor raid, Grazug took **Lyrion's statement** (terse, monosyllabic) and **Mike's** ("I'm a sorcerer," ×6, underlined on the clipboard) here, and shared the guard-side status: no movement at the manor, a guard atop the tunnel ladder, no [[Victor Stonehammer|revenant]] sightings. **Barracks housing** was floated as one option for the party's pending tavern relocation.

Nearby: **[[Aiker's Potion Explorium]]** sits in the immediate area.

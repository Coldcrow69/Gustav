# Ichor's Potion Explorium

> Transcript/Archivist variants: "Aiker's Potion Explorium," "Iker's Potion Emporium" — the proprietor's name is spelled **[[Ichor]]** (maintainer-confirmed, Session 36). First visited in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]. **Not** where the party's Revivify diamonds came from — the Archivist conflated the two shops; the diamonds were a jeweler's purchase in the Clock Tower District (see [[Powerful Diamond]]).

A potion shop in [[Stonewater]], in the immediate area of the [[Barracks (Stonewater)|guardhouse]] — built inside an **actual giant pumpkin**, hollowed and carved: window-holes cut into the rind, a chimney where the stem was, a wooden staircase up to the foyer. Inside it's fully furnished and unrecognizable as a pumpkin — an open U-shaped floor ringed with glass display cabinets, run like a jewelry store by the **kenku** proprietor **[[Ichor]]**.

## Stock (as seen, Session 35)

Potions come in **custom vegetable-shaped vials**:

- **Healing** — carrot, 50 gp
- **Greater healing** — onion, 150 gp
- **Superior healing** — pumpkin, 400 gp
- **Potions of climbing**; **elemental-resistance potions**
- **Minor luck potion** — 200 gp ([[Thorn]] bought one; one more in stock)
- **Potion of Hill Giant Strength** — 150 gp; **Potion of Fire Giant Strength** — 500 gp (STR 25 for an hour — [[Mike]]'s ears perked: his village kin to fire giants)
- **Potion of Watchful Rest** — common, 50 gp (stay awake through a long rest and still gain its benefits)
- **Potion of Vitality** — very rare, **7,000 gp** — removes all exhaustion; the item [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] (4/6 exhaustion) opened scroll-barter negotiations over. **Barter failed** (resolved off-screen before [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]]): the price gap exceeded what his scrolls could cover. Still on the shelf.

The shop deals in **healing and recovery** potions only. Asked in Session 36 about **utility potions** (Seren wanted Water Breathing), the DM's ruling for the whole city: not brewed here, not readily available — **go east, deeper into the wilds**.

The party **restocked on healing potions** here (ledger deliberately unrecorded — maintainer's call). It was also where Lyrion, digging for barter stock, opened the manor's [[Arcane Scroll Case (Cliffside Mansion)|scroll case]] and discovered the **[[Scroll of Chain Lightning]]**.

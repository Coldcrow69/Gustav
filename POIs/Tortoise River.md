# Tortoise River

A **west-to-east flowing river** that drains east to the ocean.

## Notable points along its course

- Passes **[[Bluefield]]**
- Passes the firbolg **[[Refugee Camp]]** (~20 miles from Bluefield)
- Continues east past the **elf city of [[Zadrid]]**
- Empties into the **ocean** at its eastern end

Mentioned in the 2026-03-01 transcript as a geographic landmark near the refugee camp.

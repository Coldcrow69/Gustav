# Stonewater Sewers

> The archivist export invented the name "Sewers of Eldrath." There is **no canonical proper name** — these are simply the city sewers of [[Stonewater]].

The sewer / storm-runoff complex beneath [[Stonewater]], handling the city's water runoff. Accessed by **hinged circular grates** in the streets and alleys, each with a ladder descending into arched stone tunnels (~15–20 ft across, ~10 ft tall at the crown). The tunnels are threaded with **water channels, bridges, and copper piping**, the floor slick with grime. Multiple access points open onto the main thoroughfares, letting someone move across the city **unseen**.

## Session 31

The **revenant** of [[Victor Stonehammer]] had been hiding and storing itself here, in a former **utility / maintenance closet** (lumber, stone, a bucket of dried mortar, rat carcasses, and a gnawed **[[Severed Dwarven Torso]]**). The party tracked it down via a trail of **[[Black Ichor|black ichor]]** from the alley, fought it on the bridges and canals, and sealed it inside a **[[Chained Stone Coffin]]**.

# The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)

A **ruined temple of [[Tyr]]** located in the cave system beneath [[Fort Elm]], at the upper edges of [[The Ancient Ruined City]].

Used by **[[Riknozz]]'s peaceful Drow sect** as a **temporary camp** during the [[Fish People]] arc — the location where the party was first introduced to the sect, met the [[Drow Elder]], and learned that the cavern's master was an aboleth.

## Significance

- The Drow sect's choice of a Tyr temple as refuge is consistent with their break from violent Drow ways toward something more like justice-aligned conduct.
- Co-locates with the [[The Ancient Ruined City|ancient ruined city]] — a remnant of the pre-collapse civilization.

## State after the arc

The Drow sect was granted **temporary refugee status in [[Fort Elm]]**; the temple camp is presumably abandoned or in transition.

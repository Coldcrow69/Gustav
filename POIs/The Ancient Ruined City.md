# The Ancient Ruined City

An **ancient city, ruined, deep beneath [[Fort Elm]]**. Reached via the cave system extending under the city — first via the **tunnel beneath [[Victor Barun]]'s wine cellar** (initially), and later via a **gulch in the forest** outside Fort Elm that [[Bell]] located.

## Description

Reached by descending a long, manmade tunnel followed by an enormous **staircase curving downward** out of view, past the [[Riddle Door]]. At the bottom: a city in **ancient but startlingly preserved** state, with a **near-pristine [[Cathedral of the Aboleth|cathedral]] at its center**.

The city contains:

- The **[[Cathedral of the Aboleth|central cathedral]]** with a pit of putrid water at its heart — the lair of the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]].
- The **prisoners' holding building** — where the fish-monsters kept kidnapped Fort Elm townsfolk, including [[Gwyn Moss|Gwyn]].
- The [[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)|ruined Temple of Tyr]] — used as a temporary camp by [[Riknozz]]'s peaceful Drow sect.
- A **dome-shaped chamber of skeletal remains** — what appears to be a battlefield from **just after the end of the [[Glyph Scar|Glyph War]]**, varied races (humans, elves, dwarves, halflings), all wearing the same crest as the [[Dagger of Strange Iconography]] and [[Coin of Strange Iconography]].

## History (per the [[Drow Elder]])

The Drow have oral history of this place: **"an ancient city where people lived in safety and harmony, until their society collapsed under the influence of a powerful evil."** Whether the "powerful evil" was the [[The Undead Aboleth|aboleth]] now sealed in the cathedral, the [[Glyph Scar]] itself, or some third factor, remains unresolved.

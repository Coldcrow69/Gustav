# Raise Your Spirits

> Transcript variants: "Raysia Spirits," "Razia Spirits." First visited in [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]] — the "hot springs" the party had been pointed to since Session 35.

A **spa and spirit bar** on the **highest tier of [[Stonewater]]** — a cliff-top plateau on the city's eastern side holding a lake, reached by a long staircase carved into the stone (two routes up: the northern path past the [[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|Greybraid Manor]], the Greybraid forge, and the Stoneforge estate; or the southern path past the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]]). From the patio the whole city is visible below, the manor in the distance. Owned and run by **[[Tiamor Runegrip]]**.

**The building:** large stone, framed in dark timber, with circular roofs of purplish tile; non-native plants kept as greenery out front; a wide open patio with seating. Inside, a central circular reception desk and a series of **round rooms interlinked by hallways**; a changing room with **lockable trunks** (key stays with the guest); robes and towels provided. The clientele runs to **miners** — "I have to beg some of these miners to put a towel on."

## The works — 30 gp per person

The all-inclusive package, in order:

1. **Hot-stone massage** — heated granite slabs on the tight spots.
2. **Charcoal-paste body massage** — a mud-and-charcoal detox paste worked in and left to sit, followed by an elbow-deep massage.
3. **Rock-salt cleanse** — full-body exfoliation, room big enough for the whole party.
4. **Mud bath** — a separate building across a patio walkway; a cobblestone-rimmed pool of warm, faintly bubbling mud with an iron rail, a rinse station, and extra towels. *Lore (per the attendant): in the city's founding days, before running water, miners cleaned the mine dust off in pools of mud; it stuck as a comfort.*
5. **The Stonewater Plunge** — a fenced-off stair descending into the **lake itself**: ice cold, bracing, small fish included.
6. **The hot springs** — tiered natural pools against the cave wall, four to six feet deep, elevated enough to look down over the city and the snow drifting through the cavern cracks.
7. **Rock-needle acupuncture** — "designed for dwarves"; stings.

Plus the **open bar**, run by a dwarven bartender in a Dwarvish suit jacket on the patio, who also brings platters of shots to the treatment rooms. **Spirits only — no mead.** House brews from their own local distillery: **Rockbiter Whiskey** (dark, smoky honey, not from honeybees), **Fairy's Breath** (mint vodka), **Goodberry Gin** (raspberry-ish, deceptively smooth, heals 1 HP), **Willow Whiskey** (cinnamon), **Cold Gin** (charcoal-filtered); plus an imported **[[Silvermire]] rum** (fruity, coconut). The place "wants you a little bit fucked up by the end of it — fucked up and feeling good."

## The skeleton in the lake

Near the lake's shore, visible in lantern light as what first reads as pointed rock: **several enormous ribs**. Per a spa employee, the bones have been there **since Stonewater's founding and probably long before**; the **scholars at the college in the city's upper tiers** believe they belonged to a **gargantuan beast of these mountains from before the [[Glyph Scar]]** — never identified, bigger than anything living memory has seen. The remains **leach minerals and calcium into the lake**, credited for the water's health benefits. The DM teased it the session before as "a massive skeleton in the lake"; [[Rhaenyra]] called it "almost like a guardian of the water." Nothing more is known.

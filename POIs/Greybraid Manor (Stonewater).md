# Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)

The **[[Greybraids]]** clan's estate in [[Stonewater]] — **opulent**, though a notch **smaller than the Stoneforge estate** ([[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s manor), which it sits near. **Clan guards** stand at the front gate. Seat of the clan's patriarch, **[[Kassadren]]**.

> **Do not confuse** with the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] — several archivist fragments mislabel the cult-occupied cliffside building "Greybraid Manor." The connection between the two is the landlord: Kassadren granted the [[Acolytes of Oghma]] their right to occupy the cliffside property.

## Session 33 — the gate visit

[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Thorn]] presented the [[Writ of Deputization]] to an **older, grizzled dwarf guard**, who found the papers legitimate — and asked whether they were looking for the Stoneforge estate instead. Lyrion requested someone who deals regularly with the [[Stoneforge Clan]] regarding the cave-in, asked that any reply be routed through **[[Captain Grazug]]**, and dangled the value of shared information for **the patriarch's** dealings with Stoneforge — given that the clans "don't always see eye to eye" over Stoneforge's control of the mines. The guard agreed to relay the message **to Kassadren**; the pair left without being admitted. No reply yet.

Notably, [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s Session 32 tracking sweep registered **no dragon-egg signals here** (nor at the clan's forge).

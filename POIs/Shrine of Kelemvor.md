# Shrine of Kelemvor (Fort Elm)

A shrine of [[Kelemvor]] in [[Fort Elm]]. The home of [[Seren Moss]] for as long as she can remember — she was abandoned on its steps as a baby and adopted by [[Gwyn Moss]], its keeper.

## Description

> Source: *Seren (OG Crypt Keepers)* short story.

Gwyn's study window overlooks a **carefully-kept green lawn** dotted with **gravestones of various materials** and **fresh flowers** adorning the graves. A **distant wrought-iron fence** encloses the yard, **protecting the beloved dead from robbers and vandals**. The study contains an **ancient wooden desk** (pocked and pitted) and a **cheerfully crackling hearth**.

## Staff

- [[Gwyn Moss]] — shrine keeper, [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s adoptive mother
- [[Uncle Bren]] — Gwyn's brother
- [[The Aunties]] — Gwyn's three sisters
- **Acolytes** — assist with the shrine's daily work

## Acolytes' duties (per Seren's upbringing)

- Praying
- Cleaning the chapel from top to bottom
- Tending the grounds
- Preparing bodies for burial
- Consoling the bereaved
- **Honing battle skills to frighten away graverobbers**

Acolytes lead a "strict, austere life from the moment they came to live in His shrine."

## Security history

Most frightening incident on record (per Seren): "a desperate duo of men hoping to find jewels in a casket." Routine graverobber attempts.

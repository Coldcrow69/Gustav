# Temple of Selûne (Stonewater)

> **Deity name.** Recorded by the DM as "Sylvanas" / "goddess of Sylvanas" and by [[Mike]] as "the church of Sivanis"; resolved to **Selûne** per the campaign maintainer. (Selûne does not appear elsewhere in the vault yet; the archivist filed the temple as "Temple Of Selune.") The Session 32 archivist export repeats the mishearing as "Temple of Sylvanus" — including in [[Haceran Leadbreaker]]'s generated page title — and the maintainer has **re-confirmed Selûne** stands.

A temple of **Selûne** in [[Stonewater]], up in the **tiered upper district** — a **green-domed building** that rises above the surrounding rooftops. It **doubles as the city library**: attendants shush visitors, and the central hall is lined with **pews** leading to a large **statue of the goddess**, the interior greened over with what looks like trees.

## Clergy

- Several clerics serve here; **not all** of them can cast spells. Much of the clergy **specialize in tending to the living** (per [[Haceran Leadbreaker]], [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]).
- **[[Haceran Leadbreaker]]** — the elderly dwarf cleric the party dealt with in [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]] (named in Session 32) — keeps a **heavy tome listing the city's registered clerics (devotaries)**.
- None on hand could cast **Banishment** the day the party needed it (to deal with the [[Victor Stonehammer]] revenant).

## Note

[[Stonewater]] has **no temple to [[Kelemvor]]** ([[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s deity) — only a few scattered followers.

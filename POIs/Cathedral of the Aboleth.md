# Cathedral of the Aboleth

The **central building** of [[The Ancient Ruined City]], beneath [[Fort Elm]]. Lair of the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]] until its slaying.

![[cathedral aboleth.webp]]

## Visual reference

A towering gothic cathedral rising from a vast subterranean cavern. Spires and pointed arched windows climb toward a stalactite-fanged ceiling, with shafts of pale light filtering down from a high opening above. The structure stands on a shoreline of dark rocks and jagged shards in a still, blue-green lake; the architecture extends laterally into arcaded galleries set into the cavern walls on either side. Overall palette: cold blue-white stone against black rock and luminous water. Reads as both holy and drowned — consistent with the [[The Undead Aboleth|aboleth]]'s influence and the sealed-tomb function of the site.

## Description

- **Near-pristine** condition despite the surrounding city's ruin.
- The interior centers on a **circle of ancient statues** around a **deep pit of disgusting, slimy, putrid water** — the aboleth's tomb-pit.
- The aboleth was sealed by **runes on spikes impaled in the creature** and **symbols inside the well** — a very powerful sealing spell that had been slowly failing.
- The cathedral can warp visitors' perception: **time and space dilate** inside (an hour outside felt like minutes to [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]); the **interior is reflexive / recursive** under the aboleth's influence (every direction looked like the center to Lyrion when illusion-trapped).
- An **altar** at the back of the hall — where ancient offerings were left. **[[Mystra]]** guided [[Teron]] to claim them after the battle: spell scrolls, [[Sail la Vie|the mahogany box]], 5,400 gp.
- **Ancient carvings** decorate the interior (Lyrion attempted to commit them to memory before fleeing the first incursion).

## Events recorded

- **Lyrion's first incursion**: observed kuo-toa minions transforming back into slime; nearly trapped by the aboleth's mind-illusion; thrown a fireball, fled.
- **Final battle**: party of six killed the aboleth here. Bell charmed for much of the fight. Teron briefly charmed, stabbed Seren. Final blow: Lyrion's fireball.

## Current state

Cleared. The animating magic of the aboleth's minions has presumably ceased; the slime corruption upstream in the [[Rollingbrook River]] expected to fade.

# Lotus Run

A **cave on the north side of [[Fort Elm]], past the river**.

Identified by [[Teron]] during Session 6 of the [[Fish People]] arc (7/31/24) while reviewing **Fort Elm guard records** — a site of recorded disappearance(s). Teron also mentioned **another area further north of the town where people have been going missing around there for many years**.

Connection to the broader Fish People underground network: implied but **unexplored** by the party in recorded sessions. Open lead.

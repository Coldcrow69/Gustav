# Rollingbrook River

The river running through [[Fort Elm]] — the town is built at the fork of two rivers, one of which is the Rollingbrook.

## State during the [[Fish People]] arc

- **Nasty sour smell** emanating from the river
- **Foul foam** on the river bank
- Pollution with slimy and foamy substances
- Filled with **vile fish creatures** (kuo-toa) that **turned to slime upon death** — confirmed source: kuo-toa reverse-transformation at the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth|cathedral pit]] under the influence of the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]]
- An **unnatural glow** on the river the evening after the council refused aid
- A **scent of sulfur and rot** hung over Fort Elm the morning after
- Tunnel and reservoir network beneath [[Fort Elm]] feeding into / from the river — discovered when the collapsed mansion revealed a secret passage to an open reservoir containing a slime creature

A vial of the river water and a slime sample are in [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s possession ([[River Water Vial]], [[Slime Sample]]). Per Eldrin's report: the water itself is not magical — the anomaly was a **change in the river's ecosystem** driven by the corrupting influence of the aboleth.

## Underground

Beneath the river lies a **cavern network** that ultimately leads to [[The Ancient Ruined City]] — kuo-toa, sahuagin, large squid-like creatures, and **bioluminescent algae** are all features of this network. The kuo-toa carried hostages into a **hole in the river** during the second river attack; [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s vision while unconscious revealed an **underground temple** where people were brought for **flesh sacrifices** — confirmed to be the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]].

## Post-arc state

With the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]] slain and its kuo-toa minions presumably no longer being conjured from the slime, the river's corruption is expected to fade. Confirmation pending.

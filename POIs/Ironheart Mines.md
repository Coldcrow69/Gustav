Located in / overseen from [[Stonewater]] (recorded as Stoneforge in chronicler notes). Foreman: [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] (Baldrin Stoneforge). Vital to the local economy.

## Recent troubles

The mines have faced a string of disappearances and murders. The party reported these issues during their audience with [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] alongside the news of [[Bluefield]]'s destruction and the [[Captured Half-Dragon]].

Open questions raised by the party:

- Whether the manner of death is consistent across victims
- What type of weapon was used
- Why a particular man had been suspected
- Whether the murders are ongoing
- Whether the accused man's corpse and weapon had gone missing (both confirmed vanished)

The party requested to examine the weapon and suggested a possible magical cause — a curse or compulsion.

## Session 31 — the accused man revealed

[[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]] answered several of these questions. The **accused man whose corpse and weapon vanished** was **[[Victor Stonehammer]]**, a dwarf cornered and killed by the [[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge Guard]]; his body decomposed rapidly and went missing because he returned as a **revenant**. The murder victims are **survivors of the recent cave-in**, killed in vengeance, all bearing **puncture wounds**; the weapon is a pickaxe bearing the **[[Stoneforge Clan]] insignia** (foreman [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s clan). One victim's spirit revealed the survivors had **seen "people in the cave"** during the collapse — something someone wanted silenced.

**Still open:** what was in the cave, why Victor Stonehammer was killed or left to die there, and whether [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] or the cave-in itself connects to the stolen dragon eggs.

## Session 32 — the party reaches the collapsed tunnel

[[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], [[Rhaenyra]], and [[Bell]] entered the mines with [[Mine Pass|writs]] issued at [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s manor, escorted **through checkpoints** down to the **collapsed tunnel**, where the escort left them with Baldrin himself ([[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]).

- Baldrin: the shaft was due only for **general preventative maintenance** and **should not have collapsed**; the **survivors' accounts don't match**, and he now suspects **foul play**.
- Clearing the blockage conventionally will take time; the party's magic might force a smaller opening sooner.
- Baldrin **agreed to provide complete lists of the dead and surviving miners**.
- There are **a few other mine entrances** he knows of — all **outside the city** ([[Stonewater]] itself has a **single mine entrance**, per the maintainer); locations uncatalogued in these records.
- [[Rhaenyra]] summoned a **small spider** to scout through the cracks to the collapse's far side — **not yet sent** when the session ended. The far side remains unseen.

[[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s egg-tracking sweep the same day registered **no dragon-egg signal at the city's single mine entrance** (see [[Dragon Eggs]]).

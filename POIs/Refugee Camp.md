# Refugee Camp (Firbolg)

A **firbolg encampment** **~20 miles from [[Bluefield]]**, sheltering survivors of the dragon attack on the village. Far enough that the dragon flying overhead did not spot it, close enough to evacuate a large group of people.

The party reached it after the Bluefield attack, led there by **[[Kira]]**.

## Inhabitants

- A **clan of firbolgs** — the firbolg community connected by **[[Bell]]**'s backstory; clan elders shared information about Bell's past with the party.
- The **rescued Bluefield refugees** — tended by the firbolgs and the party's healers.

## What happened here

- The firbolg **elders gave the party a tome** detailing the **ritual of communion with the spirits** that inhabit the clan (see [[Tome of Firbolg Spirit Communion]]).
- The party set up their own camp adjacent to the refugee camp.
- [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Bell]], [[Kira]], and [[Mike]] performed the communion ritual in the woods that night. The ritual completed; **a spirit spoke Lyrion's name**.
- [[Bell]] became bonded to the **[[Loa]] [[Zehal]]** through the ritual.
- The next morning the party was **awoken by the sound of flapping overhead** — likely the dragon patrolling.

> Approximate map location: the area of "Tortoise River" is referenced — this may be a tributary of the [[Rollingbrook River]] running south of Bluefield. See [[Tortoise River]].

# Refugee Camp (Firbolg)

> Aliases: **Firbolg Camp**. The "firbolg camp" and the "refugee camp" are the **same place**. The firbolgs keep **semi-permanent camps** through this region of [[The Wilds]]; this one took in the survivors who fled [[Bluefield]] when it burned — hence the two names.

A **firbolg encampment** in the forest of [[The Wilds]], **~20 miles from [[Bluefield]]** along the [[Tortoise River]]. Far enough that the dragon patrolling overhead did not spot it, close enough to evacuate a large group of survivors. The party was led here by **[[Kira]]** after the Bluefield dragon attack.

## Getting there

The party fled Bluefield aboard the **[[Sail la Vie]]** with the rescued survivors, **docked the boat on the river**, then **walked about a mile inland through the forest** to reach the firbolg camp. The **Sail la Vie stayed at the river as the party's lodging** (they camped on the deck — [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s bedroll by the helm), while the firbolgs sheltered the Bluefield refugees at the inland camp. (Leaving the camp, the party walked the ~1 mile back **south to the river** to re-board — see the 3/1/26 entry in [[Bluefield Burning]].)

## Inhabitants

- A **clan of firbolgs** — the community connected to **[[Bell]]**'s backstory; the clan elders shared information about Bell's past with the party (specifics not yet recorded).
- The **rescued Bluefield refugees**, taken in and tended by the firbolgs and the party's healers. Among them, a family with a newborn observed by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]].

## What happened here

- The firbolg **elders gave the party a tome** detailing the **ritual of communion with the spirits** that inhabit the clan (see [[Tome of Firbolg Spirit Communion]]).
- [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Bell]], [[Kira]], and [[Mike]] performed the communion ritual in the woods that night. It completed; **a spirit spoke Lyrion's name**.
- [[Bell]] became bonded to the **[[Loa]] [[Zehal]]**.
- The next morning the party was **awoken by flapping overhead** — the dragon patrolling.

## Arc boundary

**Leaving the firbolg camp marks the end of the [[Bluefield Burning]] arc and the start of [[Egg Hunt]]** — the journey south to the river and on toward [[Stonewater]] belongs to Egg Hunt.

## Possible location on the map

A **prominent ancient tree with a clustered settlement** in the **north-central forest** on the [[The Wilds|regional map]] is the leading candidate for this camp.

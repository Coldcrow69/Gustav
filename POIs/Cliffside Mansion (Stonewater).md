# Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)

> Also "the cliffside manor" / "abandoned manor on the southeastern cliffs." **Several Session 33 archivist fragments mislabel this building "Greybraid Manor"** — wrong building; the [[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|clan's actual manor]] is elsewhere in the city. The connection is the landlord: the [[Greybraids]]' patriarch **[[Kassadren]]** granted this property to its current occupants.

A **run-down manor on the southeastern cliffs** of [[Stonewater]] — windows boarded, a **graveyard out front**, every appearance of long abandonment. The appearance is false: inside, the entry shows **signs of steady traffic**, and the building houses the **[[Acolytes of Oghma]]** — roughly a dozen robed residents, mostly dwarves — as a "newer residency."

First registered in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]] as the strongest hit on [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s egg-tracking sweep: **at least two [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]]** — by the DM's framing, the **majority of the clutch** — somewhere inside. Approached and breached in [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]].

## Layout (as explored)

- **Ground floor:** dim mud-room entry; inner door (kept locked; [[Vil]] behind it); a many-doored central room where the party met the [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]]; a **northern room** (the [[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|bald mage]]'s); wall sconces, shelving, a teapot of brief historical importance.
- **Cellar:** down a dog-legged stair (where [[Mike]] killed [[Vil]]); a corridor where an ornate-robed cultist hurled fire; storage with **barrels along the wall**.
- **The hidden caves:** behind the barrels, a **cut-brick rectangle in the stone** — a **concealed door** [[Seren Moss|Seren]] pressed open onto a **natural cave system**: branching tunnels and a **ladder descending deeper**, along the fleeing [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]]'s trail. Seren's read: the tunnels **resemble the mine workings** — possibly the same system the [[Ironheart Mines]] miners breached into.

## Status (Session 33 cliffhanger)

Active battlefield. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] lies **dead** on the ground floor with the Revivify clock running; the Elder (wounded) and the bald mage are unaccounted for; several cultists are dead; [[Seren Moss|Seren]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] are in the caves below; the eggs have **not yet been found** inside.

# Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)

> Recorded location of **two dragon-egg signals**; **not yet visited**. The archivist calls it an "abandoned mansion"; its exact position on [[Stonewater]]'s cliffs/tiers is unrecorded.

An **abandoned mansion on the cliffside** in [[Stonewater]]. In [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s hour-long tracking spell registered **two [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]]** here — the largest concentration the sweep found. The party recovered the single [[Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)|lakeside]] egg first; the mansion remains unapproached.

Ownership, condition, and occupants: **unknown**.

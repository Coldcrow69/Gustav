# Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)

> Also "the cliffside manor" / "abandoned manor on the southeastern cliffs." **Several Session 33 archivist fragments mislabel this building "Greybraid Manor"** — wrong building; the [[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|clan's actual manor]] is elsewhere in the city. The connection is the landlord: the [[Greybraids]]' patriarch **[[Kassadren]]** granted this property to its current occupants.

A **run-down manor on the southeastern cliffs** of [[Stonewater]] — windows boarded, a **graveyard out front**, every appearance of long abandonment. The appearance is false: inside, the entry shows **signs of steady traffic**, and the building houses the **[[Acolytes of Oghma]]** — roughly a dozen robed residents, mostly dwarves — as a "newer residency."

First registered in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]] as the strongest hit on [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s egg-tracking sweep: **at least two [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]]** — by the DM's framing, the **majority of the clutch** — somewhere inside. Approached and breached in [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]].

## Layout (as explored)

- **Ground floor:** dim mud-room entry; inner door (kept locked; [[Vil]] behind it); a many-doored central room where the party met the [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]]; a **northern room** (the [[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|bald mage]]'s); a **kitchen** (where [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] died and was revived); wall sconces, shelving, a teapot of brief historical importance.
- **Cellar:** down a dog-legged stair (where [[Mike]] killed [[Vil]]); a corridor where an ornate-robed cultist hurled fire; storage with **barrels along the wall** (all ale — searched Session 34); a table that held the **[[Map of the Mines (Cliffside Mansion)|singed map scroll case]]**.
- **The hidden caves:** behind the barrels, a **cut-brick rectangle in the stone** — a **concealed door** [[Seren Moss|Seren]] pressed open onto a **natural cave system**: branching tunnels and a **ladder descending deeper**, along the fleeing [[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]]'s trail. The tunnels **connect to the mine workings** — a dug-out passage linking toward an existing mineshaft, now documented by the recovered [[Map of the Mines (Cliffside Mansion)|mines map]]'s crudely drawn mines-to-manor path. Down here: the **singed crates that held the [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]] — found empty** (Session 34).
- **Second floor** (swept in Session 34, [[Rhaenyra]] running Detect Magic): a room with **two locked, magical, dragon-egg-sized treasure boxes** (taken by [[Mike]]); a room of **heavy chests** and a closet of traveling supplies and **cultists' robes**; a **swivel bookshelf** concealing a hidden room of further chests — one held sacks of **gold (~5,000–6,000 gp at a glance)**, another the **[[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|cult journal]]**, personal effects, and the **[[Arcane Scroll Case (Cliffside Mansion)|arcane scroll case]]**; a **balcony room** whose door was found slightly ajar — 20–30 ft drop, the whole city visible below, **no landing marks in the mud**.

## Status (post–Session 35)

**Battle over; charred but standing — now a locked-down crime scene.** All cultists at the manor are dead, including (per the [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]] table ruling) the **[[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]]** — he was the kitchen sword-cultist [[Bell]] cleaved in two. Only the [[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|bald mage]] remains **unaccounted for**. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] died on the ground floor and was **revived by Revivify**; his second Fireball **set the building alight** and the party extinguished it. The **eggs are gone** — singed crates empty in the cave below, presumably moved **down the tunnel toward the mines**.

The Session 35 sweep finished the job: sleeping quarters showing a **large group in residence** (~10 gp in the backpacks), the previous owner's **Dwarvish business library** ([[Seren Moss|Seren]] took the mine history and a trading-cities gazetteer), the **[[Tenets of the Ones Forgotten]]** prayer book, and the **~6,000 gp chest** ([[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] took all of it). The party then reported to **[[Captain Grazug]]**, who posted **guards on the manor and atop the tunnel ladder**. As of the next morning: **no movement observed**; the mine side of the tunnel not yet entered by the guard.

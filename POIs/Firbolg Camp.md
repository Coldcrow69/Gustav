# Firbolg Camp

A **firbolg encampment** near the river in [[The Wilds]] — close to (or downstream of) [[Bluefield]]. The party arrived here in the evening after the **dragon attack on [[Bluefield]]** during the [[Bluefield Burning]] arc.

## Confirmed presence

The party docked the **[[Sail la Vie]]** here and made camp on the ship's deck. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] arranged her bedroll near the helm; the survivors rescued from Bluefield (including at least one family with a newborn baby, observed by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] from below deck) were sheltered aboard.

The firbolgs **assisted [[Bluefield]]'s refugees** after the village was destroyed — per [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s later report to [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] during the [[Egg Hunt]] audience.

## Possible location on the map

A **prominent ancient tree with a clustered settlement** is visible in the **north-central forest** on the [[The Wilds|regional map]]. This is the leading candidate for the Firbolg Camp.

## Allies

The firbolgs are a friendly faction to the party — provided sanctuary, sheltered refugees, and per Seren's later coordinating efforts, became **valuable allies in protecting the region** from further dragon assaults.

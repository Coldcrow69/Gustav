# The Apple Thicket

A **diner in [[Fort Elm]]**.

## Notable events

- **9/24/25** ([[Bluefield Burning]] Session 1): The party met [[Victor Barun]] here for a working meal. **[[Rhaenyra]] was introduced** at this meeting, with the news of her fishing village's hydra problem. Victor briefed the party on the side errand to help her village before continuing to [[Stonewater]], and delivered parting gifts ([[Black Thermal Cube]], [[Rope of Mending]], [[Everbright Lantern]], thick winter cloaks).

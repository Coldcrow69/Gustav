# Spit & Spindle

A **tavern on the east side of [[Fort Elm]]**.

**Notable feature**: a **sewer entrance in its back alleyway** — revealed to [[Thorn]] by **[[Soots]]** during Session 6 of the [[Fish People]] arc.

**Knock pattern**: **3 — wait — 3 — wait — 1**.

Used by "a lot of people [who] travel through the sewers" per Thorn. May connect to the broader cavern network under Fort Elm; not yet explored by the party in the recorded sessions.

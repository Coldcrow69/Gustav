# The Gulch

A **gulch in the side of a small hill**, located **~500–600 ft from [[Bell]]'s camp** outside [[Fort Elm]]. Marked by an **old oak tree** atop the hill.

## Discovery

Found by [[Bell]] during Session 7 of the [[Fish People]] arc (8/21/24) while tracking the **webbed prints** that had ransacked her hut. The tracks led northwest, parallel to the town wall, then were lost at 15-20 ft from her camp. Searching further into the forest, Bell located the oak hill and its gulch.

**One of Bell's stolen hunting knives was sitting at the gulch edge.**

## Features

- **Faint drops of water** (not running water) coming from deeper in.
- **Putrid smell** rising from the crevasse.
- Wide enough for **Kuo-Toa and slime monsters to fit through to the deeper part**.

## Use by the party

The gulch became the party's **alternate entry point into the cave system** beneath Fort Elm — used in Session 9 (9/11/24) when they rappelled in, found webbed claw marks on the walls, and eventually descended to the [[The Ancient Ruined City|ancient ruined city]]. This is the route that led them to the [[Riddle Door]], the [[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)|ruined Temple of Tyr]], and ultimately the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]].

# Sour Jackal

> Transcript slip: "Silver Jackal" (corrected at the table). **The party's lodging from [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]] on** — four rooms, ten nights paid.

A tavern in [[Stonewater]]'s **Clock Tower District** — on an upper tier, a few minutes' walk from the pie shop and "surprisingly close" to the jewelers where the party bought its Revivify diamonds. The wooden sign hanging from its post shows a **jackal standing on its hind legs, holding a pint**. Louder and fuller than [[Good For What Ails Ya]] — not belligerent, just crowded with loud conversations. A **downstairs pub** with rooms above. Recommended by [[Dura]].

**Owner / barkeep:** **[[Haldur]]** — an elf of the northern deserts, the first of his kind the party has met.

## Rooms

- **Five silver a night** per room; the party took **all four** available for **ten nights — 20 gp**, paid by [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] from the manor fund. Replacement keys **one gold apiece**. Tab kept open for drinks.
- Slightly larger than the rooms at Good For What Ails Ya; the furnishings are **imported rather than Stonewater-made**, and the beds are a lot nicer — "more fit for travelers."
- **Assignments:** [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] + [[Rhaenyra]]; [[Seren Moss|Seren]] + [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]; [[Bell]] + [[Thorn]]; [[Mike]] alone (he snores, and "he's stinky"). The guard's post is a (borrowed) stool in the hallway between the doors.
- The [[Dragon Eggs|dragon egg]] was **not** stashed here — it stays in Mike's backpack by group decision.

# Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)

> Archivist variants: "Junk-Strewn Corner," "Lakeside Area." [[Stonewater]]'s cavern floor is threaded with lakes and waterways; which lake this corner overlooks is not further specified.

A **junk-strewn corner above a lake** in [[Stonewater]] — heaped refuse in a neglected quarter, overlooked by at least one **run-down, boarded building**.

## Session 32

[[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s tracking spell led her and [[Mike]] here: hidden in the junk sat a **large, pristine wooden box** — conspicuously clean for its surroundings — containing a padded **[[Dragon Eggs|dragon egg]]**. Mike crowbarred it open and packed the egg out. A **[[Dwarven Child (Stonewater Lakeside)|dwarven child]]** watched from a window of the boarded building, about a hundred feet away.

Why an egg was cached here — smuggler's hide or deliberate plant — is an open question (see [[Dragon Eggs]]).

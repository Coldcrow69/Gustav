# Fort Elm Council Chambers

The seat of the **elected council of 10 individuals** that governs [[Fort Elm]] — each over a district of the city.

## Session-recorded events

- **8/21/24** ([[Fish People]] arc, Session 7): [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] accompanied [[Victor Barun]] to argue for sending guards after the underground captives. **The council refused** for fear of opening the city up for other attacks. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] **made a poor impression** on the councilor (per Victor).
- Subsequently the council issued a **curfew** for Fort Elm (town criers announced).
- **9/6/24** (Session 8): The council reversed course on direct intervention — deciding to **hire / ask for volunteers** for the search-and-rescue. [[Lilith]] equipped the party with a [[Lilith's Seal Scroll|safe-passage scroll]].
- After the [[The Undead Aboleth|aboleth]] was slain, the council put a **3,000 gp bounty** on the monster (paid to the party).

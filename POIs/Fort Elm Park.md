# Fort Elm Park

A **park within [[Fort Elm]]** — open enough to walk through and see the stars overhead.

## Significance

**[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Seren Moss|Seren]] shared their first kiss here**, during a quiet evening walk while the party was still based in Fort Elm — between the close of [[Fish People]] and the departure for [[Bluefield Burning]]. The chilled air was unable to quell the heat they shared at every touch; Lyrion's hands framed her face, gentle and light.

The encounter is one of the memories Seren reached for the night she decided she loved him.

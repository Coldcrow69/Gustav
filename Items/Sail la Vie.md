# Sail la Vie

> Name is a pun on *c'est la vie*.

A **magical item** that takes one of two forms:

- **Stored form**: an **ornately decorated mahogany box** (originally described as an inconspicuous wooden briefcase).
- **Deployed form**: a full **ship** — two-masted, ornately carved hull with Celtic/Norse-style knotwork, red-and-white sails with intricate decoration, red pennants flying from the mastheads, small lantern/gem accents strung along the rigging and hull.

## Command words

**"Sail la vie."** The same phrase performs both transformations — speak the words to unfold the box into the ship, speak them again to fold the ship back into the box.

## Capacity (house rule)

The item **traditionally holds 4** by standard rules. **House rule for this campaign**: Sail la Vie can hold the **entire player party** — whether that is 4, 8, or more — regardless of standard limits. (See [[!Crypt Keepers#House Rules|House Rules]].)

## Acquisition

Recovered from the **altar at the back of the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]]** after the slaying of the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]] in the [[Fish People]] arc. [[Teron]] was **guided by [[Mystra]]** to take the ancient offerings; alongside the box he took spell scrolls and a sack of coins totaling 5,400 gp.

**[[Thorn]] recognized the box as a transforming boat** when the party examined the haul together, and took everyone to the docks to see their new vessel.

## First voyage

**9/25/25** ([[Bluefield Burning]] Session 1) — the party set sail on Sail la Vie heading north from [[Fort Elm]] toward [[Roseview]] ([[Rhaenyra]]'s home village). During the journey they docked to investigate hydra sign and **removed the boat from the water** while baiting [[The Hydra]] ashore.

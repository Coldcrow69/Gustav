# Scroll of Chain Lightning

> **In [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s possession**, carefully re-cased.

A spell scroll of **Chain Lightning — a 6th-level evocation** — found inside the [[Arcane Scroll Case (Cliffside Mansion)|arcane scroll case]] recovered from the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] ([[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]; opened in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]], mid-rummage at [[Aiker's Potion Explorium]]).

Written in cramped, tiny "ancient wizard cursive" — channeling magic **beyond anything Lyrion has yet grasped** (the party is level 7; his own ceiling is 4th-level spells). He worked out what it was only by extrapolating from his experience with Witch Bolt, then rolled it up very slowly, smiling.

- **Far more potent than any scroll Lyrion has written.** Its presence among the [[Acolytes of Oghma|cult]]'s effects is unexplained — whose was it?
- Immediately floated as **barter capital** in the Potion-of-Vitality negotiation with [[Aiker]] — then pointedly *not* offered once Lyrion read it.

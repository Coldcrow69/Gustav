# Black Thermal Cube

A **small black cube**, burnt around the edges, **smelling of brimstone**.

## Function

A **portable campfire**. Glows with warmth without burning the hands of whoever holds it.

## Activation

**Elven command word: "Ember"** (must be spoken in Elvish).

## Provenance

Gift from [[Victor Barun]] to the party at [[The Apple Thicket]] on 9/24/25, the day before the [[Bluefield Burning]] departure. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] tossed it over to **[[Bell]]**, who carries it.

## Egg Hunt — the incubator candidate

In [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]] the cube (ambient 80–90°, can't burn) was **favored over Mike's fire as the safer incubator** for the recovered [[Dragon Eggs|dragon egg]] — with a half-serious fear of accidentally hatching it. The idea has **never been revisited**; the egg rides cold in [[Mike]]'s backpack. As of [[Through the Bloodied Rubble - Session 37 - 2026-10-25|Session 37]] the egg has **moved once** — and whether anyone now *wants* it warmer is a question nobody has asked.

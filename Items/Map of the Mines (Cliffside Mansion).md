# Map of the Mines (Cliffside Mansion)

A **copied map of the [[Ironheart Mines]]**, found in a **singed scroll case** (~7" × 2", metal clasp, fabric hinge) on a basement table of the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] during the post-battle search in [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]].

Two additions make it evidence rather than reference:

- A **cavernous space drawn in** beyond the mine workings — a rough but plausible match for the cavern the party has scouted beyond the [[Ironheart Mines|mine]] collapse.
- A **crudely drawn path leading from the mines to the manor itself** — documentary proof that the [[Acolytes of Oghma|cult]]'s dug tunnel under the manor connects to the mine system. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]]: *"I think we may have found who was behind the tunnel behind the collapse."*

**Custody (as of [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]], maintainer-confirmed):** the **original stays with the party** — the Session 35 handover to [[Captain Grazug]] sent only the [[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|cult journal]] into evidence, though the tunnel and its mine connection were reported in full. **[[Gustav]] holds a copy** — made in about two minutes at [[Mike]]'s suggestion.

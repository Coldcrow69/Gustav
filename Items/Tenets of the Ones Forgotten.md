# Tenets of the Ones Forgotten

> Archivist/transcript spelling "Tenants" — maintainer-confirmed **Tenets**. **In [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s possession**, read only at a skim.

A **handbound prayer book** recovered from the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]]'s personal-effects chest and examined in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]. The title page reads **"Tenets of the Ones Forgotten"** — the closest thing yet to a **name for the [[Acolytes of Oghma|cult]]** (or for its parent community in the north).

## Contents (cursory skim)

Doctrine matching the [[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|cult journal]], in liturgical rather than confessional form: the creed that **you do not truly die if you are never forgotten**, wrapped in extensive religious ramblings. Where the journal shows one member's slide into the ideology, this book is the ideology codified — proof the doctrine is **shared scripture**, not one man's madness.

- Handed by [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] to Lyrion on discovery (*"that seems a Lyrion thing"*); joined his growing to-read pile.
- The **journal** went to [[Captain Grazug]] as evidence the same evening; **this book stayed with the party** — the guard was not told of it.
- Deep reading pending.

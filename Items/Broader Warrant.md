# Broader Warrant

A **forged warrant** — [[Captain Grazug]]'s genuine [[Writ of Deputization]] copied and "improved" by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and **[[Gustav]]** into sweeping legalese: authority to **search wherever they want and detain whomever they want, for however long** ([[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]).

Commissioned, remarkably, by **[[Seren Moss|Seren]]** — *"I cannot believe I'm suggesting this"* — before the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] raid. Lyrion: *"Gustav and I do love a small act of forgery here and there."* Forged en route with forgery tools, Gustav doing the copying.

## Use

Presented to the [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|cult Elder]] alongside the genuine writ. The Elder read it for a long moment and handed it back without comment — **whether he spotted the forgery is unknown**; the ambush that followed may or may not be related (and with the Elder confirmed **dead** in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]], the only reader who might have known took the answer with him). Last seen tucked into Lyrion's spellbook.

## Session 35 — withheld from the guard

When [[Seren Moss|Seren]] gave [[Captain Grazug]] her nearly-full account of the raid, the forged warrant was one of **exactly two facts held back** (the other: the party's recovered [[Dragon Eggs|egg]]). The forgery remains **undiscovered — and undisclosed** — even as the captain's investigation into the manor deaths opens around it.

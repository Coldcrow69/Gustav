# Coin of Strange Iconography

A **nearly pristine golden coin** found in the caves beneath [[Fort Elm]] during Session 10 of the [[Fish People]] arc (10/2/24), in the same find as the [[Dagger of Strange Iconography]] and the [[Glyph Scar|post-Glyph War]] battlefield skeletons.

**Detect Magic** pinged the coin: faint **divination** aura.

## Iconography

- **Face**: a **human silhouette with long flowing hair tied into a braid, short features, human ears, draped in a robe or cloak**.
- **Back**: the same crest as on the [[Dagger of Strange Iconography|companion dagger]]'s pommel base — a **tree, half-sprouted above ground and roots below ground**.

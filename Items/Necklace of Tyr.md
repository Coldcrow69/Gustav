# Necklace of Tyr

A **necklace of [[Tyr]]** — religious item from the [[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)|ruined Temple of Tyr]] / cathedral lineage.

Found by **[[Riknozz]]** on a **decomposed Drow corpse** in one of the slime-pool depths during Session 11 of the [[Fish People]] arc (10/9/24), when [[Teron]] was dredging bodies from the pool where he had earlier been dragged. Riknozz **recognized the dead Drow** as his own people; the necklace was kept by him.

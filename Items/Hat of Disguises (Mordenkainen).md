# Hat of Disguises (Mordenkainen)

> Tag text: **"Mordenkainen's magical hat of disguises"** (with a local trademark symbol; transcript garble "Warden Kynan's" — maintainer-confirmed Mordenkainen, the famous mage with several spells to his name). **Attuned to and worn by [[Mike]].**

A **top hat**, surprisingly light (~half a pound), recovered from one of the two locked magical dragon-egg-sized boxes taken from the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]]'s second floor ([[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]; opened in [[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]).

**Property:** while wearing the hat, the wearer can cast **Disguise Self** at will (~10 seconds to change). The spell ends if the hat is removed. The wearer can shift apparent size only so far — Mike's floor is still enormous ("I could be nine foot right here").

- Mike identified it by attuning overnight (per the new table rule: identifying a magic item takes a long rest spent with it), felt an overwhelming urge to put it on, and has worn it since. His conclusion: **"It's a sorcerer's hat."** ([[Thorn]], keeper of the bit: *"All according to plan."*)
- First planned use: the **hot-springs prank** — Mike and Thorn both disguising as [[Rhaenyra]] (who is enthusiastically in on it) to present [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] with multiple Rhaenyras, one of them nine feet tall.
- Its **companion box** held the still-unidentified **[[Black Silk Cloth (Cliffside Mansion)|black silk cloth]]** ([[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] holds it, saving the examination for himself and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] alone).

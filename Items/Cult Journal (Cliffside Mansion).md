# Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)

An **unnamed personal journal** recovered from a chest of personal effects on the second floor of the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] ([[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]). The single best window so far into what the [[Acolytes of Oghma]] actually are.

## Contents

Read aloud by [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], the journal traces one person's slide, in three movements:

1. **Dread.** Early pages of severe existential terror about death and — more precisely — **being forgotten**. It reads like a therapy diary.
2. **Community.** The writer finds **a support group of people who share the same mortality-anxieties**.
3. **Fanaticism.** The writing dissolves into doctrine: ***"you are only truly dead when you are forgotten."*** The worst fate is not dying but being forgotten; the group's answer is remembrance at any cost.

The final stretch records the writer traveling **from the north down into the wilds on a mission from this community to recover something in [[Stonewater]]** *(the journal's "Stoneforge" is the old chronicler garble for Stonewater — confirmed by the maintainer)*.

[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s reading: *"I don't think death was their fear anymore… perhaps all they're out to do now is something so extreme that they'll never be forgotten. That would be their immortality in their eyes. Something that perhaps involves powerful artifacts… dragon eggs."* — and, more personally: *"Father always said that therapy was for hacks. Perhaps he was on to something?"*

## What it establishes

- The cult **began as a mortality-dread support group** and radicalized into a **remembrance/immortality cult** — consistent with the battle cry *"We will not be forgotten!"* from [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]].
- The cult (or at least this member) is **not local**: it sent agents **from the north** into the wilds, with Stonewater as a target.
- The mission was **recovery** of something in Stonewater — whether the [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]] themselves or something the eggs serve is open.

Found alongside: a blank leather-bound clasped book, a small coin pouch, personal items, and the [[Arcane Scroll Case (Cliffside Mansion)|arcane scroll case]].

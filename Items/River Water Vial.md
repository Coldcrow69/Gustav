A small vial of river water from the [[Rollingbrook River]] in [[Fort Elm]]. Carried by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] (gift from [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] during Session 1 of [[Fish People]]).

The river was polluted with slimy and foamy substances, filled with vile fish creatures that turned to slime after their death. The vial itself is not magical — the anomaly is attributed to **a change in the river's ecosystem**, not to the weave.

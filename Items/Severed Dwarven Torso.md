# Severed Dwarven Torso

A disembodied **dwarven torso** — no limbs and no head — found in the revenant's den (a [[Stonewater Sewers|sewer]] utility closet) in [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]]. **Gnawed**, with bite marks and chunks missing (something had been feeding on it); dead **at least a week** ([[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s assessment). Detect Magic found nothing on it.

With no head, it **cannot be reached by Speak with Dead** ([[Seren Moss|Seren]]). It may be a **discarded former host** of the [[Victor Stonehammer]] revenant, or simply one of its kills — unresolved.

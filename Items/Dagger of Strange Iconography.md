# Dagger of Strange Iconography

A **dagger** found in the caves beneath [[Fort Elm]] during Session 10 of the [[Fish People]] arc (10/2/24). Bearing **strange iconography that none of the party recognized**.

**Holder:** [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] (maintainer-confirmed). Its [[Coin of Strange Iconography|companion coin]] is stored in [[Gustav]].

## Discovery

A **strip of cloth** was buried under rocks in the side of the spiral cave path, with a piece of metal glinting beside it — a **completely decayed human skeleton** with **a rusted shield remnant** in its right hand. **The dagger was lodged in the skeleton's ribcage.**

## Crest

**A tree, half-sprouted above ground and roots below ground** — carved into the **pommel base**.

The same crest appears on the [[Coin of Strange Iconography|companion coin]] (found alongside via Detect Magic) and on the **bronze items / weapons / armor of all the skeletons** in the nearby **dome-shaped chamber** — a battlefield with mixed-race dead (mostly human, then elves, a few dwarfs, one or two halflings). The dome's bronze inventory is consistent with the **post-Glyph War transitional period** before iron and steel adoption.

Identity of the crest / faction: **unresolved**. Open lead.

# Facemask Device

An **enchanted air mask** of unknown make — confirmed by [[Lilith]] (Captain of the Guard) during the [[Fish People]] arc to be a **water-breathing device**.

Used by the fish-monsters (kuo-toa) on **kidnapped townsfolk** to keep them alive underwater. The same masks proved useful to the party during their cave incursions, allowing them to fight and rescue underwater.

## Distribution

- Originally **four** recovered from saved hostages — taken by [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Teron]], and one other.
- Later, when the party headed into the forest to find alternate cave entrances via Bell's gulch, **each party member was armed with a water-breathing mask** — implying additional masks were acquired or distributed in the intervening sessions.

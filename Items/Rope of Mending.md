# Rope of Mending

A **spool of rope with an arcane sheen**.

Implied to self-repair (the "mending" name). Specifics not yet recorded in play.

## Provenance

Gift from [[Victor Barun]] to the party at [[The Apple Thicket]] on 9/24/25, the morning of departure for [[Bluefield Burning]]. Handed to **[[Eldrin Thalor]]**, who carries it.

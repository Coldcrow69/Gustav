# Carnivorous Plant Bark Sample

A **small piece of bark** caught in the tuft of [[Mike]]'s belt after the [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|fairy-grove encounter]] east of [[Stonewater]] (Lyrion + Mike, 5/3/26). Discovered the next week at the tavern [[Good For What Ails Ya]] when [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] realized Mike had gone to bed without bathing and searched his clothes for plant residue.

## Significance

- Likely **bark from the carnivorous plant itself** — the angler-fish-style fae-illusion tree at the center of the anti-magic pocket. The plant survives by **siphoning weave-magic** locally; the bark may carry residual magical properties.
- [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] held it up at the table as a useful sample. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] asked whether he could determine **magical properties** from it; Lyrion was non-committal — *"Who could say? I doubt my abilities as a scholar at this point."*

Currently in Lyrion's possession; analysis pending.

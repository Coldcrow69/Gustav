# Everbright Lantern

A **shuttered lantern** whose **flame will never go out**.

## Provenance

Gift from [[Victor Barun]] to the party at [[The Apple Thicket]] on 9/24/25, the day before the [[Bluefield Burning]] departure. Handed to **[[Rhaenyra]]**, who carries it.

## In play

First on-screen use in [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]: arriving mid-battle at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]], Rhaenyra unshielded it over the corpse-strewn foyer.

# Lilith's Seal Scroll

A **scroll bearing [[Lilith]]'s seal**, granting the party **safe passage in and out of [[Fort Elm]]'s gates**.

Given to the party on **9/6/24** during Session 8 of the [[Fish People]] arc, when the council decided to use volunteers for the search-and-rescue and Lilith urged the party to continue their investigations.

## Storage

**Stored in [[Gustav]]** for safekeeping. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] also instructed Gustav to **memorize Lilith's handwriting for future use** — implying Gustav can replicate or verify her hand.

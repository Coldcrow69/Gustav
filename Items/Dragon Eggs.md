# Dragon Eggs

The **stolen eggs of [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]]** — the multi-headed red dragon whose warband destroyed [[Bluefield]] in retaliation. Recovering them is the central objective of the [[Egg Hunt]] arc: per the [[Captured Half-Dragon]]'s testimony, the dragons **will not cease their attacks until the eggs are returned**.

The eggs are the god of the Iron Peaks' **own clutch** — the warband's missing "children" are these unhatched kin, not the captive's offspring.

## Known locations (Session 32 tracking)

[[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s hour-long tracking spell swept [[Stonewater]] in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]]:

- **Recovered: one egg** — found hidden in a **large, pristine wooden box**, packed in protective padding, buried in the [[Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)|lakeside junk pile]]. Pried open by [[Mike]] (crowbar); the egg now rides in **Mike's backpack** (his fur blankets were discarded to make room). A [[Dwarven Child (Stonewater Lakeside)|dwarven child]] witnessed the retrieval.
- **Located, not recovered: two eggs** — at an **abandoned [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|mansion on the cliffside]]**. Not yet approached.
- **Cleared:** the [[Greybraids]]' **manor and forge**, and the **city's single mine entrance** — no egg signals at any of them.

Total clutch size is **unrecorded** — three eggs are accounted for by signal; whether more exist beyond the spell's reach is unknown.

## Open questions

- Who hid the junk-pile egg — a **smuggler's cache**, or a **plant meant to incite conflict between rival factions** if discovered? (Both theories floated by Seren and Mike on the walk back.)
- Who holds the cliffside mansion, and why two eggs are there.
- Whether the [[Greybraids]] — cleared by signal at their known holdings — were ever the thieves at all, or only linked by the [[Basil|second cart]].

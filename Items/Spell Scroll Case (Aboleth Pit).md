# Spell Scroll Case (Aboleth Pit)

A **spell scroll case** retrieved by [[Bell]] from the pit of putrid water at the center of the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth]] — *after* the death of the [[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]].

[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] judged it to be **"very valuable and powerful."**

## Contents

- **Scroll of Protect from Fey** — *copied into [[Gustav]]; consumed*. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] now knows the spell.
- **Scroll of Divination** — *copied into [[Gustav]]; consumed*. Lyrion now knows the spell.

(Wizard scroll-copying mechanic: the scroll is consumed during the transcription process, and the spell becomes part of the wizard's spellbook for future preparation.)

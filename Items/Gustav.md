A named sentient magical codex. Carried by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]. In mechanical terms, Gustav is Lyrion's **Awakened Spellbook** — the signature feature of his **Order of Scribes** wizard subclass — which is what makes the book sentient and opinionated.

**Personality**: reactive and opinionated. When Lyrion writes emotional or romantic prose (e.g., his love letter to [[Seren Moss|Seren]]), Gustav **ruffles his pages and makes disapproving noises**.

> **Out-of-fiction meta-note.** This Obsidian vault — the entire shared campaign notebook — is named **Gustav** after this codex, and the GitHub repository carries the same name. The conceit is that readers (players, friends, public visitors) are turning Gustav's pages when they browse the vault. See the repo `README.md` for the full setup. Treat all of the vault's other entries as marginalia, inserts, pressed flowers, and pasted-in scraps that have accumulated inside the book over the course of the campaign.

## Notable collaborations

- **The [[Broader Warrant]]** ([[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]) — Gustav copied [[Captain Grazug]]'s [[Writ of Deputization]] into a sweeping forged warrant at [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s request. Lyrion: *"Gustav and I do love a small act of forgery here and there."* (Gustav declines to record his own opinion of this entry.)
- Contains Lyrion's **measurements and sketches of the recovered [[Dragon Eggs|dragon egg]]** (Session 33).
- **Witnessed Lyrion's death** at the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] — floating at his side through the battle; Lyrion was reaching for something in Gustav's pages as he fell. *(Outcome, per [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]: [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s Revivify brought him back. Gustav thanks readers for the condolences he declined to accept.)*
- **Copied the [[Map of the Mines (Cliffside Mansion)|mines map]]** ([[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16|Session 34]]) — the cult's mine map with its drawn-in cavern and mines-to-manor path, duplicated in about two minutes at [[Mike]]'s suggestion, so the party keeps the intelligence when the original goes to the authorities. Gustav is, by his own account, "quite adept" at this. Also consulted mid-fire on the finer points of firefighting.
- **Went to the spa** ([[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]]) — Lyrion would not be parted from him "given our current situation," so Gustav took the full works: carried through the hot-stone massage, **submerged entirely in the mud bath** (no sound, no reaction; he does not need to breathe), rinsed, and carried dripping by his tassel to the lake. At the **Stonewater Plunge** he came alert for the first time all day — eyes darting between the ice water and Lyrion, pages furrowed in an unmistakable *do not* — moments before Eldrin's Mage Hand settled the matter. Gustav's opinion of the outcome is not recorded. Later that night he was **consulted for the troupe's name** when the whole table had forgotten it: the [[Twilight Masqueraders]].

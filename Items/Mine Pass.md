# Mine Pass

A pass authorizing entry to the **[[Ironheart Mines]]**. Issued by **[[Baldrin Stoneforge]]** in his capacity as foreman.

## Status — issued (Session 32)

[[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]] told the party at the close of [[The Search for Dragon Eggs - Session 29 - 2026-05-10|Session 29]] that he would be at his house **the next morning** to provide a mine pass. The pickup was skipped in [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]] (the revenant intervened). In [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]], [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], [[Rhaenyra]], and [[Bell]] were received at Baldrin's manor and issued the paperwork — **writs authorizing entry to the [[Ironheart Mines]]** — then escorted through the mine checkpoints to the collapsed tunnel.

## Caution

[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] flagged that **Baldrin himself may not be trustworthy** despite presenting as the wronged employer. The pass should be treated as a useful instrument, not as evidence of Baldrin's good faith.

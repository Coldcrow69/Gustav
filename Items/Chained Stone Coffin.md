# Chained Stone Coffin

An improvised **stone coffin** with a fitted, hinged lid, created by [[Rhaenyra]] with **Stone Shape** (her only 4th-level spell slot that day) to contain the **revenant** of [[Victor Stonehammer]] in the [[Stonewater Sewers]] ([[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21|Session 31]]).

The bound, blindfolded revenant was set on the stone; the lid was grown over it and **chained and locked shut**, with the chain **spiked to the floor**. It was never meant to hold permanently — the party expected the revenant to break free in time.

## Status

Inside, the creature snapped its ropes, then fell silent. On opening, the coffin held only a **moving liquid** — the host body had **dissolved**, the revenant not yet ended. The coffin **currently holds the liquefied remains** (Session 31 cliffhanger).

# Writ of Deputization

Official documentation issued by **[[Captain Grazug]]** of the [[Stonewater]] city guard in [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19|Session 32]], **deputizing a named four-person team** — [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Mike]], and [[Thorn]] — for the supernatural-murder investigation. Drawn up at the third-tier Barracks in about fifteen to twenty minutes. Carried by Lyrion.

## Uses so far

- **[[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|Greybraid Manor]] gate** ([[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02|Session 33]]) — presented as authority for cave-in questions; the clan guard examined it and found it legitimate.
- **[[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|Cliffside manor]]** (Session 33) — presented to the [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|cult Elder]] together with the forged **[[Broader Warrant]]** Lyrion and [[Gustav]] copied from it.

> The party has been reading the writ's scope generously — it was issued for the murder investigation, and is being brandished for the cave-in and the egg hunt as well.

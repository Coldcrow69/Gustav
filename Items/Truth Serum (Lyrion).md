# Truth Serum (Lyrion)

> **In [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s possession.** First mentioned on-page in [[Through the Bloodied Rubble - Session 37 - 2026-10-25|Session 37]]; **maintainer-confirmed as a real inventory item**. The quotation marks are the maintainer's: a *"truth serum."*

A potion Lyrion bought **weeks ago** (not "months" — his own correction), **super cheap**, from a **merchant who seemed shady** — where, exactly, he no longer remembers. He has been **trying to get someone to drink it** ever since; by his own admission to [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], *"this could kill someone,"* which is why he hasn't tested it on a friend — or on himself.

- **Session 37:** on the walk down to the mine breach he offered [[Dura]] **five gold** to drink "a potion that at worst would give you an upset stomach." Dura: *"Not right now… maybe if you get me drunk enough. At least buy me a beer first."* [[Thorn]], unasked: *"I have done a lot worse for a lot less."* Eldrin's suggestion — pour it into [[Mike]]'s mead — was declined on the grounds that Lyrion doesn't want to kill Mike.
- Whether it does anything at all is unknown. Lyrion's framing to Eldrin was "the truth serum"; his framing to Dura omitted the word.

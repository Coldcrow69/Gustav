# Spark Beasts

> Source: [[Lyrion's Research Journal]], Entry 6.

Massive creatures **infused with magic from leaks in [[The Weave]]**. Mutated and dangerous. Often appear **near areas where the Weave has been significantly damaged** — i.e., where the [[Glyph Scar]]'s damage is most pronounced.

Encountering a Spark Beast is a **perilous event** — their magical nature makes them unpredictable and deadly.

Lyrion's elders told him stories of them.

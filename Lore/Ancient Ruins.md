# Ancient Ruins (Displaced)

> Source: [[Lyrion's Research Journal]], Entry 4.

Ruins of ancient civilizations that appear to be from **different places in the world** — as if the ruin was **displaced** and shouldn't be there.

These ruins are remnants of a time when [[The Weave]] was **whole and unbroken**. They hold secrets that are yet to be uncovered.

The displacement is presumed to be a side effect of the [[Glyph Scar]].

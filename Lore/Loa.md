# Loa

A **spirit** that can attach to a person — context: **firbolg clan spirits** invoked through the [[Tome of Firbolg Spirit Communion|ritual of spirit communion]] practiced by the clan at the [[Refugee Camp]].

## In play

**[[Bell]] is bonded to [[Zehal]]** — her Loa — acquired during the firbolg communion ritual the party performed in the woods near the refugee camp. The ritual completed and "a spirit spoke [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s name"; subsequent sessions reference "the loa that's attached to Belle."

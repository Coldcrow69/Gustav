# Glyph Spark

> **Colloquial / in-character name** for the [[Glyph Scar]]. The form Lyrion's elders used when telling him stories of the cataclysm; recorded as such in [[Lyrion's Research Journal]] (Entry 3). The canonical / scholarly term per the broader world is **[[Glyph Scar]]**.

## What Lyrion's elders said

*Many hundreds of years ago, there was a massive war that engulfed the land called the Glyph Spark. Magic users destroyed much of the land in massive battles that abused the weave and its power. The weave used to be stronger before this calamity. Now, it seems to be getting stronger but is obviously still broken. The Glyph Spark was fought between many powerful arch-mages from different nations who went to war, and their magic caused the calamity.*

## Reconciliation with the canonical record

The DM's world-doc clarifies and corrects several details Lyrion's elders had only roughly:

- **Timing**: "many hundreds of years ago" → narrowed to roughly **700–900 years ago**, with the historical record intentionally ambiguous (see [[Glyph Scar]])
- **Combatants**: "many powerful arch-mages" → specifically **eight mighty archmages**
- **Name**: "Glyph Spark" → **Glyph Scar** in the scholarly record

See [[Glyph Scar]] for the full canonical account, including the legacy phenomena ([[Floating Islands]], [[Spark Beasts]], [[Ancient Ruins]]) and the implications for [[The Weave]] and magic-user demographics in [[Azadin]].

# The Weave

> Source: [[Lyrion's Research Journal]], Entries 1–2.

A network of magical threads connecting the world and its inhabitants to the arcane forces that permeate reality. An **invisible series of leylines** around the world that **carry magic**. These leylines **leak** magic into the world around them all the time, which is how magic users can pull power from them to cast spells.

The Weave is currently **broken**. It used to be stronger before the [[Glyph Scar]] calamity (roughly 700–900 years ago — ambiguous; called the [[Glyph Spark]] in Lyrion's earlier journal entries). Now it seems to be getting stronger but is obviously still damaged.

**In [[Azadin]] today:** pre-Scar the setting was extremely high magic; post-Scar it has slowly slowed to **low/mid magic levels**. Individuals with the skill or knowledge to manipulate the Weave are **rarer than in standard fantasy settings (~1/90 people, vs ~1/30 in baseline D&D)** and are treated with a small degree of **stigma**.

## Phenomena

- **Leaks**: some leylines have **massive leaks**, permeating the surrounding area with so much magic that it distorts their surroundings. This phenomenon morphs the local flora and fauna and causes **wild magic surges** — unpredictable and dangerous.
- **Spark Beasts**: see [[Spark Beasts]].
- **Floating rocks and islands**: see [[Floating Islands]].
- **Displaced ancient ruins**: see [[Ancient Ruins]].

## Magic users

Different classes draw on the Weave in different ways:

- **Wizards** (like [[Lyrion Thalor]]): pull on the magic of the world and manipulate it
- **Priests / clerics** (like [[Seren Moss]]): draw on the magic of gods
- **Sorcerers**: harness the wild nature of the Weave
- **Warlocks**: bargain with dangerous entities for power

## Lyrion's open questions (research)

- Can local "leaks" in the Weave be repaired? If so, **should** they?
- Can the power of the Weave be redirected back into itself? What effect might this have?
- Is there a material which can **insulate** from the Weave to allow for isolated testing?
- Is there a material which **attracts or amplifies** the magic of the Weave?
- Can the magic of the Weave be **captured and saved** for later use?
- Can the magic of the Weave be used to imbue items, foods, potions? (Perhaps at large leaks magic can be channeled to empower mundane items.)
- Can the Weave **create life of its own** — can a magical being exist without first being a biological entity?
- What **medicinal properties** might the magic of the Weave have when used by a healer?
- Can a **device** be created to measure the flow of the Weave locally?

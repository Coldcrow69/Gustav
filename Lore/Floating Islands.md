# Floating Islands

> Source: [[Lyrion's Research Journal]], Entry 5.

A phenomenon observed in the world: **floating rocks and islands that seem to defy gravity, drifting freely across the skies.**

These floating masses are believed to be **remnants of the [[Glyph Scar]]'s cataclysmic magic**, infused with arcane energies that allow them to hover in defiance of natural laws.

# The Glyph Scar

> **Canonical name** (per DM world-doc). [[Glyph Spark]] is the colloquial / in-character name used by [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s elders and recorded in [[Lyrion's Research Journal]] — same event.

A **cataclysmic war** that took place in [[Azadin]] **roughly 700–900 years ago** — **the historical record is intentionally ambiguous** because the war itself wiped out most of its own records, and the world is still recovering. The 700–900 year window is the best modern estimate; no narrower date is reliably known.

## The combatants

**Eight mighty archmages** clashed in a titanic struggle that shook the foundations of [[The Weave]], leaving behind a scar that forever changed the course of magic itself. They came from different nations and went to war; their magic caused the calamity.

## The aftermath

- Magic was **fractured**. [[The Weave]] used to be stronger before the Scar; now it exists in **fragments**, recovering slowly but still broken.
- The **skies shimmer with iridescent hues**, dotted with [[Floating Islands]] — gravity-defying remnants infused with the war's arcane energies.
- **Thunderous peaks and deep valleys** mark the landscape — formed by the magical violence.
- Creatures were **mutated by the unstable magic** — see [[Spark Beasts]].
- **Ancient ruins** appear displaced — civilizations whose remnants seem to belong somewhere else entirely (see [[Ancient Ruins]]).
- Pre-Scar [[Azadin]] was an **extremely high-magic** setting; post-Scar it sits at **low/mid magic** levels.
- Magic users are rarer and treated with **stigma** (~1/90 people in Azadin vs ~1/30 in standard D&D worlds).
- **[[Fort Elm]] and [[The Wilds]] as a whole** were among the **few places not completely ravaged** by the Glyph Scar — preserving history and stories that have been wiped elsewhere.

## Records

**Records of the Glyph Scar are rare and far between** — most were wiped from the face of Azadin during the war itself. Recovering them is an open avenue for arcanists and adventurers.

## Open questions (Lyrion's research)

- What caused the calamity?
- What began the **feuds between the archmages**?
- What was the **final spell** that fractured reality?
- Can the local leaks in [[The Weave]] left by the Scar be repaired? *Should* they?

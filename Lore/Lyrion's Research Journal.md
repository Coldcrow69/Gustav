# Lyrion's Research Journal

> Author: [[Lyrion Thalor]]. An ongoing record begun at the start of the campaign.

This journal represents the culmination of Lyrion's initial knowledge and understanding of magic as he begins his journey into the wider world. There is much to learn and discover, and he will document his findings meticulously, seeking to understand the mysteries of [[The Weave]] and the magical forces that shape reality.

---

## Entry 1: Foundations of Magic

*I am a wizard. I can pull on the magic of our world and manipulate it to create spells and effects. This, I know. From a young age, I was taught that magic comes from "the weave," an invisible series of leylines around the world that carry magic. These leylines leak magic into the world around them all the time, which is how magic users can pull power from them to cast spells.*

*I will begin by seeking out other magic users and comparing our experiences. I should like to discover the knowledge held by others who use magic differently than I do. I hear tell of priests who draw on the magic of gods, sorcerers who harness the wild nature of the weave, and warlocks who bargain with dangerous entities for power. They should all have insights that I do not, and would be valuable to me.*

## Entry 2: The Weave and Leylines

See [[The Weave]] for the full reference.

*The weave is a network of magical threads, connecting the world and its inhabitants to the arcane forces that permeate our reality. Some leylines have massive leaks, permeating the surrounding area with so much magic that it distorts their surroundings. This phenomenon morphs the local flora and fauna, causing wild magic surges that can be unpredictable and dangerous.*

## Entry 3: The Calamity of the Glyph Spark

See [[Glyph Spark]] for the full reference.

*Many hundreds of years ago, there was a massive war that engulfed the land called the Glyph Spark. Magic users destroyed much of the land in massive battles that abused the weave and its power. The weave used to be stronger before this calamity. Now, it seems to be getting stronger but is obviously still broken. The Glyph Spark was fought between many powerful arch-mages from different nations who went to war, and their magic caused the calamity.*

## Entry 4: Ancient Ruins and Displacement

See [[Ancient Ruins]] for the full reference.

*In my studies, I have learned that there are ruins found of ancient civilizations that appear to be from different places in the world, as if the ruin was displaced and shouldn't be there. These ruins are remnants of a time when the weave was whole and unbroken, and they hold secrets that are yet to be uncovered.*

## Entry 5: Floating Rocks and Islands

See [[Floating Islands]] for the full reference.

*Another phenomenon observed in our world is the existence of floating rocks and islands that seem to defy gravity, drifting freely across the skies. These floating masses are believed to be remnants of the Glyph Spark's cataclysmic magic, infused with arcane energies that allow them to hover in defiance of natural laws.*

## Entry 6: Spark Beasts

See [[Spark Beasts]] for the full reference.

*My elders told me stories of the Spark Beasts, massive creatures infused with magic from leaks in the weave. These beasts are mutated and dangerous, often appearing near areas where the weave has been significantly damaged. Encountering a Spark Beast is a perilous event, as their magical nature makes them unpredictable and deadly.*

*I have a small vial of river water from the [[Rollingbrook River]] in [[Fort Elm]] — this river was polluted with slimy and foamy substances, filled with vile fish creatures that turned to slime after their death. ## Ideas for Future Study

- Can local "leaks" in [[The Weave]] be repaired? If so, **should** they?
- Can the power of the Weave be redirected back into itself? What effect might this have?
- Is there a material which can **insulate** from the Weave to allow for isolated testing?
- Is there a material which **attracts or amplifies** magic of the Weave?
- Can the magic of the Weave be **captured and saved** for later use?
- Can the magic of the Weave be used to **imbue items**, foods, potions? Perhaps at large leaks magic can be channeled to empower mundane items.
- Can the Weave **create life of its own** — can a magical being exist without first being a biological entity?
- What **medicinal properties** might the magic of the Weave have when used by a healer?
- Can a **device** be created to measure the flow of the Weave locally?
- If I obtain a device to **divine the stars**, can they tell us more about our reality?
- **Fortune tellers** who can use cards to divine the future — is it legitimate?
- What caused the calamity? What began the feuds between the archmages, and what was the final spell that fractured our reality?

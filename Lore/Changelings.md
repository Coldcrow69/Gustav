# Changelings

> Player-sheet race: **Changeling**. In-world, this lineage is known as **Shapeshifters** — the term the [[Drow Elder]] and [[Riknozz]]'s people use. The two names refer to the same thing: "Shapeshifter" is the in-fiction lore word, "Changeling" the rules term.

A lineage of **shape-changers** able to alter their appearance at will. In [[Azadin]] they are called **Shapeshifters** and are treated as outcasts.

## What the [[Drow Elder]] told the party

- Shapeshifters are a group of **elves banished from both Drow and elf societies**, considered **cursed by the gods**.
- The trait **can skip generations** — a Shapeshifter can be born to non-Shapeshifter parents, who often abandon the child at birth.
- A Shapeshifter's **true form is somewhat Drow-like**.
- They are rare and scattered; the Drow Elder had only ever heard stories of them from her own father.

## In the vault

- **[[Thorn]]** is the only known Shapeshifter in the campaign. Her true form is grey-skinned with glowing pink/red eyes and Drow-like pointed ears (see her page). Her heritage was revealed by the [[Drow Elder]] during the [[Fish People]] arc; she has never knowingly met another of her kind, and continues to research her lineage (the Bard / Clown College trip during the post-Fish-People downtime).

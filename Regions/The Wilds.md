A region of [[Azadin]]. A large expanse of jungle on the southern end of a larger, unknown continent. The Wilds are dotted with small isolated settlements, and they are largely cut off from the wider world.

## Description (DM world-doc)

Lush and dense **woodlands**, punctuated by **rolling hills** and **imposing mountains** — a tapestry of nature's grandeur that defies taming. The air is thick with the intoxicating scents of **wildflowers and ancient trees**; the ground beneath one's feet seems to **pulse with an energy** that hints at the enchantment coursing through every fiber of this untamed expanse.

## Sky / atmospheric phenomenon

The skies over The Wilds — particularly near the [[Iron Peaks]] — display **iridescent lenticular cloud formations**: huge sweeping curls of color banded in pink, lavender, peach, mint, and gold, arcing across the horizon at dawn or dusk. The light over a still mountain lake reflects the spectrum back in muted pastel. This is the **atmospheric signature** of the magic-rich region — visible from far enough to be a recognizable navigation cue and consistent with the "unstable magic that litters the rest of [[Azadin]]" bleeding through into the natural environment here.

Here, **nature's forces intertwine with the arcane**, causing nature to take on a life of its own. **Creatures both familiar and fantastical** roam the woods, their forms often touched by the unstable magic that litters the rest of [[Azadin]] (see [[Spark Beasts]]).

Many other towns and cities dot The Wilds, drawn by the region's enchanting call.

## Government

The Wilds have **no central government**. The individual cities have formed many pacts and dealings, producing an **alliance** between them.

## Historical note

Per the [[Glyph Scar]] section of the DM world-doc, [[Fort Elm]] and The Wilds **as a whole** were among the **few places not completely ravaged** by the Glyph war — meaning the region preserves history and stories that have been wiped from the rest of Azadin.

## Travel wisdom

- Some parts of the forest are more dangerous than others; common wisdom is to **stick to the less dense sections** and **avoid deep undergrowth**.
- The hills and mountains hide many caves and crags. Many myths and folktales warn passing visitors and children to **steer far clear**.

## Geography (from regional map)

The Wilds occupy a roughly **kite-shaped** stretch of land bordered by:

- **North**: the [[Iron Peaks]], whose mountain wall **wraps around the entire northern edge** of the region — a high rocky barrier between The Wilds and whatever lies further north.
- **Southwest** and **south**: **ocean / coastline**. The Wilds meets the sea along its bottom-left and bottom-right margins, with sandy beach lining the coast.
- **East** and **west**: forested transitions into territory not yet mapped.

**Interior features visible on the map:**

- A **river system** drains south through the region — multiple tributaries converge from the mountains, then split and re-fork through the central forest before reaching the southern coast. Bridges cross the rivers at several points.
- A **central fortified settlement** sits at the convergence of two rivers in the interior — the position matches [[Fort Elm]]'s description as a town "at the fork of two rivers."
- **Multiple smaller settlements** are scattered across the region — riverside villages, hill camps, and walled hamlets. Most are unnamed in the vault thus far; candidates for future arcs.
- A **prominent ancient tree** with a clustered settlement around it stands in the north-central forest — a candidate location for the **firbolg** community that aided [[Bluefield]]'s refugees during [[Bluefield Burning]].
- A **small mountain lake with a lone tower** sits among the northern peaks — landmark unidentified.
- A **structure set into the western mountains** at the base of the Iron Peaks — the location matches [[Stonewater]]'s description as the "Dwarf fortress at the SW base of The Iron Peaks."
- A **large tower/lighthouse complex** sits at the southeastern coastal tip — possibly a port or watch-tower city; not yet visited.
- A **central watchtower / battle-marker** in the mid-forest — unidentified.

## Major rivers

- **[[Tortoise River]]** — flows **west to east**, passing [[Bluefield]] and the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]], then continues east past the elf city of [[Zadrid]] and empties into the ocean.
- **[[Winternora Channel]]** — flows **north to south**, emptying into the ocean at its delta where the coastal city of [[Autumnbreak]] sits.
- **[[Rollingbrook River]]** — runs through [[Fort Elm]] (one of two rivers at the town's fork).

## Notable forests

- **[[Plainmore Woods]]** — forest area directly **west of [[Fort Elm]]**. Subject of [[A Brief Delve into the History of Plainmore Woods]].

## Notable Cities

[[Fort Elm]] — Our campaign began here. A primarily human trading post in the interior of the wilds located at the fork of 2 rivers. One of the larger settlements in The Wilds. *Visible on the map as the central fortified settlement at the river fork.*
[[Stonewater]] — A Dwarf fortress at the SW base of [[Iron Peaks|The Iron Peaks]]. Major city and power in the region. *Likely the structure set into the western mountain wall on the map.*
[[Bluefield]] — A small village along the river in The Wilds. The party found this village on fire and besieged by a dragonkin warband, looking for stolen red dragon eggs.
[[Roseview]] — A small fishing village; [[Rhaenyra]]'s home. North of Fort Elm, en route toward Stonewater.
[[Zadrid]] — An elf city along the [[Tortoise River]], east of [[Bluefield]] and the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]].
[[Autumnbreak]] — A coastal city at the delta of the [[Winternora Channel]]. Origin of [[Basil]]'s caravan and (loosely) of the [[Greybraids]]-emblem cart that traveled alongside it on the road to [[Stonewater]].

Aliases: **The Iron Peaks**.

A mountainous region known for its rugged terrain and the [[Ironheart Mines]]. Rocky landscape with natural formations and possibly carved structures. Local inhabitants include dwarves, notably [[Baldrin Stoneforge]] (foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]]).

## Position relative to The Wilds

The Iron Peaks **wrap around the entire north side of [[The Wilds]]**, forming the region's northern border. The mountain wall runs east–west across the top of the map, with several rivers issuing from its slopes and draining south into the Wilds' forest interior.

## Notable features

- [[Stonewater]] — a Dwarf fortress at the **SW base** of the Iron Peaks — serves as a central settlement within the range. (On the regional map: the structure set into the western mountains.)
- **A mountain lake with a lone tower** among the northern peaks (visible on the [[The Wilds|regional map]]) — landmark unidentified.

## Recent events

Destruction of [[Bluefield]] by the **multi-headed red dragon** [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]] (epithet "god of the Iron Peaks"). Discussions at [[Baldrin Stoneforge]]'s residence in [[Stonewater]] have covered the involvement of a [[Captured Half-Dragon]] in the attack and the need to locate stolen dragon eggs to prevent further assaults. Inquiries about recent disappearances and murders linked to the [[Ironheart Mines]] suggest a possible magical cause.

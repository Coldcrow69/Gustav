# Azadin

> The world. The setting of this campaign.

A realm where the echoes of a cataclysmic war — the **[[Glyph Scar]]** — still reverberate through the very fabric of reality. Once, **eight mighty archmages** clashed in a titanic struggle that shook the foundations of [[The Weave]], leaving behind a scar that forever changed the course of magic.

In the aftermath, Azadin bears the indelible marks of that ancient conflict:

- The **skies shimmer with iridescent hues**, dotted with [[Floating Islands|floating crags of rock and stone]].
- **Thunderous peaks and deep valleys** stand as testament to the power that once surged through the world's veins.
- Magic, once boundless and seamless, now exists in **fractured fragments**. Its flow is disrupted, its manifestations unpredictable.
- Creatures **mutated by unstable magic** roam the wilds (see [[Spark Beasts]]).
- Arcanists study tirelessly to decipher the cryptic remnants of spells, seeking to mend the rifts and restore the essence of magic to what it used to be.

Amidst this recovering equilibrium, a tapestry of civilizations thrives. Towering spires of forgotten knowledge stand as testament to an era of unbridled magic. Fledgling adventurers seek ancient artifacts that hold the promise of unlocking Azadin's mysteries (see [[Ancient Ruins]]), while **secretive orders guard the fragile threads** that bind magic and reality.

## Key facts

- **Pre-Scar Azadin** was an extremely high-magic setting.
- **Post-Scar Azadin** is **low/mid magic** and has been slowly recovering.
- The Glyph Scar took place **roughly 700–900 years ago** — the historical record is intentionally ambiguous; see [[Glyph Scar]]. Society has recovered to a good level of normality since.
- **Individuals capable of manipulating [[The Weave]]** are rare and **treated with a small degree of stigma**. (For comparison: standard D&D ~1/30 people; Azadin ~1/90.)
- **Records of the Glyph Scar are rare and far between** — most were wiped from the face of Azadin during the war itself.

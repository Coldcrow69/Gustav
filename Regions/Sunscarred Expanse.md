# Sunscarred Expanse

> First named in [[Healing Waters, Heavy Memories - Session 36 - 2026-09-27|Session 36]], when [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s history check placed the [[Sour Jackal]]'s barkeep. **Unvisited.**

A **desert region to the north, above [[The Wilds]]** — on the far side of the [[Iron Peaks]], whose wall closes off the Wilds' entire northern edge. Home to **elven tribes** known in the Wilds only through traders' stories: a people with much **darker complexions** than the high elves and **natural, flowing line-markings** across the skin. [[Haldur]], owner of the Sour Jackal in [[Stonewater]], is the first of them the party has met.

Earlier breadcrumb: the previous owner of the [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]] kept gazetteers of **trading cities in the wilds and "the desert to the north"** ([[Tension and Triumph in Snow - Session 35 - 2026-08-30|Session 35]]) — one of which [[Seren Moss|Seren]] now carries. Whether that desert is the Sunscarred Expanse is likely but unconfirmed.

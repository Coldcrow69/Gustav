# Prologue

**World:** [[Azadin]] — a recovering, post-cataclysm setting still scarred by the [[Glyph Scar]].
**Setting:** [[Fort Elm]] — a primarily human trading post in the interior of [[The Wilds]], built at the fork of two rivers (the [[Rollingbrook River]] runs through it).

## Session 1 Hook (DM)

> *The party has been individually invited to a little social gathering through a mutual friend, a man by the name of [[Victor Barun]]. You all arrive to find quite a few people had been invited to the Braun household, taken aback seeing several **figureheads of [[Fort Elm]]** walk in through the front door ahead of you. Food and drinks flow as you all settle in for a long night of mingling with the guests.*

## Session 1 Info (DM)

- [[Victor Barun]] is a **middle-aged man, kind and jolly fellow**. Always one to help in a time of need.
- Mr. Barun runs a **small fishing operation** and is a popular figurehead in the town.
- His household is in the **northern part of the city, in a nicer part of town**. The household itself is covered in flowering ivy and greenery; its placement on the river indicates that Braun may just be a little bit more well off than he lets on.

## Inciting event

The "little social gathering" turned out to be [[Victor Barun]]'s **retirement party** — he was handing off control of his fishing business to [[Evelyn]]. Six individuals were invited; several **figureheads of Fort Elm** also attended. [[Thorn]]'s performing troupe (the **Twilight Masqueraders**, including [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Eldrin Thalor]]) was engaged to provide entertainment. After their set, Lyrion and Eldrin left; Thorn and the others were invited to stay after for a private talk with Victor.

That talk never happened: **Victor's body was found** at the mansion. Commotion and mystery ensued. The investigation that followed became the opening session of the [[Fish People]] arc — which would in turn reveal that the corpse was a doppelganger, that Victor was still alive, and that something far stranger than embezzlement was happening beneath [[Fort Elm]].

## The party assembles

The future [[!Crypt Keepers|Crypt Keepers]] is assembled around this incident — each PC individually invited as a mutual friend of Victor's:

- [[Bell]] was camping south of [[Fort Elm]]
- [[Teron]] was training on the east side of the city
- [[Seren Moss|Seren]] was spending time in the shrines of [[Kelemvor]] doing charity
- [[Thorn]], [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] were performing at the mansion

## DM expectations for the campaign

- Backstories will be touched on and fleshed out through play.
- A mix of **exploration and mystery** as the party discovers more about the world and what is going on in [[The Wilds]].
- A **slow descent into horror**.
- Lots of fun and interesting creatures — a slightly more **whimsical world**.

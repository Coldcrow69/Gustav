# Egg Hunt

**Arc location:** [[Stonewater]] (and the [[Ironheart Mines]]); the [[Iron Peaks]] region.
**Status:** Active.
**Central mystery:** Recover the **stolen dragon eggs** of [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]] before further settlements are destroyed. Investigate disappearances and murders linked to the [[Ironheart Mines]].
**Arc span:** Begins when the party **leaves the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]]** (~3/1/26) and heads south toward [[Stonewater]]; runs through current events in Stonewater. The opening **travel leg** — the [[Iron Peaks]] foothills, the [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|anti-magic fairy grove]], and the road-meeting with [[Basil]]'s caravan (logged 3/15/26–5/3/26 in [[Bluefield Burning]] for continuity) — precedes the party's arrival in Stonewater at **Session 29**, the first session recorded in this vault. See [[Campaign Arcs]] for all arc boundaries.

## Session 29 — The Search for Dragon Eggs (2026-05-10)

> First **recorded** session of the [[Egg Hunt]] arc — the party arrives in [[Stonewater]]. (The arc's earlier travel leg from the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]] is summarized in [[Bluefield Burning]].) **Campaign Session 29** overall — the party migrated note-taking platforms at this point, so this is the first session captured in this Obsidian vault. Earlier sessions are summarized in [[Fish People]] and [[Bluefield Burning]].

Full recap: [[The Search for Dragon Eggs - Session 29 - 2026-05-10]] (in `Recaps/`).

In brief: The party arrives in [[Stonewater]] and gains audience with [[Baldrin Stoneforge]], foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]] and business associate of [[Victor Barun]]. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] reports the destruction of [[Bluefield]] by the **multi-headed red dragon** [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]] and the [[Captured Half-Dragon]]'s testimony, requesting aid locating the stolen eggs. The party also presses Baldrin on the recent mine disappearances and murders — manner of death, suspect's vanished corpse, the feather-emblem insignia [[Rhaenyra]] spotted on travelers, and a suspicious caravan lost in the city. [[Thorn]] shapeshifts to demonstrate the cart-driver suspect's appearance. The group agrees to reconvene the following day.

[[Mike]] and [[Lyrion Thalor]] arrive at the tavern [[Good For What Ails Ya]] ahead of the party. Mike collapses upstairs; Lyrion drinks sparkler-cocktails, laments a lost "wizard battle" against an anti-magic zone + magical instability, and gets sick. [[Thorn]] (still disguised as Lyrion) tends to him in the alley with Mage Hand. Group orders dinner, secures rooms, plans to resume in the morning.

## Session 30 — Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas (2026-05-17)

Full recap: [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-17]] (in `Recaps/`).

In brief: morning at [[Good For What Ails Ya]]. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] secretly healed [[Mike]] overnight. Over breakfast, [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Mike]] recount the [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|fairy-grove encounter]] in full — sheep transformation, carnivorous-plant lure, narrow escape. Lyrion recovers a **[[Carnivorous Plant Bark Sample|bark sample]]** from Mike's belt; Mike rebrands himself "Mike the Sorcerer" (still mechanically a Monk). [[Seren Moss|Seren]] sketches the **feather-and-dagger insignia** for the barkeep **[[Nastrin]]**, who identifies it as belonging to the **[[Greybraids]]**, a dwarf clan in [[Stonewater]]. [[Thorn]] connects the mine murders to **cave-in survivors** who likely saw something they should not have. Party plans to split: pair to approach the Greybraids subtly, rest to investigate the mines after picking up a [[Mine Pass|mine pass]] from [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] requests a disguise from [[Thorn]]; [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] step into the alley and have a long emotional reconciliation about their childhood and their father. As they're leaving, a frightened scream draws them to a **[[Dwarven Woman (Stonewater Pickaxe Body)|dwarven woman]] crouched over a pickaxe-killed body**.

## Session 31 — Unearthing Dark Secrets (2026-06-21)

Full recap: [[Unearthing Dark Secrets - Session 31 - 2026-06-21]] (in `Recaps/`).

In brief (continuous with the Session 30 cliffhanger): the pickaxe body is a **cave-in survivor**, husband of the [[Dwarven Woman (Stonewater Pickaxe Body)|dwarven woman]]. [[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s **Speak with Dead** reveals the killer is a rotting-skinned dwarf, "**Victor**," and that the survivors had **seen "people in the cave."** A guard names the suspect — **[[Victor Stonehammer]]**, already cornered and killed by the **[[Stoneforge Clan|Stoneforge Guard]]**, his corpse vanished — now returned. Following a **[[Black Ichor|black-ichor]]** trail into the **[[Stonewater Sewers]]**, the party fights the thing: a **revenant**. Unable to Banish it (none had the spell prepared), they bind it, [[Rhaenyra]] grows a **[[Chained Stone Coffin|stone coffin]]** (Stone Shape) around it, and [[Mike]] fetches an [[Haceran Leadbreaker|elderly cleric]] from the **[[Temple of Selûne (Stonewater)|Temple of Selûne]]** — who also can't Banish it. Opened, the coffin holds only **moving liquefied remains**. The mine murders are revealed as the revenant's **vengeance on cave-in survivors**, decoupling them from the [[Greybraids]] (still suspected of the egg theft).

## Session 32 — The First Egg (2026-07-19)

Full recap: [[The First Egg - Session 32 - 2026-07-19]] (in `Recaps/`).

In brief (continuous with the Session 31 cliffhanger, same in-fiction day): the opened [[Chained Stone Coffin]] holds only **liquefied remains** — the revenant's spirit has fled the host and can **re-form in another corpse in roughly half a day**. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] samples the goo ([[Vial of Decomposed Black Goo]]); [[Haceran Leadbreaker]] (Session 31's elderly cleric, now named) can offer little until the creature is caught again; Banishment must wait until the next day. The party splits: [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], [[Rhaenyra]], and [[Bell]] collect the long-pending **[[Mine Pass|mine writs]]** at [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]'s manor and are shown the **collapsed tunnel** — Baldrin himself now suspects **foul play** (maintenance-only shaft; survivors' stories don't match) and promises **lists of the dead and surviving miners**. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Mike]], and [[Thorn]] get themselves **deputized** by the [[Captain Grazug|guard captain]] (who will warn the gravekeepers, and refers them to **[[Havid]]** on how [[Victor Stonehammer]] died). Seren's hour-long **egg-tracking sweep** (with Mike carrying her city-wide) clears the [[Greybraids]]' holdings and finds **one egg in a [[Lakeside Junk Pile (Stonewater)|lakeside junk pile]]** — recovered from a pristine hidden box, now in Mike's backpack, a [[Dwarven Child (Stonewater Lakeside)|dwarven child]] watching — and **at least two eggs at an abandoned [[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside mansion]]**, not yet approached.

## Session 33 — Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces (2026-08-02)

Full recap: [[Dragons, Deceit, and Dark Forces - Session 33 - 2026-08-02]] (in `Recaps/`). Speaker-labeled transcript kept offline.

In brief (same in-fiction day, late afternoon into evening): [[Rhaenyra]]'s **crab familiar** scouts beyond the mine collapse — a **pre-existing passage**, left and right branches, the link severed twenty minutes in — while the mine guide's map confirms the shaft was supposed to **end there**. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Thorn]] hit the **[[Greybraid Manor (Stonewater)|Greybraid Manor]] gate** (writ accepted; message routed via [[Captain Grazug]]; the patriarch named: **[[Kassadren]]**). The party reunites over the recovered **[[Dragon Eggs|egg]]** — warm, stone-like — then moves on the **[[Cliffside Mansion (Stonewater)|cliffside manor]]** ([[Thorn]] and [[Rhaenyra]] staying to guard the egg), armed with a [[Gustav]]-forged **[[Broader Warrant]]**. Inside: the **[[Acolytes of Oghma]]** — a dozen robed "healers," mostly dwarves, granted the house by **Kassadren himself** — an **[[Aberration (Cliffside Mansion)|aberration]]** below, and a flat denial of any eggs. The escort turns ambush; the manor erupts. [[Mike]] kills [[Vil]]; [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] kills two cultists; the [[Cult Elder (Cliffside Mansion)|Elder]] flees Witch-Bolt-maimed; the cult **kills its own** rather than allow surrender; [[Seren Moss|Seren]] finds a **concealed cellar door** onto natural caves and chases the aberration down with Eldrin behind her. Then a **[[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|bald mage]]**'s wand bolts drop Lyrion; his death save fails on **lingering [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|plant-fight]] exhaustion**; a cultist's sword follows. **[[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] dies.** Cliffhanger: next session opens on a **ten-round Revivify countdown**.

## Session 34 — Clash at the Cliffside Manor (2026-08-16)

Full recap: [[Clash at the Cliffside Manor - Session 34 - 2026-08-16]] (in `Recaps/`). Speaker-labeled transcript kept offline.

In brief (resuming the Session 33 cliffhanger mid-battle, same in-fiction evening): the party relays [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s body down the manor — [[Bell]] cleaves his killer in two and clears the path, [[Mike]] carries him, [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] **Misty Steps with the corpse** — and **[[Seren Moss|Seren]]'s Revivify beats the ten-round clock**, consuming the [[Powerful Diamond|five-carat diamond]]. Lyrion glimpses an afterlife (yellow hills, [[Endra Thalor|his mother]] in the distance) and turns back: *"I need a vacation."* [[Rhaenyra]] (released from egg-guard by [[Thorn]], who hides the egg under an illusion) arrives, wild-shapes into a **saber-toothed tiger**, and kills the last cultist — before **[[Zehal]] seizes Bell** mid-battle (*"Hunt! Kill! Murderer!"* — first-ever use of her bestial claws) and turns her briefly on the tiger. The [[Bald Mage (Cliffside Mansion)|bald mage]] fights the whole party — 52 damage in a turn into Eldrin, **Cone of Cold freezing Mike unconscious**, Counterspelling Lyrion's Fireball — and **escapes or hides**: invisible, upstairs, doors closing, a balcony door ajar, no landing marks. Lyrion's second Fireball **sets the manor alight**; the party douses it. The search finds the [[Acolytes of Oghma|cult]]'s **[[Map of the Mines (Cliffside Mansion)|mines map]]** (crude mines-to-manor path — they dug the tunnel), the **[[Cult Journal (Cliffside Mansion)|cult journal]]** (mortality support group → *"you are only truly dead when you are forgotten"* remembrance-cult, sent from the north to recover something in [[Stonewater]]), **~5,000–6,000 gp** (take/leave undecided), two **locked magical egg-sized boxes** (Mike carries), an unopened **[[Arcane Scroll Case (Cliffside Mansion)|arcane scroll case]]** — and, in the cave below, the **singed crates of the remaining [[Dragon Eggs|dragon eggs]], empty**. The eggs are gone down the tunnel; the party's next step is the **city authorities**.

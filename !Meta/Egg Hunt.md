# Egg Hunt

**Arc location:** [[Stonewater]] (and the [[Ironheart Mines]]); the [[Iron Peaks]] region.
**Status:** Active.
**Central mystery:** Recover the **stolen dragon eggs** of [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]] before further settlements are destroyed. Investigate disappearances and murders linked to the [[Ironheart Mines]].
**Arc span:** Begins when the party **leaves the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]]** (~3/1/26) and heads south toward [[Stonewater]]; runs through current events in Stonewater. The opening **travel leg** — the [[Iron Peaks]] foothills, the [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|anti-magic fairy grove]], and the road-meeting with [[Basil]]'s caravan (logged 3/15/26–5/3/26 in [[Bluefield Burning]] for continuity) — precedes the party's arrival in Stonewater at **Session 29**, the first session recorded in this vault. See [[Campaign Arcs]] for all arc boundaries.

## Session 29 — The Search for Dragon Eggs (2026-05-10)

> First **recorded** session of the [[Egg Hunt]] arc — the party arrives in [[Stonewater]]. (The arc's earlier travel leg from the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]] is summarized in [[Bluefield Burning]].) **Campaign Session 29** overall — the party migrated note-taking platforms at this point, so this is the first session captured in this Obsidian vault. Earlier sessions are summarized in [[Fish People]] and [[Bluefield Burning]].

Full recap: [[The Search for Dragon Eggs - Session 29 - 2026-05-10]] (in `Recaps/`).

In brief: The party arrives in [[Stonewater]] and gains audience with [[Baldrin Stoneforge]], foreman of the [[Ironheart Mines]] and business associate of [[Victor Barun]]. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] reports the destruction of [[Bluefield]] by the **multi-headed red dragon** [[The God Of The Iron Peaks]] and the [[Captured Half-Dragon]]'s testimony, requesting aid locating the stolen eggs. The party also presses Baldrin on the recent mine disappearances and murders — manner of death, suspect's vanished corpse, the feather-emblem insignia [[Rhaenyra]] spotted on travelers, and a suspicious caravan lost in the city. [[Thorn]] shapeshifts to demonstrate the cart-driver suspect's appearance. The group agrees to reconvene the following day.

[[Mike]] and [[Lyrion Thalor]] arrive at the tavern [[Good For What Ails Ya]] ahead of the party. Mike collapses upstairs; Lyrion drinks sparkler-cocktails, laments a lost "wizard battle" against an anti-magic zone + magical instability, and gets sick. [[Thorn]] (still disguised as Lyrion) tends to him in the alley with Mage Hand. Group orders dinner, secures rooms, plans to resume in the morning.

## Session 30 — Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas (2026-05-17)

Full recap: [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-17]] (in `Recaps/`).

In brief: morning at [[Good For What Ails Ya]]. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] secretly healed [[Mike]] overnight. Over breakfast, [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Mike]] recount the [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|fairy-grove encounter]] in full — sheep transformation, carnivorous-plant lure, narrow escape. Lyrion recovers a **[[Carnivorous Plant Bark Sample|bark sample]]** from Mike's belt; Mike rebrands himself "Mike the Sorcerer" (still mechanically a Monk). [[Seren Moss|Seren]] sketches the **feather-and-dagger insignia** for the barkeep **[[Nastrin]]**, who identifies it as belonging to the **[[Greybraids]]**, a dwarf clan in [[Stonewater]]. [[Thorn]] connects the mine murders to **cave-in survivors** who likely saw something they should not have. Party plans to split: pair to approach the Greybraids subtly, rest to investigate the mines after picking up a [[Mine Pass|mine pass]] from [[Baldrin Stoneforge|Baldrin]]. [[Seren Moss|Seren]] requests a disguise from [[Thorn]]; [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] step into the alley and have a long emotional reconciliation about their childhood and their father. As they're leaving, a frightened scream draws them to a **[[Dwarven Woman (Stonewater Pickaxe Body)|dwarven woman]] crouched over a pickaxe-killed body**.

# Campaign Arcs

Arc boundaries (per the DM). **Session counts and some early dates are estimates** — the early campaign was poorly recorded (no reliable notes or recordings at first), so a number of session numbers and dates are assumptions. See [[!Crypt Keepers]].

1. **[[Prologue]]** — a single session with most of the players, **before the campaign officially began**.
2. **[[Fish People]]** — **start of the campaign through the party leaving [[Fort Elm]]** (includes the post-aboleth Fort Elm downtime, up to the sail north).
3. **[[Bluefield Burning]]** — **after leaving [[Fort Elm]] through leaving the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]]** ([[Roseview]], the hydra, the burning of [[Bluefield]], the refugee camp).
4. **[[Egg Hunt]]** — **after leaving the firbolg [[Refugee Camp]] through current events at [[Stonewater]]** (the current arc). Its opening travel leg precedes the party's arrival in Stonewater at Session 29, the first session recorded in this vault.

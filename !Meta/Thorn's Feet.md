# Thorn's Feet

> **Subject of record.** A recurring item in the campaign log. This page consolidates the observed pattern around [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]]'s sustained scholarly engagement with the **shapeshifted feet of [[Thorn]]**.

## Documented incidents

### 9/6/24 — Bell's camp (Fish People, Session 8)

After tracking webbed prints around [[Bell]]'s ransacked camp, [[Thorn]] (Changeling) **shapeshifted her feet** to test the morphology against the Kuo-Toa-print hypothesis. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] then negotiated, on the spot, to **draw the shapeshifted foot in his sketchbook** in exchange for **one free performance** owed to Thorn at a later date.

The justification offered, on the record, was: *"for science."*

This is the foundational event of the dossier.

## Procedural observations

The following operational details are noted for completeness; the reader is encouraged to draw no specific conclusions.

- The drawing was **freely volunteered** by Lyrion, not requested by the party.
- The drawing was specifically of **Thorn's foot**, not of the Kuo-Toa prints already visible in the wet earth — which could have been sketched more easily and at lower cost (no performance owed).
- The negotiated rate (**one free performance** in exchange for sketching access) is non-trivial. The standard street rate of a [[Twilight Masqueraders]] performance has not been logged but is presumed to exceed the cost of a graphite rubbing of an unmoving footprint.
- Lyrion is a **Wizard** of the Cloistered Scholar background; possession of a sketchbook and inclination to draw observed phenomena are consistent with his profession. The choice of subject is not.

## Contextual pattern

Lyrion's wider engagement with Thorn forms a relevant backdrop. See [[Thorn]] for the broader relationship; the salient items here are:

- The disguised-as-Lyrion incident at [[Good For What Ails Ya]] (Session 29) in which Thorn (in Lyrion's shape) attended to Lyrion's hair while Lyrion was indisposed in an alley. Lyrion subsequently embraced Thorn-in-his-shape and forgave "himself" for not being perfect.
- Lyrion's general comfort with Thorn's shapeshifting — markedly higher than the party median.

Neither item is itself diagnostic. The pattern is what is logged.

## Party-table assessment

It is the prevailing assessment of the [[!Crypt Keepers|Crypt Keepers]] party that **Lyrion is a foot guy**.

The assessment is not formally on the in-fiction record. It is durable, however, in out-of-character commentary.

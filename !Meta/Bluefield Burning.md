# Bluefield Burning

**Arc location:** [[The Wilds]] — north of [[Fort Elm]], traveling via the [[Sail la Vie]]; [[Roseview]] and then [[Bluefield]].
**Status:** Concluded — bridges [[Fish People]] → [[Egg Hunt]].
**Dated session log:** [[Bluefield Burning - Sessions 1-5 (Arc Two)]] (the early entries) and [[Transcripts/Transcript Index]] (later sessions).

## Setup

After the [[Fish People|Fish People arc]] closed, [[Victor Barun|Victor]] tasked the party with one more detour before Stonewater: help **[[Rhaenyra]]**, whose fishing village [[Roseview]] had been plagued by a multi-headed beast. The party met Rhaenyra at the diner [[The Apple Thicket]] in [[Fort Elm]], received parting gifts, and set sail on the **[[Sail la Vie]]**.

The party at this point: [[Lyrion Thalor]], [[Eldrin Thalor]], [[Seren Moss]], [[Bell]], [[Thorn]], plus the newly joined [[Rhaenyra]] and [[Mike]] (both introduced during the 9/17/25 downtime). **[[Teron]] had already departed** before the arc began.

## Journey north — the hydra at Roseview

En route to [[Roseview]], the party encountered evidence of a **five-headed hydra** stalking the riverbank: uprooted 6 ft trees, snake-trail tracks, asymmetric reptilian claw prints, a bobcat carcass with a chunk bitten out. The hydra bore a **scarred decapitated head stump** and **singed, damaged scales** — implying prior combat. The party baited it ashore with bear traps and killed it (sessions 10/1–10/8). Loot: **eyes, teeth, blood, hide** ([[The Hydra]]). [[Mike]] ate some of the meat.

## Roseview — arrival and festival

Party arrived at [[Roseview]] on 10/15/25. On 11/5/25, **[[Thalrin]] hosted a festival** celebrating the hydra slaying — feasting, town exploration, and a **wizard duel** ([[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] participated, ended scorched, healed by [[Seren Moss|Seren]]). Roseview has no tavern; party slept on the boat. On 11/19/25 they gave heartfelt goodbyes and set sail north toward [[Stonewater]].

## Bluefield — encounter and destruction

About halfway to [[Stonewater]], the party stopped to give a **young pregnant couple with their animal** passage to their destination — **[[Bluefield]]**, a separate town further upriver. Approaching at twilight, the party saw a red-orange light pollution over the trees and the smell of burning wood. **Bluefield was already on fire.**

### The fight

The party fought **kobolds and a half-dragon riding a [[Spark Beasts|Spark Beast]] — a flaming bear**. They **captured the half-dragon** ([[Captured Half-Dragon]]) and **escaped the attention of [[The God Of The Iron Peaks|the multi-headed red dragon]]** circling overhead.

Per Seren's POV-recollection in the long-form short story, the attack scene included a **fiery bear** (the [[Spark Beasts|Spark Beast]] mount that the half-dragon was riding), a **roaring half-dragon** (member of the warband; see [[Captured Half-Dragon]]), and the **enormous multi-headed dragon swooping down** — all three confirmed canonical by the 2/15/26 recap.

### After the battle

The party escaped with survivors to the [[Firbolg Camp|Refugee Camp]] led there by **[[Kira]]**, a firbolg known to [[Bell]] from her past. They made camp on the deck of the [[Sail la Vie]]. Among the rescued was a family with a newborn baby, observed by Lyrion below deck. Seren spent the night fearing that **"the dragons" would not be assuaged** and that **Fort Elm would be next to feel their fury**.

That same night, [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] slipped a **love letter** into Seren's bedroll. (See [[Seren Moss#Current relationship — Lyrion|Seren's page]] for the verbatim text and her response.)

## Refugee camp interlude

At the [[Refugee Camp]] the firbolg clan elders shared information about [[Bell]]'s backstory and gave the party the [[Tome of Firbolg Spirit Communion]]. The party performed the ritual in the woods that night — [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], [[Bell]], [[Kira]], and [[Mike]] participating. The spirit **spoke Lyrion's name**; Bell became attached to her [[Loa]] **[[Zehal]]**. The next morning the party was awoken by **flapping overhead** — the dragon patrolling.

## The route to Stonewater

The party traveled south to the river, took the [[Sail la Vie]] down past a leftover kobold from a raiding party, stowed the boat back into its box form, and walked into the [[Iron Peaks]] foothills. **East of [[Stonewater]] they found the [[Fairy Grove (Anti-Magic Pocket)|anti-magic pocket]]** — they passed through and camped outside Stonewater.

That night, [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Mike]] split off to investigate the grove. They were **lured to its center by a fairy-illusion** — a trap from a **carnivorous plant** disguised as a tree on an island in a pond, mimicking an angler-fish lure. The plant tried to grab Mike; Lyrion **Polymorphed himself into a sheep** to escape; Mike fled the fight and carried sheep-Lyrion out; both barely survived. This is the literal "wizard battle in the anti-magic zone followed by magical instability" Lyrion later bemoans at [[Good For What Ails Ya]] in [[Egg Hunt]] Session 29.

The main group continued to [[Stonewater]] and met **[[Basil]]'s caravan from [[Autumnbreak]]** on the road, traveling loosely alongside a **separate second cart** whose occupants wore a **feather-and-dagger emblem** — per [[Dragons, Disguises, and Dilemmas - Session 30 - 2026-05-18|Session 30]], that emblem belongs to the **[[Greybraids]]** dwarf clan of [[Stonewater]], and the cart (not Basil's caravan) is the chief suspect for the egg theft.

# Fish People

**Arc location:** [[Fort Elm]], the [[Rollingbrook River]], and the cave system / [[The Ancient Ruined City|ancient ruined city]] beneath the city.
**Sessions:** First arc of the campaign (~June 2024 onward — earliest sessions in this vault; full per-session breakdowns from the OneNote raw notes are preserved below).
**Central antagonist:** an **[[The Undead Aboleth|undead aboleth]]** entombed in the [[Cathedral of the Aboleth|cathedral]] at the heart of the [[The Ancient Ruined City|ancient ruined city]].
**Outcome:** Aboleth slain. Captives rescued. Drow refugees granted temporary status in [[Fort Elm]]. 3,000 gp council bounty + 5,400 gp altar treasure (+ [[Sail la Vie]] + spell scrolls + miscellany).

## DM canonical narrative

> Verbatim from the DM-provided arc summary.

Hundreds of years after the devastating war known as the [[Glyph Scar]], the world of [[Azadin]] is still coping with the fallout. The scarred land and pulsating skies were mere portents of a deeper sickness, one that is only just beginning to make itself known to the races that occupy this post-apocalyptic realm. Our story begins in [[Fort Elm]], a bulwark of civilization against the encroaching threat of [[The Wilds]].

**The cast.** [[Seren Moss|Seren]], a knightly half elf adopted by an aging human family and sworn into the service of the god of death, [[Kelemvor]]. [[Bell]], a solitary firbolg dancing on the line between the safety of [[Fort Elm]] and the dangerous bounty of [[The Wilds]]. [[Teron]], a friendly dragonborn paladin searching for a forgotten past. [[Thorn]], a vivacious one-woman circus with a thousand faces. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], tight-knit **high elf brothers** with a painful history seeking a new start *(the DM summary's "forest elf" framing is superseded by the canonical race; see [[Lyrion Thalor]] and [[Eldrin Thalor]])*.

### The retirement party

Seren, Bell, Teron, and Thorn attended [[Victor Barun]]'s boisterous retirement party. The host went missing and there were signs of foul play — even what seemed to be his body — though hints suggested things were not as they first appeared. A descent into the **cave system beneath Victor's lodge** (via the tunnel beneath the wine cellar) revealed a nest of fish-like monsters and **a doppelganger** (Victor's impostor), as well as the kidnapped gentleman himself. After a harrowing battle, Victor was returned safely home. Impressed, he invited them to join him the following morning for breakfast and a business proposal.

### Sparring and investigation

Thorn invited her newest circus recruits and friends, [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]], to assist in a brief investigation of the befouled river — which revealed only that the **source of the pollution was nonmagical**.

Teron and Bell sparred that same evening, during which Teron revealed he could not remember much of his past and that moments he did remember **often slipped away as soon as he tried to examine them**. He shared a **map of his hometown** with Bell, but she was unable to help him recognize the area.

### The proposal and the collapse

The following morning, Victor laid out his proposal to the group of six would-be adventurers: in [[Stonewater]], a settlement to the north, there had been **eight disappearances in a single section of the [[Ironheart Mines|mine]]**. Supernatural events had been reported. Victor felt this group was best for the job.

Before they could accept or decline, **the house collapsed** into the cave system beneath, knocking all present unconscious. Their final memory before all went black was a snarling, unnatural voice in their minds:

> *"Gather the living, bring me more flesh!"*

### The river kidnappings

The group awoke one by one in the flooded ruins of Victor's lodge and evacuated to firmer ground — only to find all of [[Fort Elm]] thrown into chaos. Dozens of fish-like monsters of the same kind they had fought the night before were **kidnapping townspeople at random** and fleeing into the depths of the tainted river. Among the captives: **[[Gwyn Moss|Seren's mother Gwyn]], Shrinekeeper of [[Kelemvor]]**.

After ensuring Victor's safety, the group did battle with the monsters and rescued as many as they could. Captain **[[Lilith]]** of the town guard revealed that the fish monsters fitted captives with **enchanted air masks** ([[Facemask Device]]) to keep them alive — meaning the kidnapped townsfolk might still be rescued.

### First failed rescue

Lyrion, Teron, Thorn, and Seren attempted a direct rescue using the available masks, venturing into the now-flooded cave system. The effort earned them a vicious fight and a close brush with death — nearly losing Teron to the fish monsters — and all returned alive but failed. They spent the day seeking other entrances to the caves and asking the authorities for help.

### Tension and preparation

Efforts to persuade the **Council of [[Fort Elm]]** to send an army were unsuccessful, **producing brief tension between Eldrin and Seren**. The party retreated to the [[Shrine of Kelemvor|Temple of Kelemvor]] for the night. That evening the river took on an **unnatural glow**; the next day a foul **scent of sulfur and rot** hung over Fort Elm.

The next morning, after restocking and checking in with Lilith, the party headed into the forest. **[[Bell]]'s camp had been raided — all her weapons and hunting traps were stolen.** Through a **Sending** spell, Seren contacted Gwyn and confirmed the prisoners were still alive. Thorn shapeshifted [[Thorn's Feet|her feet]] to examine the strange tracks; Lyrion took notes and drawings for scientific study (see [[Thorn's Feet]]). They made for a gulch Bell had found, each armed with a water-breathing mask just in case.

### Descent into the deeper caves

Using an anchored rope, the party descended into the evil-smelling gulch — slime and foam coating walls and floors in extreme amounts. The gulch opened to an as-yet unseen section of the cave system: flowing water, **bioluminescent algae**, and more fish monsters.

### The Drow prisoner incident

Battle was joined. Moving deeper, the group spotted a small group of bound, unconscious prisoners. When he saw a **Drow** among them, [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] reacted without thinking and **killed the Drow with a spell**. **[[Teron]], enraged, smashed his shield into Lyrion's head, knocking him off his feet. [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] retaliated by shooting Teron with an arrow.**

Monsters still loomed, so the party redirected their aggression. Lyrion and Eldrin maintained they had done nothing wrong; Teron declared his intent to **report the murder** when they returned to Fort Elm. Eldrin issued a thinly veiled threat. The group moved on uneasily, revived the two surviving prisoners and sent them back through the cave to town.

In an adjoining chamber, Bell and Lyrion found a stone idol — **[[Stone Idol of Blibdoolpoolp|Blibdoolpoolp, The Drowning Goddess]]**, patron of the Kuo-Toa. They could discern nothing else about it.

### Underground discoveries

The party found a **[[Dagger of Strange Iconography|dagger]]** and **[[Coin of Strange Iconography|coin]]** with unfamiliar iconography, both faintly magical.

Further on, a **dome-shaped chamber full of skeletal remains** — what appeared to be the remains of a battlefield. All wore the same crest as the coin and dagger; varied races (humans, elves, dwarves, halflings). The group judged the remains to be from **just after the end of the [[Glyph Scar|Glyph War]]**.

### Meeting Riknozz

Continuing downward, Lyrion and Eldrin both felt they had entered the **inner lair of whatever evil creature was emitting the slime**. They came upon a group of fish monsters and Drow doing battle — the party joined on the Drow's side, and an uneasy peace was struck since the Drow claimed to have rescued humans. The Drow led them to their **camp in a [[The Temple of Tyr (Ruined)|ruined temple of Tyr]]**.

On the way, Teron recognized **the place he had been taken** during the first cave excursion and **dredged the human and Drow bodies from one of the pools of slime-water** — none were Gwyn. During this Lyrion had a terse exchange with the Drow leader, **[[Riknozz]]**, and discovered that **these Drow do not worship the Spider Queen**.

At the Drow camp, none of the rescued humans were Gwyn either. Seren steadied herself and addressed the captives. **[[Riknozz]] drew the party to meet the [[Drow Elder]]**, who revealed her belief that the monster causing the madness was an **aboleth** that had taken up residence in the ruined city below. Lyrion suggested a short rest. Riknozz volunteered to join the rescue — more Drow had been taken.

### The Lyrion–Teron reconciliation seed

While Lyrion and Teron forged ahead to set up ropes and pitons down a treacherous vertical drop, Lyrion offered to **turn himself over to the Drow in a trial by combat** to atone for the prisoner's death. Teron agreed to think on it.

### The riddle door

Beyond the drop, the party entered a long manmade tunnel into the ancient ruined city. The tunnel ended in a circular chamber with a fountain holding a **statue gripping a white gem**. Lyrion magically walked the ceiling to examine a large door on the far side. Seren sensed undead among the skeletons on the ground.

Trying to disarm a skeleton, Bell **triggered the room's defense** — all the undead rose. Seren noticed the statue's eyes flashed each time an undead fell, raising another. Lyrion spotted the door's gem-arch — **six gems in all**: five set in the arch with one slot empty, the sixth (the statue's white Opal) waiting to be placed.

Working together — Lyrion, Thorn, and Teron pried the gem from the statue while Seren held the line with **Spirit Guardians**, with Riknozz and Bell pushing skeletons into her spell range. Lyrion reunited the gem with its socket; all six gems flashed.

Eldrin found a plaque on an alcove statue reading:

> *"Our home is sealed in riddle.*
> *Prepare to speak an answer.*
> *Employ the word of legend.*
> *Now you may enter."*

Solution: **OPEN** — touch the gems in the order whose first letter matches each riddle line. **Opal, Peridot, Emerald, Nuummite**. The door unlocked. The undead's animating magic withered away. (See [[Riddle Door]].)

### The Ancient Ruined City

Behind the door: an enormous staircase curving downward to an ancient city with a near-pristine **[[Cathedral of the Aboleth|cathedral]]** at its center. Riknozz revealed his people's stories: an ancient city of safety and harmony, until its society **collapsed under the influence of a powerful evil**.

As they traveled into the city, Eldrin became aware of many undead nearby and a strong **aberration** presence. Lyrion warned spellcasters that any magic might draw its attention. The party found the building where the fish monsters were keeping their prisoners, approaching from the opposite end of the street while the monsters were absent. **Seren awoke and healed [[Gwyn Moss|her mother]]**, who in turn helped heal the other survivors.

### Lyrion's solo trip into the cathedral

Lyrion stayed behind to investigate the cathedral, promising Eldrin to follow soon. Magically invisible, he followed lines of fish monsters into the cathedral. Within a circle of ancient statues lay a deep pit of putrid water. **He witnessed the beasts transforming from kuo-toa back into the same slime that coated the caverns and flooded the river.** He had found the source of Fort Elm's corruption.

Knowing his time was short, he began to creep back out. A voice hissed in his mind:

> *"I KNOW YOU'RE THERE. COME CLOSER."*

Enormous rotting tentacles lashed out from the pit as something anchored to the statues, pulling itself upward. Lyrion bolted. At the edge of the inner ring he met Eldrin — furious — who said what had felt like minutes had been **over an hour**. Lyrion concluded the monster was an **undead beholder** *(later corrected to aboleth)*.

As Eldrin took his hand to lead him out, **Lyrion realized he was still inside the cathedral; the "Eldrin" was the creature's illusion**. The aboleth was in his mind.

Using **[[Gustav]]** to determine the true exit, Lyrion talked the creature into conjuring an image of his **dead mother** as a deception. Enraged, he threw a **fireball** at the well and fled along Gustav's indicated path. The real Eldrin, sprinting back for him, heard the explosion and snapped Lyrion out of an illusion that nearly had him running into the tentacles. They fled together as the voice cried:

> *"NO! FREE ME! FREE ME!"*

Lyrion threw a second fireball that struck true. A psionic scream of rage chased them out.

### Thorn's heritage

Back at the Drow camp, **Thorn revealed her true form** to Lyrion and Eldrin — somewhat Drow-like. She had been a one-woman circus partly because her appearance kept her on the outside of every community.

Thorn, Lyrion, and Eldrin secured audience with the [[Drow Elder]], trading information about the aboleth for answers. The elder explained: Thorn likely came from a group called **Shapeshifters**, banished from both Drow and elf societies, considered **cursed by the gods**. The trait can skip generations; her parents likely abandoned her at birth. The elder had only ever heard stories from her father — she had never met another Shapeshifter.

### Lyrion's reconciliation

Lyrion confessed the Drow prisoner's death to the elder, offering trial by combat. The elder asked the brothers if they had lost someone to the Drow. **Eldrin told her of the torture and murder of their mother at the hands of Drow.**

The elder explained her people had **broken away from more violent sects** and chosen a peaceful path. She offered Lyrion's blood debt paid in exchange for his help killing the aboleth. He agreed.

### Returning to Fort Elm

Seren, Bell, and Teron escorted the townsfolk back to Fort Elm and reported to Lilith. Teron helped **train new recruits** for the coming battle; Seren and **Gwyn** assisted the wounded.

Lyrion, Eldrin, and Thorn rejoined at the barracks. Seren invited everyone to stay at the [[Shrine of Kelemvor|Temple of Kelemvor]] again. **Thorn summoned a magical tent** in the guest room to shield their planning. Teron cooked; Seren raided the temple cellars. The group celebrated, played, and had a heartfelt conversation about the future.

### Final battle

The next morning the party headed to the barracks. **Lilith granted the Drow temporary refugee status.** Plan: the party of six fights the aberration directly; the Drow withdraw into town; the guards and Lilith's summoned mercenaries defend the riverbanks.

In the cathedral, Teron approached the pit and opened his mind to taunt the creature. **It answered back inside his mind**; Bell noticed his eyes were filmed with slime. He turned and **stabbed Seren with a spear**.

The aboleth rose fully — rotting tentacles, slime-summoned fish minions. Lyrion fireballed; Eldrin loosed arrows; **Seren shouted Teron back to himself with a spell** that broke the charm.

Battle joined in earnest against the **undead aboleth**. **Bell spent much of the fight charmed by the enemy.** Near the end, the monster nearly slew Teron and Seren. **A decisive fireball from Lyrion struck the death blow.** Eldrin revived the fallen — Thorn nearly drowned Teron in tears; Seren unceremoniously threw up on Lyrion when he tried to embrace her.

### Aftermath in the cathedral

**Bell dove into the pit** and retrieved a **valuable, powerful spell scroll case**. Lyrion judged from the **runes on spikes impaled in the creature for untold ages** — and from symbols inside the well — that **a very powerful spell had once been cast to keep the thing entombed**.

**Teron explored the altar** at the back of the hall; guided by **[[Mystra]]**, he took the ancient offerings: **spell scrolls**, an ornately decorated **[[Sail la Vie|mahogany box]]**, and a sack of coins totaling **5,400 gp**.

### Returning home

Lyrion magically cleaned the party. They shared news of their success at the gates; the guards saluted. Speaking quietly to Lyrion, **Seren took his hand shyly and suggested they give the hug a second try later**.

The party met Lilith and Victor at the river's edge. Lilith — astonished — thanked them. Victor expressed his relief. The Council had put a **3,000 gp bounty** on the monster in their absence; the party would collectively receive it.

When they were alone again, Teron revealed the altar treasure. **Thorn realized the mahogany box was actually a transforming boat** ([[Sail la Vie]]) and took everyone to the docks to see their new vessel.

The party agreed to continue traveling together — both as a traveling circus and to fulfill the business proposition Victor had spoken of days before. **The Stonewater mine job** was the seam that led into [[Bluefield Burning]] and ultimately [[Egg Hunt]].

## Session-by-session (per Lyrion's OneNote raw notes)

> Player-perspective per-session bullets from the OneNote raw notes, preserved for granularity. Some details overlap or differ slightly from the DM narrative above; both records are retained.

### Session 1 — Retirement & Doppelganger

See [[Prologue]] for setup.

**Two distinct underground events** happen in this arc — separated below to prevent confusion.

#### Underground event 1 — Wine-cellar tunnel rescue

- The party investigates [[Victor Barun]]'s "death" at the mansion. The body is revealed to be **a doppelganger** (singular).
- Investigation traces the scheme to a man named **[[Gorge]]**, an opportunistic embezzler who used the doppelganger to siphon Victor's funds. When the gig was up, Gorge killed the doppelganger. The dead doppelganger had **death-white skin**.
- Victor himself is recovered through a **tight tunnel beneath his wine cellar** — wet earth and gray darkness. The passage contained **strange algae** and a **gel-like encasement around a screaming skeleton**. **Descent team: [[Seren Moss|Seren]], [[Bell]], [[Teron|Teran]].**
- The party joins [[Thorn]]'s troupe (the Twilight Masqueraders).
- Recovered Victor lends [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] *[[A Brief Delve into the History of Plainmore Woods]]*.

#### Day-2 interlude

- [[Eldrin Thalor|Eldrin]] browses the city, tries to steal books/magical items for [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]], brings him a [[River Water Vial|vial of river water]] from the [[Rollingbrook River]]. Not magical; ecosystem change.
- [[Seren Moss|Seren]] does charity work at the shrines of [[Kelemvor]].
- [[Bell]] camps south of the city; [[Teron]] trains on the east side.
- **Victor's specific commission to Seren**: ongoing employment to investigate the **missing farmers** of [[Fort Elm]] — the offer that draws her onto the road (see [[Seren Moss#Backstory|Seren's leaving-home moment]]). *Resolved later in arc — the missing farmers were kidnapped by the fish people.*

#### Underground event 2 — The collapse and the slime-reservoir

- Party is summoned back to Victor's mansion for breakfast to discuss the [[Stonewater]] mine job. **The house collapses** into the cave system beneath. Voice in their heads: *"Gather the living, bring me more flesh!"* Party knocked unconscious.
- The party awakens in a **sinkhole / flooded lodge ruins**. Investigating beneath, a **secret tunnel** leads to an **open reservoir** containing a **slime creature** in a large pool. Creature dispatched.
- Lyrion observes: the foam matches the riverbank foam.

### Session 2 — Whisper in the Collapsed House

- The party begins in the collapsed house. A whisper enters their heads: *"Gather the living, bring me more flesh."*
- One of the hostages they saved has a **[[Facemask Device|device on their head]]** — confirmed to be an enchanted breathing/water-breathing device.

### Session 3 — Riverbank Combat & The Cave

- The party awakens on the banks of the river under attack from a group of **fish people** carrying humans on their shoulders.
- They had **milky white eyes** and appeared under the effects of something — possibly the same command heard in the party's minds ("bring me flesh").
- Combat initiated with a group of **sahuagin** and **kuotoa**, plus a **particularly large fish monster** that nearly killed [[Thorn]]. A large group of kuotoa **escape carrying hostages into a hole in the river** — including [[Gwyn Moss|Gwyn]] (Seren's mother).
- [[Teron|Teren]] and [[Seren Moss|Seren]] enter the cave and spot 3 enemies. Combat begins. [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and [[Thorn]] hear bells from Toll the Dead and turn back to the cave to assist.
- [[Teron]] is taken by a **large squid-like creature** and dragged down unconscious.

### Session 4 — Victor's Charge

- The party goes to Victor's house. He wants them to **deliver fish to [[Stonewater]] and meet someone there** about the **eight disappearances** in a single section of the [[Ironheart Mines]]. This is the seam that bridges Fish People → [[Bluefield Burning]] → [[Egg Hunt]].

### Session 5 — Surfacing & Visions

- On the surface, [[Seren Moss|Seren]] is dragged up by [[Thorn]] and [[Lyrion Thalor|Lyrion]] and tended by a medic. While unconscious she had a vision: creatures taking people down to an **underground temple**, being called for **flesh sacrifices**.
- [[Teron]] awoke and fought off creatures in a cavern (which leads somewhere deeper underground), winning his fight "rambo style" and returning to the surface.
- [[Lilith]], **Captain of the Guard**, requests the party accompany her to the local garrison to regroup. The party levels up.

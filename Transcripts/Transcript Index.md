# Transcript Index — WhisperX session transcripts

> **Source**: WhisperX auto-transcripts of campaign sessions across the Fish People, Bluefield Burning, and pre-Egg Hunt arcs. **Treat as low-fidelity** — speakers are not labeled, transcription is often inaccurate, names are routinely mangled. When transcripts contradict the DM-canonical summaries or the dated session-note PDFs, the canon wins.
>
> **Useful for**: confirming session dates, capturing in-the-room banter and quoted lines, providing anchor points for later recall, and — most importantly — the **DM recap statements at the start of each session**, which are higher-fidelity than the conversational middle.
